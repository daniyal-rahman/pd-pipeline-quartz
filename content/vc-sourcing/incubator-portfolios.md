## ARCH Venture Partners

### Aspen Neuroscience
- **Focus**: iPSC-derived autologous neuron replacement therapy for Parkinson's disease
- **Lead Program**: ANPD001 - personalized dopaminergic neuronal precursor cell (DANPC) therapy
- **Status**: Phase 1/2a ASPIRO trial, FDA Fast Track designation
- **Funding**: $115M Series C (November 2025), total raised >$340M
- **Co-investors**: OrbiMed, Frazier Life Sciences, Revelation Partners

### SciNeuro Pharmaceuticals
- **Focus**: Neurotherapeutics for neurodegenerative diseases
- **Targets**: Lp-PLA2, beta amyloid, LRRK2
- **PD Relevance**: LRRK2 program directly targets Parkinson's
- **Funding**: $53M (December 2025), led by LAV and ARCH
- **Additional**: $5M grant from Michael J. Fox Foundation

### Denali Therapeutics (NASDAQ: DNLI)
- **Focus**: Neurodegenerative disease therapeutics
- **Lead PD Program**: BIIB122/DNL151 - LRRK2 inhibitor (partnered with Biogen)
- **Status**: Phase 2b LUMA study enrollment complete, readout expected 2026
- **Additional**: Phase 2a BEACON study ongoing in LRRK2-PD patients

### Kisbee Therapeutics
- **Focus**: Apolipoprotein-based drugs for neurodegeneration
- **Therapeutic Areas**: Alzheimer's, NPC, TBI, stroke, MS
- **PD Relevance**: Lipid biology approach may extend to PD (not explicitly stated)
- **Status**: Preclinical

### Neumora Therapeutics (NASDAQ: NMRA)
- **Focus**: Precision medicines for brain diseases
- **PD Programs**:
  - NMRA-GCase (preclinical) for Parkinson's disease
  - NMRA-NLRP3 inhibitor (Parkinson's UK funding of $2.1M)
- **Status**: Preclinical PD programs; primary focus on depression/psychiatry

## Third Rock Ventures

### Casma Therapeutics
- **Focus**: Autophagy and lysosomal system therapeutics
- **Lead Program**: CSM-101 - first-in-class TRPML1 agonist
- **Target Indications**: GD-PD (Gaucher's disease with PD), GBA-PD, broader PD
- **Status**: IND filing planned H1 2026
- **Mechanism**: Restores lysosomal function by activating TRPML1
- **Funding**: MJFF grant support

### Trace Neuroscience
- **Focus**: RNA-binding therapy to preserve motor function in ALS
- **Lead Program**: UNC13A restoration therapy
- **Funding**: $101M Series A (November 2024)
- **Co-investors**: Atlas Venture, RA Capital, GV
- **PD Relevance**: Broader neurodegeneration potential beyond ALS

## Additional Companies of Interest

### Gain Therapeutics (Public)
- **Focus**: GCase modulator for Parkinson's disease
- **Lead Program**: GT-02287 - allosteric GCase modulator
- **Status**: Phase 1b extension study; 81% GluSph reduction in CSF
- **Key Data**: First GCase modulator to show CSF substrate reduction in PD
- **Next**: 9-month extension data expected September 2026

### Teitur Trophics
- **Focus**: Disease-modifying cyclic peptides for neurodegeneration
- **Lead Program**: TT-P34 - SorCS2-derived peptide
- **Mechanism**: Reactivates CREB signaling, improves lysosomal/mitochondrial function
- **Status**: Phase 1; Phase 2 planning underway
- **Funding**: EUR28M
- **Investors**: Sound Bioventures

### Prevail Therapeutics (Eli Lilly subsidiary)
- **Focus**: Gene therapy for GBA1-PD
- **Lead Program**: PR001/LY3884961 - AAV9-based gene therapy
- **Status**: Phase 1/2a PROPEL trial; FDA Fast Track designation
- **Mechanism**: Delivers healthy GBA1 gene copy

### Voyager Therapeutics
- **Focus**: AAV gene therapies for neurodegeneration
- **PD Program**: GBA1 gene therapy (with Neurocrine)
- **Status**: IND filing expected 2025; clinical trials targeted 2026
- **Platform**: TRACER AAV capsid discovery

### Stealth BioTherapeutics
- **Focus**: Mitochondria-targeted therapeutics
- **PD Relevance**: Systemic bevemipretide showed neuroprotective effects in PD preclinical studies (MJFF-supported)
- **Status**: Evaluating next steps for PD development
- **Commercial**: FORZINITY approved for Barth syndrome (September 2025)
