## Dementia Discovery Fund / SV Health Investors

### Explicit Thesis
> "DDF invests in a broad spectrum of innovative therapeutics and technologies that have the potential to revolutionize the treatment of neurodegenerative diseases."

### Investment Approach
- **Mandate**: Dual mandate of measurable impact AND financial returns
- **Strategy**: "Full spectrum of company and asset stage opportunities"
- **Geography**: Global (offices in London and Boston)
- **AUM**: >$600M raised for dementia/neurodegeneration strategy
- **Fund II**: DDF-2 closed with $269M (May 2025)

### Scientific Focus Areas
- Neuroinflammation
- Genomic instability
- Synaptic physiology
- Proteostasis

### PD-Specific Investments
The DDF portfolio explicitly targets PD through:
1. **NRG Therapeutics**: Mitochondrial dysfunction (mPTP)
2. **Endlyz Therapeutics**: Lysosomal function (ATP13A2/ATP10B)
3. **Harness Therapeutics**: Undisclosed PD target
4. **Montara Therapeutics**: Brain-only pharmacology for PD

### Notable Quote
> "DDF capitalizes on global investment opportunities to fulfill its dual mandate of delivering measurable impact and generating significant financial returns."

## ARCH Venture Partners

### Investment Pattern
- Most prolific biotech VC (74+ funding rounds since 2022)
- $3B raised in latest fund (2024)
- Heavy concentration in neuroscience/neurodegeneration

### Neuro Thesis (Inferred from Investments)
ARCH demonstrates a "multi-modality, multi-target" approach to neurodegeneration:

1. **Cell Therapy**: Aspen Neuroscience (iPSC-derived neurons for PD)

### PD Target Focus
- LRRK2 (SciNeuro, Denali partnership)
- Dopaminergic neuron replacement (Aspen)
- GBA/lysosomal pathways (multiple)

### Recent Quote (BioPharma Dive, December 2025)

## Emerging Consensus: Key PD Investment Themes

### 1. GBA/Lysosomal Pathway
**VCs Investing**: DDF, OrbiMed, Third Rock, ARCH
**Companies**: Vanqua Bio, Casma, Endlyz, Gain Therapeutics, Prevail
**Rationale**: Most common genetic risk factor; druggable with small molecules

### 2. LRRK2 Inhibition
**VCs Investing**: ARCH
**Companies**: Denali/Biogen, SciNeuro
**Rationale**: Proven genetic link; may benefit non-LRRK2 patients

### 3. Cell Replacement Therapy
**VCs Investing**: ARCH, OrbiMed, Cure Ventures
**Companies**: Aspen Neuroscience, Kenai Therapeutics
**Rationale**: Potentially curative; addresses root cause (dopamine neuron loss)

### 4. Mitochondrial Protection
**VCs Investing**: DDF
**Companies**: NRG Therapeutics, Stealth BioTherapeutics
**Rationale**: Mitochondrial dysfunction is hallmark of PD

### 5. Gene Therapy
**VCs Investing**: Versant, (Eli Lilly internal)
**Companies**: Capsida, Prevail, Voyager
**Rationale**: One-time treatment potential; genetic cause correction

### 6. Alpha-Synuclein Targeting
**VCs**: Less VC-backed activity; more pharma-driven
**Companies**: Roche/Prothena (prasinezumab), AC Immune
**Rationale**: Central disease pathology; challenging but high reward

## Red Flags / Areas VCs Are Avoiding

### Alpha-Synuclein Antibodies (Cautious)
- Multiple Phase 2 failures (SPARK, PASADENA borderline)
- Roche proceeding to Phase 3 despite narrow miss
- VCs less active; pharma-driven

### GDNF/Neurotrophic Factors
- Historical failures
- Limited new VC investment
- Teitur Trophics (trophic peptide) is exception

### Symptomatic Therapies
- Crowded market
- Generic competition
- VCs prefer disease-modification
