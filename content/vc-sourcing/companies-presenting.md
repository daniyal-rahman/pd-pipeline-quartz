# Companies Presenting PD Data at Major Conferences (2024-2025)

#claude


## Overview
Comprehensive list of companies presenting Parkinson's disease pipeline data at MDS, AD/PD, SfN, ISSCR, and ASGCT conferences. Organized by company stage and therapeutic modality.

## Large Pharma / Big Biotech

### AbbVie
- **Programs:** Tavapadon (D1/D5 agonist), VYALEV (subcu levodopa)
- **Conferences:** MDS 2024, MDS 2025
- **Key Data:** Phase 3 TEMPO program completed, NDA filed
- **Acquisition:** Cerevel Therapeutics ($8.7B, Dec 2023)
- **Headquarters:** North Chicago, IL, USA

### Roche / Genentech
- **Programs:** Prasinezumab (anti-alpha-synuclein), Selnoflast (NLRP3)
- **Conferences:** MDS 2024, AD/PD 2024-2025
- **Key Data:** PADOVA Phase 2b, advancing to Phase 3 (June 2025)
- **Partner:** Prothena (prasinezumab)
- **Headquarters:** Basel, Switzerland

### Bayer / BlueRock Therapeutics
- **Programs:** Bemdaneprocel (hESC cell therapy)
- **Conferences:** ISSCR 2024
- **Key Data:** Phase 1 18-month data in Nature, Phase 3 initiated
- **Regulatory:** SAKIGAKE designation (Japan)
- **Headquarters:** Cambridge, MA, USA (BlueRock)

### Biogen
- **Programs:** BIIB122/DNL151 (LRRK2 inhibitor), ION859/BIIB094 (LRRK2 ASO)
- **Conferences:** MDS 2024
- **Key Data:** LUMA Phase 2b ongoing
- **Partner:** Denali Therapeutics, Ionis Pharmaceuticals
- **Headquarters:** Cambridge, MA, USA

### Merck
- **Programs:** MK-7337 (alpha-synuclein PET tracer)
- **Conferences:** AD/PD 2025
- **Key Data:** First-in-human imaging in idiopathic PD
- **Headquarters:** Kenilworth, NJ, USA

- **Programs:** NT-0796 (NLRP3 inhibitor)
- **Conferences:** AD/PD 2024
