## Tier 1: Highest-Conviction Early-Stage

### Endlyz Therapeutics (Belgium/UK)
**Launched**: April 2025
**Seed Round**: 16M (SV Health + others)
**Focus**: Small molecules modulating lysosomal recycling (ATP13A2/ATP10B pathways)
**Indication**: Parkinson's disease, possibly ALS
**MJFF Involvement**: Confirmed
**Signal**: Fresh seed, novel target (ATP13A2 = MJFF priority target), top-tier VC

### Acurex Biosciences (US)
**Funding**: 1.6M from Parkinson's UK Virtual Biotech
**Program**: CU-13001
**Stage**: Late preclinical / IND-prep
**Target**: 15-lipoxygenase inhibitor
**Signal**: Novel mechanism, UK non-profit validation, pre-institutional round

### Axial Therapeutics (Woburn, MA)
**Funding**: Multiple MJFF grants
**Program**: AX-5006
**Stage**: IND-enabling (Phase 1 targeted 2024)
**Target**: Gut-restricted amyloid inhibitor
**Signal**: Gut-brain axis play; differentiated from crowded CNS space

## Mechanism-Based Early Signals

### Mitochondrial Quality Control (Hot)
- **USP30 inhibitors**: Vincere, Mission (both advanced)
- **mPTP inhibitors**: NRG Therapeutics (Series B)
- **Mitophagy inducers**: Academic grants (Harvard - Daniel Finley)
- **Signal**: Multiple funders validating mechanism; Vincere + Mission racing

### Lysosomal Targets (Emerging)
MJFF Targets to Therapies Initiative priority targets:
1. **TMEM175** - lysosomal potassium channel
2. **ATP13A2** - lysosomal P-type ATPase (Endlyz pursuing)
3. **MCOLN1/TRPML1** - lysosomal calcium channel
4. **OGA** - O-GlcNAcase

**Signal**: $19M committed; knowledge base launching end 2025 = company formation catalyst

### GBA/GCase (Validated Target, Crowded)
- Gain Therapeutics - MJFF funded
- Multiple academic programs
- **Signal**: Watch for differentiated approaches (chaperones vs enzyme replacement)

### LRRK2 (Validated, Competitive)
- SciNeuro - $5M MJFF for ASO approach (SNP614)
- Joined MJFF LITE consortium
- **Signal**: Next-gen modality (ASO) vs small molecule competitors
