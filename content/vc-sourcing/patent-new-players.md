## Companies NOT Currently in Our Main Tracking List

### 1. SciNeuro Pharmaceuticals (Clinical-stage)
- **Location**: China/US (clinical stage biotech)
- **Focus**: LRRK2 antisense oligonucleotide (ASO)
- **Lead Asset**: SNP614 - next-generation LNA-based ASO targeting LRRK2 mRNA
- **Stage**: Preclinical (IND-enabling, funded by $5M MJFF grant Nov 2025)
- **Mechanism**: Downregulates LRRK2 mRNA/protein expression in CNS
- **Notable**:
  - GSK license/option agreement for CNS therapies
  - Joined MJFF's LRRK2 LITE program
  - Demonstrated potent knockdown efficacy in NHP studies
- **Patent Status**: Technology based on validated LNA scaffold
- **Verification Needed**: IND timeline, clinical entry timeline
- **Why Interesting**: ASO modality differentiates from small molecule LRRK2 inhibitors; MJFF backing suggests validation

### 2. Progenra Inc (Preclinical)
- **Location**: Malvern, PA, USA
- **Focus**: PINK1 activators for mitochondrial dysfunction
- **Lead Asset**: PINK1 activator compounds (undisclosed names)
- **Stage**: Preclinical; targeting first-in-human within 2 years (announced July 2025)
- **Mechanism**: Small molecule activators restore function of mutant PINK1 kinase variants
- **Notable**:
  - June 2025 breakthrough: First evidence that pharmacological activation of defective PINK1 variants is therapeutically viable
  - Claims superior profile to MTK-458 (Mitokinin/AbbVie PINK1 activator)
- **Patent Status**: Filed (details unknown)
- **Verification Needed**: Funding status, preclinical data, IND timeline
- **Why Interesting**: Novel approach - activating mutant PINK1 rather than compensating for loss of function

### 3. Mission Therapeutics (Phase 1)
- **Location**: Cambridge, UK
- **Focus**: USP30 inhibitor for mitophagy enhancement
- **Lead Asset**: MTX325 - first-in-class USP30 inhibitor
- **Stage**: Phase 1 (PET scans confirmed brain penetration; Phase 1b in PD patients expected early 2025)
- **Mechanism**: Inhibits USP30 deubiquitylating enzyme to restore mitophagy of damaged mitochondria
- **Notable**:
  - $5.2M funding from MJFF and Parkinson's UK
  - $13.3M raised October 2025 for Phase 1b
  - Nature Communications publication on preclinical data
- **Patent Status**: Expected to have composition of matter patents (unverified)
- **Verification Needed**: Phase 1b enrollment status, biomarker data
- **Why Interesting**: Novel target (USP30); strong academic validation; PINK1/Parkin pathway adjacent

### 4. Capsida Biotherapeutics (IND-stage)
- **Location**: Thousand Oaks, CA, USA
- **Focus**: IV-administered AAV gene therapy for GBA-PD
- **Lead Asset**: CAP-003 - IV gene therapy delivering functional GBA1
- **Stage**: IND cleared June 2025; Phase 1 expected to initiate
- **Mechanism**: Engineered AAV capsid crosses BBB, delivers GBA1 gene broadly across CNS
- **Notable**:
  - Founded by Viviana Gradinaru (Caltech) research; backed by Versant and Westlake Village BioPartners
  - Patented novel human receptors for engineered capsids
  - Claims >70% neuronal expression, >20x liver detargeting vs AAV9
- **Patent Status**: Multiple patents on engineered capsids and receptors
- **Verification Needed**: Phase 1 enrollment, dosing timeline
- **Why Interesting**: IV-administered gene therapy (vs intracranial); differentiates from Voyager, Prevail (Lilly)

### 5. InBrain Pharma (Phase 1/2)
- **Location**: France
- **Focus**: Intracranial dopamine delivery for advanced PD
- **Lead Asset**: Anaerobic dopamine delivery system
- **Stage**: Phase 1/2 (results published Nature Medicine Jan 2025)
- **Mechanism**: Direct dopamine delivery to brain (symptomatic, not disease-modifying)
- **Notable**:
  - Exclusive worldwide patent license from SATT Nord
  - "Durably modify advanced PD management"
- **Patent Status**: Exclusive license from academic institution
- **Verification Needed**: Phase 2 plans, commercialization pathway
- **Why Interesting**: Novel approach to symptomatic treatment; may improve motor fluctuations

### 6. Kenai Therapeutics (Preclinical/IND)
- **Location**: San Diego, CA, USA
- **Focus**: iPSC-derived cell therapy for PD
- **Lead Asset**: iPSC-derived dopaminergic neurons
- **Stage**: Clinical trial expected within 2024 (per Feb 2024 announcement)
- **Mechanism**: Induced pluripotent stem cell-derived DA neurons
- **Notable**:
  - $82M Series A (Feb 2024) - Cure Ventures, The Column Group, Alaska Permanent Fund
  - First investment by Cure Ventures ($350M fund)
- **Patent Status**: Expected cell therapy composition/method patents
- **Verification Needed**: Current trial status, differentiation vs BlueRock
- **Why Interesting**: Major VC backing; Cure Ventures specializes in PD

### 7. NRG Therapeutics Ltd (IND-enabling)
- **Location**: UK
- **Focus**: Mitochondrial permeability transition pore (mPTP) inhibitor
- **Lead Asset**: NRG5051
- **Stage**: IND-enabling; clinical trials initiated 2025 per funding round
- **Mechanism**: Small molecule blocks mPTP to protect mitochondria
- **Notable**:
  - Parkinson's Foundation Virtual Biotech investment
  - October 2024: NRG5051 selected for development
  - Sept 2025: 50M GBP Series B funding
- **Patent Status**: Expected composition of matter patents
- **Verification Needed**: Clinical trial registration, IND status
- **Why Interesting**: Novel target (mPTP); broad CNS applicability (also ALS)

### 8. Hope Biosciences Research Foundation (Phase 2)
- **Location**: Texas, USA
- **Focus**: Adipose-derived mesenchymal stem cell therapy
- **Lead Asset**: HB-adMSC (allogeneic)
- **Stage**: Phase 2 completed; Phase 3 planned
- **Mechanism**: Allogeneic adipose-derived MSCs
- **Notable**:
  - Statistically significant motor function improvements in Phase 2
  - Eyeing Phase 3 confirmatory trial
- **Patent Status**: Unknown
- **Verification Needed**: Phase 3 protocol, FDA interactions
- **Why Interesting**: Non-profit advancing cell therapy; may offer insights into MSC mechanism

### 9. Zambon Biotech (Phase 3b)
- **Location**: Italy
- **Focus**: Extended-release levodopa/carbidopa
- **Lead Asset**: IPX203
- **Stage**: Phase 3b (ADIP study; first patient dosed Dec 2025); EMA submission June 2025
- **Mechanism**: Extended-release LD/CD formulation (symptomatic)
- **Notable**:
  - Licensed from Amneal Pharmaceuticals
  - Competing with VYALEV (AbbVie), ND0612 (Mitsubishi Tanabe)
- **Patent Status**: Licensed IP from Amneal
- **Verification Needed**: EMA approval timeline
- **Why Interesting**: European market focus; late-stage symptomatic treatment

### 10. Advanced Protein Technologies Corp (Early)
- **Location**: Asia
- **Focus**: 2'-FL containing compositions for dopamine diseases
- **Patent**: US20240358729A1 (2024)
- **Stage**: Early development
- **Mechanism**: Novel compositions addressing dopamine reduction
- **Verification Needed**: Development status, clinical plans
- **Why Interesting**: Asian company with novel approach
