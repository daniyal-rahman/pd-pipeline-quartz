## IV. Due Diligence Questions for University IP

When evaluating university patents for licensing potential:

1. **IP Strength**:
   - [ ] Is composition of matter protected or just methods?
   - [ ] What is the remaining patent life?
   - [ ] Are there blocking patents from competitors?
   - [ ] Is there freedom to operate in key markets?

2. **Development Status**:
   - [ ] Has the technology been validated in disease-relevant models?
   - [ ] Is there any clinical data (even pilot studies)?
   - [ ] What is the IND-enabling requirement?
   - [ ] Who would conduct preclinical work if licensed?

3. **Inventor Involvement**:
   - [ ] Is the key inventor willing to consult/advise?
   - [ ] Would they consider founding or joining a spin-out?
   - [ ] Do they have competing interests?

4. **Licensing Terms**:
   - [ ] Is the university seeking exclusive or non-exclusive license?
   - [ ] What are typical milestone and royalty expectations?
   - [ ] Are there field-of-use restrictions?
   - [ ] Government rights (if NIH-funded)?

5. **Competitive Landscape**:
   - [ ] Are there commercial programs targeting the same mechanism?
   - [ ] What is the differentiation vs. existing approaches?
   - [ ] Would the licensed IP provide freedom to operate?
