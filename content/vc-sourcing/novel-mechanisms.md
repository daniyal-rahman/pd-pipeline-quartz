## Overview
New therapeutic approaches presented at major conferences, representing potential investment opportunities in emerging mechanisms beyond traditional dopamine replacement.

## 1. Alpha-Synuclein PET Imaging

### The Opportunity
First-ever ability to visualize alpha-synuclein aggregates in living PD patients. Enables:
- Earlier/more accurate diagnosis
- Patient stratification for clinical trials
- Target engagement assessment for therapies

### Companies & Programs

**Merck - MK-7337**
- Presented at AD/PD 2025 (Vienna)
- First-in-human study: 8 idiopathic PD patients (3+ years diagnosis)
- Detected alpha-synuclein in substantia nigra and brainstem
- Radioactively labeled tracer binds to toxic fibrils but not monomers
- Stage: First-in-human completed

**AC Immune - ACI-12589 / ACI-15916**
- ACI-12589 detects alpha-synuclein in MSA (denser aggregates)
- ACI-15916: More sensitive derivative for PD
- First-in-human trial planned for early 2026
- Stage: Preclinical/IND-enabling

**SY08 (MassGeneral Brigham/Harvard)**
- FDA approved for human studies October 2023
- Tested in 7 PD patients, 4 DLB patients, 7 controls
- Signal in brainstems of PD patients
- Stage: Early clinical

**18F-C05-05 (Japanese Academic)**
- Binds midbrain in PD/DLB patients
- Technical challenges: slow uptake, rapid metabolism
- Stage: Early clinical optimization needed

### Investment Signal
- MJFF funding 4 projects (Ken Griffin Alpha-synuclein Imaging Competition)
- Field-wide "acute need" for validated tracer
- Major pharma (Merck) and academic centers competing

## 2. NLRP3 Inflammasome Inhibition

### The Opportunity
Neuroinflammation increasingly recognized as driver of PD progression. NLRP3 activated by alpha-synuclein aggregates. IL-1beta and IL-18 elevated in PD CSF.

### Companies & Programs

**NodThera - NT-0796**
- Presented at AD/PD 2024 (Lisbon)
- Brain-penetrant NLRP3 inhibitor
- Phase 1b: Reduced neuroinflammatory biomarkers to healthy control levels
- 28-day sustained dosing demonstrated
- Stage: Phase 1b completed

**Ventyx Biosciences - VTX3232**
- Presented at MDS 2025 (Honolulu)
- Phase 2a: 40mg daily oral dose
- Safe, improved motor AND nonmotor symptoms
- CSF drug levels >3-fold over IC90
- Significant NLRP3 biomarker reduction
- Stage: Phase 2a completed

**Roche - Selnoflast**
- Phase 1b randomized placebo-controlled trial
- 72 participants with early PD
- 28-day treatment
- Results expected 2025
- Stage: Phase 1b ongoing

**Olatec Therapeutics - Dapansutrile (OLT1177)**
- Phase 2 planned for early PD
- 36 patients, 6-month placebo-controlled + 6-month open label
- Funded by Cure Parkinson's grant
- Stage: Not yet initiated

### Investment Signal
- Multiple companies validating the mechanism
- Phase 2 data emerging showing clinical benefit
- Fast-moving field with 2025-2026 inflection points

## 3. GBA1/GCase Modulation

### The Opportunity
GBA1 mutations present in up to 15% of PD patients - largest genetic risk factor. Restoring GCase function may address underlying pathology.

### Companies & Programs

**Gain Therapeutics - GT-02287**
- Presented at SfN 2025, AD/PD conferences
- Allosteric modulator restoring GCase function
- Phase 1: Safe, CSF penetration confirmed
- Phase 1b: First-ever reduction of GluSph substrate in PD patient CSF
- 21 patients enrolled, 79% continuing extension
- MJFF, Silverstein Foundation funding support
- Stage: Phase 1b ongoing, extension to September 2026

**BIA 28-6156 (ACTIVATE Trial)**
- Phase 2 brain-penetrant GCase activator
- 237 genetically confirmed GBA-PD participants
- Three arms: 10mg, 60mg, placebo
- Stage: Phase 2 recruiting

**Prevail Therapeutics - Gene Therapy (PROPEL)**
- One-time injection gene therapy for GBA1-PD
- Intracisterna magna delivery
- Stage: Clinical trial ongoing

**Capsida Therapeutics - CAP-003**
- IV-delivered gene therapy for GBA-associated PD
- Presented at ASGCT 2025
- >200-fold brain RNA expression vs AAV9
- On track for clinic H1 2025
- Stage: IND-enabling

### Investment Signal
- Multiple modalities: small molecule, gene therapy
- First biomarker evidence (CSF GluSph reduction) achieved
- Clear genetic subset for patient stratification

## 4. LRRK2 Inhibition

### The Opportunity
LRRK2 mutations: major genetic cause of autosomal-dominant PD. 4-5% familial, 1-2% sporadic PD.

### Companies & Programs

**Denali Therapeutics/Biogen - BIIB122 (DNL151)**
- Presented at MDS 2024
- Oral, brain-penetrant LRRK2 inhibitor
- BEACON study: Phase 2a in LRRK2-associated PD (50 participants)
- LUMA study: Phase 2b in early-stage PD (650 enrolled at 98 centers)
- Expected completion December 2025
- LIGHTHOUSE study discontinued (complexity/timeline concerns)
- Stage: Phase 2a/2b ongoing

**Neuron23 - NEU-411**
- Presented at AD/PD 2025
- Brain-penetrant, potent, selective LRRK2 inhibitor
- Phase 1: Robust target and pathway engagement in 150 volunteers
- NEULARK Phase 2 trial (NCT06680830) initiated early 2025
- Potential to slow progression in 30% of LRRK2-driven PD
- Stage: Phase 2 initiating

**Ionis Pharmaceuticals - ION859 (BIIB094)**
- Antisense medicine targeting LRRK2 mRNA
- Prevents LRRK2 protein production
- Stage: Phase 2 evaluation

**Arvinas - ARV-102**
- First PROTAC approach to LRRK2
- Stage: Preclinical/early development

### Investment Signal
- Established genetic validation
- Multiple mechanisms: small molecule, ASO, PROTAC
- Phase 2b data expected 2025

## 5. Anti-Alpha-Synuclein Antibodies

### The Opportunity
Direct targeting of pathological alpha-synuclein aggregates for disease modification.

### Companies & Programs

**Roche/Prothena - Prasinezumab**
- Presented at MDS 2024 (PASADENA extension)
- Phase 2b PADOVA: 586 patients, missed primary endpoint (p=0.0657)
- Pre-specified levodopa subgroup: HR=0.79 (significant)
- First biomarker evidence of impacting disease biology
- Decision June 2025: Proceeding to Phase 3
- 900+ patients treated, 750+ in open-label extensions
- Stage: Phase 3 initiating

### Investment Signal
- Despite missed primary, advancing to Phase 3
- Subgroup analysis and long-term OLE data supportive
- Largest alpha-synuclein program in development

## 6. Mitochondrial Protection

### The Opportunity
Mitochondrial dysfunction implicated in PD pathogenesis. Blood-brain barrier penetrant mitochondrial protectants.

### Companies & Programs

**NRG Therapeutics - NRG5051**
- Small molecules patching mitochondrial walls
- October 2024: Selected lead compound
- September 2025: Raised funding for clinical trials
- Dual indication: PD and motor neuron disease
- Virtual Biotech funded
- Stage: IND-enabling/Phase 1 preparing

### Investment Signal
- Novel mechanism with strong preclinical rationale
- Dual indication opportunity
- Recently funded for clinical advancement

## 7. TYK2/JAK1 Inhibition

### The Opportunity
Targeting inflammatory pathways via kinase inhibition.

### Companies & Programs

**Biohaven - BHV-8000**
- Phase 1 in 58 healthy adults
- Safe, well tolerated
- Brain-penetrant with sustained CNS levels
- Significant reduction in blood inflammation biomarkers
- Stage: Phase 1 completed

### Investment Signal
- Clean safety profile
- CNS exposure demonstrated
- Moving toward PD-specific trials

## 8. Novel D1/D5 Dopamine Agonism

### The Opportunity
Selective D1/D5 receptor partial agonism as differentiated symptomatic therapy.

### Companies & Programs

**AbbVie (ex-Cerevel) - Tavapadon**
- Presented at MDS 2025 (TEMPO-4 extension)
- First selective D1/D5 dopamine receptor agonist
- Phase 3 TEMPO program completed
- NDA submitted to FDA
- Monotherapy AND adjunctive therapy data
- Stage: NDA filed, awaiting approval

### Investment Signal
- Regulatory filing achieved
- New mechanism vs existing D2 agonists
- $8.7B acquisition validates opportunity
