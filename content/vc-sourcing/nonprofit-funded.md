# Non-Profit Funded Industry Programs (PD)

#claude


*Last Updated: January 2026*

Parkinson's UK Virtual Biotech, Cure Parkinson's iLCT, and ASAP represent major non-profit funding sources that often identify companies before they raise institutional capital.

## Parkinson's UK Virtual Biotech

**Committed**: >30M (program lifetime)
**2024 Commitment**: 8M to 5 projects
**Model**: Research-driven investment fund; partners with 60+ organizations globally

### Funded Companies

| Company | Investment | Program | Stage | Mechanism |
|---------|------------|---------|-------|-----------|
| **NRG Therapeutics** (UK) | 1M initial + 50M follow-on | NRG5051 | Phase 1 ready (2026) | mPTP inhibitor; mitochondrial protection |
| **Mission Therapeutics** (UK) | 1.25M | MTX325 | Phase 1 | USP30 inhibitor; mitochondrial quality |
| **Herantis Pharma** (Finland) | 1.55M | HER-096 | Phase 1 | Dopamine neuron protection/restoration |
| **Acurex Biosciences** (US) | 1.6M | CU-13001 | Preclinical/IND-prep | 15-lipoxygenase inhibitor |

### NRG Therapeutics Deep Dive
- **Location**: Stevenage Bioscience Catalyst, UK
- **Investors**: Parkinson's UK, Omega Funds, Brandon Capital
- **Total Raised**: 67M Series B (2025)
- **Grants**: MJFF, Target ALS, Innovate UK Biomedical Catalyst
- **Timeline**: Clinical trials begin 2026 for PD and ALS
- **Signal**: Multi-funder validation; rare dual-indication approach (PD + ALS)

### Herantis Pharma - HER-096 Update
- Phase 1 safety study in 60 healthy participants completed
- October 2025: Results showed safe, well-tolerated, effective brain penetration in PD patients
- **Signal**: De-risked safety profile

## Cure Parkinson's iLCT Program

**Total Investment**: 10.3M+ (with partners)
**Model**: Drug repurposing - testing approved drugs for PD disease modification
**Partnership**: Van Andel Institute (VAI), John Black Charitable Foundation

### Program Stats (2012-2024)
- 239 drug dossiers evaluated
- 171 unique agents reviewed
- 21 completed clinical trials
- 20 ongoing clinical trials

### iLCT Pipeline Research Acceleration Programme (2024-2025)

**2024 Funded Projects**:
| Drug | Researcher | Institution | Focus |
|------|------------|-------------|-------|
| Probucol | Dr Poonam Thakur | Indian Inst Science Education & Research | Neuroprotection |
| Chlorogenic acid | Dr Poonam Thakur | Indian Inst Science Education & Research | Neuroprotection |
| Carnosic acid | Dr Sinead O'Sullivan, Prof Donato Di Monte | German Centre for Neurodegenerative Diseases | Neuroprotection |

**2025 Drugs Under Evaluation** (max 250K per project):
- Empagliflozin (SGLT2 inhibitor)
- Theracurmin (curcumin formulation)
- Pyridoxal-5-phosphate (Vitamin B6 derivative)
- Alogliptin (DPP-4 inhibitor)

### Major iLCT Clinical Trials

| Trial | Drug | Status | Notes |
|-------|------|--------|-------|

## ASAP (Aligning Science Across Parkinson's)

**Total Commitment**: $321M over 8 years
**Model**: Open science, collaborative research, data sharing
**Implementation Partner**: Michael J. Fox Foundation

### Funded Programs (Academic-Focused)

| Program | Funding | Focus |
|---------|---------|-------|
| Collaborative Research Network (CRN) | $290M+ | 35 teams, 163 investigators, 80+ institutions |
| PPMI | $125M+ injection | Biomarker study; 4,000+ volunteers |
| Global Parkinson's Genetics Program (GP2) | Significant | Genotype 150K+ volunteers globally |
| AMP PD | Public-private | Data harmonization; biomarker validation |
| iNDI-PD | Significant | iPSC resources for PD research |

### Industry Involvement

**PPMI Industry Partners**: 40+ biotech/pharma companies (financial + in-kind support)
- Pre-competitive consortium model
- Data/biorepository access for drug developers

**AMP PD Partners**: NIH + multiple biopharma companies + non-profits

### 2025 Funding Opportunities

**Technical Track** (announced 2025):
- Up to $2M/year for 3 years ($6M total)
- Focus: Novel tools for PD research
- Decisions: February 2026

**Scientific Track**:
- Applications Jan-March 2025
- Focus: PD heterogeneity mechanisms
