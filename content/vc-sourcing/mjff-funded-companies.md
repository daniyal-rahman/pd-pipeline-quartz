Grant funding often precedes public announcements by 1-2 years. MJFF is the world's largest nonprofit funder of Parkinson's research ($2B+ funded to date).

## Therapeutics Pipeline Program Recipients (2024-2025)

The Therapeutics Pipeline Program is **industry-only** (or industry-academia collaborations). Awards range from $250K for targeted programs to $2M+ for multi-stage preclinical/clinical programs.

## Other Major MJFF Industry Grants (2024-2025)

### Grifols/Alkahest - $21M (January 2025)
- **Project**: Chronos-PD biomarker study
- **Focus**: Analyzing plasma proteins from PD patients over 10 years to identify early biomarkers
- **Significance**: Largest single diagnostic grant; leverages massive plasma sample collection

## MJFF Industry Programs

### LITE Program (LRRK2 Investigative Therapeutics Exchange)
Pre-competitive consortium for LRRK2-focused development. SciNeuro joined in 2025.

### Targets to Therapies Initiative - $19M (December 2025)
Five priority targets being advanced:
1. **TMEM175** - lysosomal potassium channel
2. **ATP13A2** - lysosomal P-type ATPase
3. **MCOLN1/TRPML1** - lysosomal calcium channel
4. **OGA** - O-GlcNAcase
5. **NOD2** - innate immune receptor

*Public knowledge base launching end of 2025 for drug developers*

## VC Sourcing Signals

### Highest Conviction (Multiple Funding Sources)
- **NRG Therapeutics** - MJFF + Parkinson's UK Virtual Biotech + Target ALS; raised $67M Series B
- **Mission Therapeutics** - MJFF + Parkinson's UK; Phase 1 ongoing
- **Vincere Biosciences** - Prior MJFF grants (2019, 2022) + new $5M; Boston-based

### Early-Stage Watch
- **Neuronity Therapeutics** - gene therapy approach, less crowded space
- **Axial Therapeutics** - gut-brain axis, novel mechanism
- **Endlyz Therapeutics** - launched April 2025 with MJFF involvement, 16M seed

### Mechanism Trends
1. **Mitochondrial quality control** - USP30 inhibitors (Vincere, Mission), mPTP (NRG)
2. **Proteasome activation** - Booster Therapeutics
3. **LRRK2** - SciNeuro (ASO approach)
4. **GBA/Lysosomal** - Gain Therapeutics, Targets to Therapies Initiative
