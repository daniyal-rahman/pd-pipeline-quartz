# Parkinson's Disease Conference Presentations (2024-2025)

#claude


## Overview
This document catalogs company-sponsored presentations and key abstracts from major neuroscience and movement disorder conferences. Conference data typically appears 6-12 months before peer-reviewed publication.

---

## MDS International Congress 2024 (Philadelphia, September 27 - October 1)

### Company-Sponsored Sessions
- 60-minute company-led informational sessions (Non-CME)
- 30-minute company-led presentations highlighting therapeutic advancements

### Key Abstracts Presented

**Alpha-synuclein Antibodies (PASADENA Extension)**
- Extended data from participants receiving prasinezumab
- Showed slower decline in MDS-UPDRS Part III vs external control
- Sponsor: Roche/Prothena

**Subcutaneous Foslevodopa/Foscarbidopa**
- Post hoc efficacy analysis of Phase 3 trials
- Demonstrated improved motor function, daily activities, sleep, and QoL vs oral carbidopa/levodopa
- FDA approved formulation
- Sponsor: AbbVie

**NouvNeu001 First-in-Human**
- iPSC-derived dopaminergic precursor cells
- First administration in a PD patient
- Good safety/tolerability profile
- Improvement in motor scores
- Sponsor: NouvNeu

**Aspen Neuroscience Surgical Safety Data**
- ASPIRO trial first cohort surgical data
- Autologous iPSC-derived cell therapy
- Sponsor: Aspen Neuroscience

**BIIB122/DNL151 Study Design**
- LRRK2 inhibition safety, pharmacodynamics, and pharmacokinetics
- Sponsor: Denali Therapeutics/Biogen

## MDS International Congress 2025 (Honolulu, October 5-9)

### Key Presentations

**TEMPO-4 Open-Label Extension (Late-Breaker)**
- Tavapadon sustained long-term safety data
- Sponsor: AbbVie

**VTX3232 NLRP3 Inhibitor Phase 2a**
- Safe and improved motor/nonmotor symptoms in early PD
- 40mg oral daily dose over 28 days
- Significant reduction in NLRP3-related biomarkers
- CSF drug levels exceeded IC90 by >3-fold
- Sponsor: Ventyx Biosciences

## AD/PD Conference 2025 (Vienna, April 1-5)

### Statistics
- 5,500+ participants from 70+ countries
- 2,500+ abstracts submitted

### Key Presentations

**Alpha-Synuclein PET Tracers**
- MK-7337 first-in-human results (Merck)
- Detected alpha-synuclein in substantia nigra in idiopathic PD
- 8 patients with 3+ years PD diagnosis tested
- Major breakthrough for in vivo synuclein imaging

**PPMI Data Updates**
- Parkinson's Progression Markers Initiative findings
- Largest PD biobank driving research

**CUE1 Device Pilot Study**
- Non-pharmacological intervention
- May improve motor symptoms and reduce fall risk

**NEU-411 Phase 1 Data**
- Brain-penetrant LRRK2 inhibitor
- Healthy volunteer pharmacokinetic data
- Robust target and pathway engagement
- Sponsor: Neuron23

## ASGCT 2024 Annual Meeting (May 7-11)

### Statistics
- 1,983 abstracts presented (12% increase from 2023)
- Session tracks: Cancer, Clinical Science, Gene Editing, Regulatory, Viral Vectors

### Key Gene Therapy Updates

**AAV2-GAD Phase 1/2 Study**
- Double-blind placebo-controlled study (14 PD patients)
- Completed September 2024
- Safe, well tolerated
- Significant motor improvement in high-dose group

**AAV2-AADC (VY-AADC01)**
- Phase 1 safety and AADC enzyme activity
- 15 subjects with moderately advanced PD
- Well tolerated, enzyme activity correlated with dose
- Improved UPDRS III, motor fluctuations, QoL

## Key Trends from Conference Circuit

1. **Cell Therapy Acceleration**: Multiple companies presenting clinical data (BlueRock, Aspen, NouvNeu)

3. **NLRP3 Inflammasome**: Emerging target with multiple Phase 1/2 programs
4. **GBA1/GCase**: Active clinical development across multiple modalities
5. **LRRK2 Inhibitors**: Continued investment despite program adjustments
