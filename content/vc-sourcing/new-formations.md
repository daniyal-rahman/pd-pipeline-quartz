## Companies Founded 2024-2025

### Endlyz Therapeutics
- **Founded:** 2025 (launched April 2025)
- **Location:** Belgium/UK
- **Funding:** EUR 16M seed
- **Investors:** SV Health Investors, Michael J. Fox Foundation (strategic)
- **Focus:** Small molecules modulating lysosomal recycling (ATP13A2/ATP10B pathways)
- **Target Diseases:** Parkinson's disease, ALS
- **Mechanism:** Cellular "clean-up" to slow/halt neurodegeneration
- **Stage:** Preclinical
- **Website:** TBD
- **Source:** [Labiotech](https://www.labiotech.eu/in-depth/parkinsons-disease-biotechs-pursuit-for-more-treatments/)

### Nilo Therapeutics
- **Founded:** 2024 (launched October 2025)
- **Location:** New York, NY
- **Funding:** $101M Series A
- **Investors:** The Column Group, Gates Frontier, Lux Capital, DCVS Bio, Alexandria Venture Investments
- **Founders:**
  - Charles Zuker, Ph.D. (Columbia University)
  - Ruslan Medzhitov, Ph.D. (Yale School of Medicine)
  - Steve Liberles, Ph.D. (Harvard Medical School)
- **Leadership:**
  - CEO: Kim Seth (ex-Repare Therapeutics)

- **Focus:** Neuro-immunology therapies targeting neuronal circuits
- **Relevance to PD:** Neuro-immune axis increasingly implicated in neurodegeneration
- **Stage:** Discovery/Preclinical
- **Source:** [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/immunology-biotech-nilo-launches-101m-and-mission-harness-neural-circuits)

### Trace Neuroscience
- **Founded:** 2024 (launched November 2024)
- **Location:** South San Francisco, CA
- **Funding:** $101M Series A
- **Investors:** Third Rock Ventures (lead), Atlas Venture, GV, RA Capital Management
- **Founders/Leadership:**
  - Pietro Fratta, MD, PhD (UCL, Professor of Cellular and Molecular Neuroscience)
  - Aaron Gitler, PhD (Stanford, Professor of Genetics)
  - Eric Green, CEO (co-founder of Maze Therapeutics)
- **Focus:** Restoring UNC13A protein function in neurodegenerative diseases
- **Target Diseases:** ALS (initial), potential expansion to other neurodegeneration
- **Mechanism:** Novel therapies to restore healthy nerve-muscle communication
- **IP:** Exclusive license from UCL
- **Stage:** Preclinical
- **Source:** [UCLB Press Release](https://www.uclb.com/2024/11/12/trace-neuroscience-launches-with-exclusive-license-from-ucl-and-101-million-series-a-funding-to-take-forward-potential-new-therapeutic-for-motor-neurone-disease/)

- **Investors:** Nexus NeuroTech Ventures (lead), Foothill Ventures, Verge HealthTech Fund, WARF

## Companies Founded 2022-2023

### Kenai Therapeutics (formerly Ryne Bio)
- **Founded:** 2022
- **Location:** San Diego, CA
- **Funding:** $82M Series A (February 2024) + $4M CIRM grant
- **Investors:** Cure Ventures (co-lead), The Column Group (co-lead), Alaska Permanent Fund Corp
- **Founders:**
  - Howard Federoff, MD, PhD (Cure Parkinson's iLCT committee)
  - Jeff Kordower, PhD (Cure Parkinson's iLCT committee)
- **Focus:** Allogeneic iPSC-derived cell therapies for neuron replacement
- **Lead Candidate:** RNDP-001 (dopamine neuron replacement for Parkinson's)
- **Technology:** Genetically reprogrammed stem cells to restore dopamine production
- **Stage:** Phase 1 clinical trial (2024/2025)
- **Source:** [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2024/02/29/kenai-therapeutics-parkinsons-disease-stem-cell-therapy/)

### NRG Therapeutics (Recent Growth Phase)
- **Founded:** Earlier (significant growth 2023-2025)
- **Location:** UK
- **Funding:** GBP 49.7M Series B (2025)
- **Investors:** SV Health Investors' Dementia Discovery Fund (lead), Parkinson's Virtual Biotech (early supporter)
- **Focus:** Mitochondrial permeability transition pore (mPTP) targeting
- **Lead Candidate:** NRG5051
- **Target Diseases:** Parkinson's disease, ALS/MND
- **Stage:** Clinical trials initiated (2025)
- **Mechanism:** Blood-brain barrier-penetrating small molecules that patch mitochondrial wall holes
- **Source:** [BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/nrg-therapeutics-series-b-parkinsons-als-mitochondria-mptp/759403/)

### Cure Ventures (VC Fund - Enabler)

- **Focus:** Cell therapy and regenerative medicine for neurological conditions

## Collaborations and Joint Ventures (2023-2024)

### Neurocrine/Voyager GBA1 Gene Therapy Collaboration
- **Announced:** January 2023
- **Partners:** Neurocrine Biosciences + Voyager Therapeutics
- **Focus:** GBA1 gene therapy for Parkinson's and GBA1-mediated diseases
- **Stage:** Preclinical
- **Significance:** Major pharma commitment to genetic PD approaches

### GSK/Muna Therapeutics Alliance
- **Announced:** December 2024
- **Partners:** GSK + Muna Therapeutics
- **Focus:** Identify and validate novel drug targets for Alzheimer's (potentially expandable to PD)

- **Significance:** Validates Muna's neurodegeneration approach

- **Value:** $1.4 billion

- **Significance:** Validates BBB-crossing technology value

## Company Formation Trends (2024-2025)

### Therapeutic Approaches Gaining Traction
1. **Cell Therapy** - Multiple companies pursuing iPSC-derived dopamine neurons
2. **Gene Therapy** - GBA1, GDNF delivery, Parkin gene approaches
3. **Mitochondrial Targeting** - mPTP, mitophagy enhancement
4. **Lysosomal Modulation** - ATP13A2/ATP10B pathways
5. **Neuro-Immunology** - Brain-body circuit regulation
6. **BBB-Crossing Platforms** - Enabling delivery of various modalities

### Geographic Distribution
- **USA:** San Francisco Bay Area, San Diego, Boston/Cambridge, New York
- **UK:** London, Cambridge, Oxford
- **Europe:** Belgium, Sweden, Germany
- **Emerging:** Israel, Australia

### Funding Environment
- 2024 neurotech: $2.3B across 129 deals (up from $1.4B in 2023)
- Projection for 2025: Potentially $4B+
- Series A sizes increasing: $80-100M+ becoming common for neuro
- Seed rounds: Average ~$2.1M in neurotech
