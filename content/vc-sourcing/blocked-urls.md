## Tech Transfer Database Access Issues

### Stanford TechFinder
- **URL**: https://techfinder.stanford.edu/search?q=parkinson
- **Issue**: 404 Error - search endpoint not accessible via automated fetch
- **Workaround**: Direct contact with Stanford OTL (otl.stanford.edu)
- **Note**: Known to have LRRK2 inhibitors from Montine Lab available for licensing

### University of California Tech Transfer
- **URL**: https://techtransfer.universityofcalifornia.edu/search?q=parkinson
- **Issue**: Error page displayed during automated access
- **Workaround**: Contact UC.technologies@ucop.edu or ite_support@universityofcalifornia.edu
- **Note**: UC Davis serotonin modulators for PD confirmed available

### Harvard OTD Commercialization Portal
- **URL**: https://otd.harvard.edu/industry-investors/commercialization-opportunities/
- **Issue**: Landing page only - no direct technology listings in crawlable format
- **Workaround**: Navigate to CNS/Neurology category manually or contact OTD directly
- **Note**: CNS/Neurology category exists but requires interactive database access

## Recommended Alternative Sources

For complete portfolio information, consider:

1. **SEC EDGAR Filings**
   - Form D filings disclose some private investments
   - URL: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany

2. **Crunchbase**
   - Free tier provides basic investment data
   - URL: https://www.crunchbase.com/

3. **BioPharma Dive / Fierce Biotech**
   - Funding round announcements
   - No paywall for news articles

4. **LinkedIn Company Pages**
   - Often list investor relationships
   - Employee movements signal portfolio activity

5. **Direct VC Contact**
   - Most VCs have contact forms for deal flow
   - May provide portfolio information upon request

## Data Quality Notes

### High Confidence Sources
- Company press releases (direct information)
- VC news pages (curated announcements)
- Clinical trial registrations (verified data)

### Medium Confidence Sources
- News articles (may have errors)
- Database aggregators (may be outdated)
- Web searches (snapshot in time)

### Low Confidence Sources
- Unofficial databases
- Social media
- Unverified blog posts
