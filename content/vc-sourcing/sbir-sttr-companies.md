# NIH SBIR/STTR Parkinson's Disease Companies

#claude


*Last Updated: January 2026*

SBIR/STTR grants are America's Seed Fund - the largest source of early-stage capital for tech commercialization in the US. These identify companies 1-2 years before they typically raise institutional rounds.

**Important**: SBIR/STTR legislative authority expired October 1, 2025. NIH is not accepting new applications until Congress acts.

---

## Confirmed SBIR/STTR Parkinson's Recipients (2024-2025)

### CND Life Sciences (Scottsdale, AZ)
**Award**: $3M SBIR (October 2025) - 4th NIH-SBIR grant in 5 years
- **Product**: Syn-One Test (cutaneous alpha-synuclein diagnostic)
- **Study**: "Syn-T Study" - testing if P-SYN in essential tremor patients predicts PD risk
- **Stage**: Commercial product (diagnostic already available)
- **Signal**: Repeat SBIR winner = strong execution; diagnostic focus

### Allyx Therapeutics
**Award**: $3.3M SBIR Commercial Readiness Pilot (October 2024)
- **Product**: ALX-001 - oral, synapse-targeted disease-modifying therapy
- **Indication**: Alzheimer's and Parkinson's disease
- **Total SBIR/Foundation Funding**: $23M+ (NIH, SBIR, Alzheimer's Assoc, MJFF)
- **Signal**: Commercial readiness pilot = late-stage SBIR, closer to market

### Sinopia Biosciences
**Award**: $3.3M Fast-Track SBIR (NINDS)
- **Product**: Novel small molecule for PD
- **Stage**: IND-enabling studies
- **Platform**: LEADS platform - ML/multi-omics drug discovery
- **Mechanism**: Novel MOA; showed unique pharmacology in rodent/NHP models
- **Signal**: Fast-Track = accelerated review, high NIH confidence

### Automated Imaging Diagnostics
**Award**: $736K STTR Phase II (2024) - Contract 2R42NS132614-02
- **Product**: neuropacs software - AI-powered brain imaging diagnostic
- **Application**: Differentiate PD vs MSAp vs PSP (>90% accuracy in Phase I)
- **Technology**: Diffusion MRI-based classification
- **Signal**: Phase II = validated Phase I; diagnostic tools market

## SBIR/STTR Program Structure

| Phase | Purpose | Typical Award |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Phase I | Establish technical feasibility | Up to $314K |
| Phase II | Continue R&D toward commercialization | Up to $2.1M |
| Phase IIB | Bridge "Valley of Death" to FDA approval | Variable |

## How to Find More SBIR/STTR Recipients

### NIH RePORTER Database
1. Go to: https://reporter.nih.gov
2. Search keywords: "Parkinson" or "Parkinson's disease"
3. Filter by Funding Mechanism: "Research Project Grants SBIR/STTR"
4. Filter by Fiscal Year: 2024, 2025

### SBIR.gov Awards Database
1. Go to: https://www.sbir.gov/awards
2. Search by topic area (neurodegenerative) or keyword (Parkinson)
