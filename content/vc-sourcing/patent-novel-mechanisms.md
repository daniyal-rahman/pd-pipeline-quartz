## I. Novel Targets Emerging in Patents

### 1. USP30 (Deubiquitylating Enzyme) - Mitophagy Enhancement

**Mechanism**: USP30 is a mitochondrial deubiquitylating enzyme that removes ubiquitin "flags" from damaged mitochondria, preventing their clearance via mitophagy. Inhibiting USP30 restores normal mitophagy.

**Key Players**:
| Company | Asset | Stage | Notes |
|---------|-------|-------|-------|
| Mission Therapeutics | MTX325 | Phase 1 | First-in-class; brain penetrant |

**Scientific Rationale**:
- USP30 inhibition is downstream of PINK1/Parkin pathway
- Preclinical evidence shows protection against dopaminergic neuron loss
- Reduces phospho-alpha-synuclein and glial activation in models
- Published in Nature Communications (2024)

**Investment Thesis**: Novel target with solid academic validation; complements PINK1 activator approaches

---

### 2. PINK1 Direct Activation - Restoring Mutant Function

**Mechanism**: PINK1 kinase is essential for mitophagy initiation. Loss-of-function PINK1 mutations cause familial PD. Direct PINK1 activators restore function of mutant enzymes.

**Key Players**:
| Company | Asset | Stage | Notes |
|---------|-------|-------|-------|
| Progenra Inc | Undisclosed | Preclinical | Claims first evidence of mutant rescue |
| Mitokinin/AbbVie | MTK-458 | Preclinical | Published PINK1 activator |

**Scientific Rationale**:
- Paradigm shift: Previously, PINK1 mutations considered "loss of function" - untreatable
- Progenra claims pharmacological activation of defective PINK1 variants is possible
- Addresses ~10-20% of genetic PD cases linked to PINK1/Parkin mutations

**Investment Thesis**: High-risk/high-reward; if validated, could be transformative for PINK1-PD subpopulation

---

### 3. mPTP (Mitochondrial Permeability Transition Pore) - Mitochondrial Protection

**Mechanism**: mPTP opening leads to mitochondrial swelling, ATP depletion, and cell death. Blocking mPTP preserves mitochondrial integrity.

**Key Players**:
| Company | Asset | Stage | Notes |
|---------|-------|-------|-------|
| NRG Therapeutics | NRG5051 | IND-enabling | Brain-penetrant small molecule |

**Scientific Rationale**:
- Mitochondrial dysfunction is a hallmark of PD
- mPTP inhibition protects against energy loss and cell death
- Applicability beyond PD (ALS, other neurodegeneration)

**Investment Thesis**: Novel target; validated by Parkinson's Foundation Virtual Biotech investment

---

### 4. NAD Augmentation - Metabolic Rescue

**Mechanism**: NAD (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) is essential for cellular energy and mitochondrial function. NAD levels decline in PD brains.

**Key Patents**:
| Assignee | Patent | Technology |
|----------|--------|------------|
| Vestlandets Innovasjonsselskap AS (VIS) | US20240366648A1 | Nicotinamide riboside (NR) for PD |

**Scientific Rationale**:
- Clinical trial data shows oral NR increases brain NAD levels
- Symptomatic improvement observed in pilot studies
- Repurposing approach with established safety profile

**Investment Thesis**: Lower-risk repurposing play; near-term data possible

## II. Emerging Modalities in PD Patents

### 1. PROTAC Degraders (Targeted Protein Degradation)

**Technology**: PROTACs induce degradation of disease proteins rather than just inhibiting them.

**PD Applications**:
| Target | Company | Asset | Stage | Notes |
|--------|---------|-------|-------|-------|
| LRRK2 | Arvinas | ARV-102 | Phase 1 | First PROTAC in PD |
| LRRK2 | Various | XL01126 | Preclinical | Academic |

**Advantages Over Inhibitors**:
- Event-driven (catalytic) vs occupancy-driven
- May achieve deeper pathway suppression
- Potential for improved therapeutic index

**Patent Activity**: Significant patent filings around LRRK2 degraders in 2024-2025

**Investment Thesis**: Arvinas leading; LRRK2 PROTAC data (Oct 2025) showed CSF biomarker effects - validates approach

---

### 2. Antisense Oligonucleotides (ASOs)

**Technology**: ASOs reduce expression of target proteins by degrading mRNA.

**PD Applications**:
| Target | Company | Asset | Stage | Notes |
|--------|---------|-------|-------|-------|
| LRRK2 | SciNeuro | SNP614 | Preclinical | LNA scaffold; MJFF funded |
| LRRK2 | Biogen | BIIB094 | Phase 1 | In development |
| Alpha-synuclein | Academic | Various | Preclinical | Tokyo Med Dental research |

**Advantages**:
- Highly specific target knockdown
- Proven CNS delivery (spinal muscular atrophy precedent)
- Complementary to small molecule inhibitors

**Patent Activity**: Multiple academic patents on alpha-synuclein ASOs (US10815480B2, etc.)

**Investment Thesis**: Strong precedent in SMA; LRRK2 ASOs may complement inhibitor strategies

---

### 3. Engineered AAV Gene Therapy - IV Administration

**Technology**: Engineered AAV capsids that cross blood-brain barrier when administered intravenously, avoiding intracranial surgery.

**Key Patents/Players**:
| Company | Asset | Target | Stage | Patent Activity |
|---------|-------|--------|-------|-----------------|
| Capsida Biotherapeutics | CAP-003 | GBA1 | IND cleared | Multiple capsid patents |
| City Univ Hong Kong | N/A | Various | Academic | US20240110200A1 |

**Advantages**:
- Non-invasive administration
- Potentially broader CNS distribution
- Avoids neurosurgical risks

**Investment Thesis**: If IV gene therapy proves viable, could transform PD treatment landscape

---

### 4. Exosome-Based Delivery

**Technology**: Using exosomes (cell-derived vesicles) to deliver therapeutic cargo to the brain.

**Key Patents**:
| Assignee | Patent | Technology |
|----------|--------|------------|
| Univ South Florida/VA | WO2024186658A1 | Adipose stem cell exosomes with MALAT1 lncRNA |

**Advantages**:
- Natural delivery vehicles
- Cross BBB
- Lower immunogenicity than viral vectors
- Intranasal administration potential

**Investment Thesis**: Early stage; proof-of-concept needed; potential for non-invasive neurotrophic delivery

---

### 5. Nanotechnology-Based Enzyme Replacement

**Technology**: Polymer-enzyme conjugates for enhanced enzyme delivery.

**Key Patents**:
| Assignee | Patent | Technology |
|----------|--------|------------|

## IV. Investment Themes from Patent Analysis

### Theme 1: Mitochondrial Health
- USP30 inhibition (Mission)
- PINK1 activation (Progenra, Mitokinin)
- mPTP blockade (NRG)
- NAD augmentation (VIS)

**Thesis**: Converging evidence that mitochondrial dysfunction is central to PD pathogenesis

### Theme 2: Genetic Subtype Targeting
- GBA-PD: GCase activators, gene therapy (Vanqua, Gain, Capsida, Voyager)
- LRRK2-PD: Inhibitors, degraders, ASOs (Biogen/Denali, Arvinas, SciNeuro)
- PINK1-PD: Activators (Progenra)

**Thesis**: Precision medicine approach; largest near-term opportunity for disease modification

### Theme 3: Beyond Alpha-Synuclein
- Most patent activity now in non-alpha-synuclein targets
- Recognition that alpha-synuclein antibodies have struggled (cinpanemab failed)
- Alternative pathways gaining traction

**Thesis**: Diversification away from alpha-synuclein as primary target

### Theme 4: Non-Invasive Gene/Cell Therapy Delivery
- IV-administered AAV (Capsida)
- Exosome delivery (USF)
- Nanotechnology (VHIR)

**Thesis**: If successful, removes major barrier to gene therapy adoption
