# Seed/Angel Funded Parkinson's Disease Companies

#claude


*Last updated: January 2026*

This document tracks very early-stage (seed/angel) funding rounds for Parkinson's Disease and neurodegeneration-focused companies. These companies are typically 2-3 years away from mainstream coverage.

---

## Confirmed Seed Rounds (2024-2025)

### Endlyz Therapeutics
- **Location:** Belgium/UK
- **Founded:** 2025
- **Funding:** EUR 16M seed round (April 2025)
- **Lead Investors:** SV Health Investors
- **Other Investors:** Michael J. Fox Foundation involvement
- **Focus:** Small molecules modulating lysosomal recycling (ATP13A2/ATP10B pathways) to treat Parkinson's disease and ALS
- **Stage:** Preclinical
- **Thesis:** Aims to slow or halt neurodegeneration via cellular "clean-up" mechanisms
- **Source:** [Labiotech](https://www.labiotech.eu/in-depth/parkinsons-disease-biotechs-pursuit-for-more-treatments/)

### NeuraWorx Medical Technology
- **Location:** USA (University of Wisconsin-Madison spinout)
- **Founded:** ~2024
- **Funding:** Oversubscribed seed round (December 2025)
- **Lead Investors:** Nexus NeuroTech Ventures
- **Other Investors:** Foothill Ventures, Verge HealthTech Fund, Wisconsin Alumni Research Foundation (WARF)
- **Focus:** Bioelectronic solutions for brain health, addressing cerebrovascular and glymphatic dysfunction implicated in neurodegenerative diseases
- **Stage:** Preclinical
- **Source:** [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251217796461/en/NeuraWorx-Closes-Oversubscribed-Seed-Round-Led-by-Nexus-NeuroTech-to-Advance-Neurotechnology-Therapy-for-CNS-Disorders)

### Elemind
- **Location:** USA
- **Founded:** ~2023
- **Funding:** $12M seed (February 2024)
- **Focus:** AI-enhanced neurotech wearable platform using noninvasive brainwave stimulation for health enhancement
- **Stage:** Early clinical validation
- **Source:** [2024 Neurotech Funding Snapshot](https://neurotechnology.substack.com/p/2024-funding-snapshot)

## Small Grants (Under $10M) - Foundation Funding

### Kannalife Sciences
- **Funding:** $1.49M grant (June 2024)
- **Source:** Michael J. Fox Foundation
- **Focus:** Development of KLS-13019 targeting neuroinflammation and mitochondrial dysfunction in Parkinson's disease
- **Source:** [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/06/25/2903784/0/en/Kannalife-Secures-Funding-of-1-49-Million-from-The-Michael-J-Fox-Foundation-for-Parkinson-s-Research.html)

### NysnoBio GT Neurology
- **Funding:** $2.5M award
- **Source:** CIRM (California Institute for Regenerative Medicine)
- **Focus:** Gene therapy delivering healthy Parkin gene to patients with Parkin mutation-caused PD
- **Source:** [CIRM Blog](https://blog.cirm.ca.gov/2025/04/25/new-gene-therapy-aims-to-restore-function-in-early-onset-parkinsons/)

### Stealth BioTherapeutics
- **Funding:** Research grant (amount undisclosed)
- **Source:** Michael J. Fox Foundation
- **Focus:** Evaluating mitochondria-targeted molecule SBT-272 for Parkinson's disease
- **Collaboration:** Duke University School of Medicine (Laurie Sanders, Ph.D.)
- **Source:** [Stealth BT](https://stealthbt.com/stealth-biotherapeutics-awarded-grant-from-the-michael-j-fox-foundation-to-support-development-of-sbt-272-in-parkinsons-disease/)

### SciNeuro Pharmaceuticals

## Series A Companies to Watch (Recently Emerged from Seed)

### Kenai Therapeutics (formerly Ryne Bio)
- **Location:** San Diego, USA
- **Founded:** 2022
- **Funding:** $82M Series A (February 2024)
- **Lead Investors:** Cure Ventures, The Column Group, Alaska Permanent Fund Corp
- **Focus:** Allogeneic iPSC-derived cell therapies for neuron replacement; lead candidate RNDP-001 for Parkinson's disease
- **Stage:** Moving to Phase 1 clinical trial
- **Note:** Received $4M from CIRM at earlier stage
- **Source:** [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2024/02/29/kenai-therapeutics-parkinsons-disease-stem-cell-therapy/)

### Nilo Therapeutics
- **Location:** New York, USA
- **Founded:** 2024
- **Funding:** $101M Series A (October 2025)
- **Lead Investors:** The Column Group, with backing from Gates Frontier, Lux Capital, DCVS Bio, Alexandria Venture Investments
- **Founders:** Charles Zuker (Columbia), Ruslan Medzhitov (Yale), Steve Liberles (Harvard)
- **Focus:** Neuro-immunology therapies targeting neuronal circuits to regulate immune system
- **Note:** While focused on autoimmune diseases, neuro-immune intersection relevant to neurodegeneration
- **Source:** [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/immunology-biotech-nilo-launches-101m-and-mission-harness-neural-circuits)

### Trace Neuroscience
- **Location:** South San Francisco, USA
- **Founded:** 2024
- **Funding:** $101M Series A (November 2024)
- **Lead Investors:** Third Rock Ventures, Atlas Venture, GV, RA Capital Management
- **Founders:** Pietro Fratta (UCL), Aaron Gitler (Stanford), Eric Green (CEO, ex-Maze Therapeutics)
- **Focus:** UNC13A protein restoration for ALS; potentially applicable to other neurodegenerative diseases
- **Source:** [UCLB](https://www.uclb.com/2024/11/12/trace-neuroscience-launches-with-exclusive-license-from-ucl-and-101-million-series-a-funding-to-take-forward-potential-new-therapeutic-for-motor-neurone-disease/)

### Brenig Therapeutics
- **Location:** USA
- **Funding:** $65M Series A
- **Focus:** Neurodegenerative diseases therapies, preclinical in Parkinson's disease
- **Source:** [Labiotech](https://www.labiotech.eu/recent-biotech-fundings/)
