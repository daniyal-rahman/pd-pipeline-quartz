- Watch for: Patent filings, industry collaborations, scientist departures

**Alpha-Synuclein Targeting**
- Labs developing novel immunotherapy approaches

- GP2 (Global Parkinson's Genetics Program) identified new functional effects for non-coding GBA1 risk variant
- Voyager/Neurocrine GBA1 gene therapy collaboration (announced January 2023) may spawn spinouts
- Watch for: Small molecule chaperone developers

**Cell Therapy Labs**
- iPSC dopaminergic neuron programs beyond Aspen and Kenai
- Watch for: Manufacturing process innovations, immune evasion technologies
