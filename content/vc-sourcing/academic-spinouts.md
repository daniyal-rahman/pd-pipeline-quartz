### Endlyz Therapeutics (UK/Belgium)
**University Origin:** Oxford University (academic shareholder), KU Leuven
**Founded:** 2021 (by SV Health's Dementia Discovery Fund)
**Scientific Founders:** Dr. Joanna Wolak (CEO), Prof. Richard Wade-Martins (Oxford), Prof. John Davis (Oxford), Prof. Peter Vangheluwe (KU Leuven)
**Technology:** Small molecule potentiators of lysosomal transporters (ATP13A2/ATP10B)
**Lead Target:** ATP13A2 (PARK9 gene) - lysosomal polyamine transporter

**Funding:**
- Seed (April 2025): EUR 16M ($16M equivalent)
  - Investors: SV Health Dementia Discovery Fund, Oxford Science Enterprises, AbbVie Ventures, Parkinson's UK, CD3 (KU Leuven)
- MJFF: $2.2M (Parkinson's Disease Therapeutics Pipeline Program)

**Rationale:** ATP13A2 mutations cause early-onset PD; enzyme critical for lysosomal/mitochondrial health
**Source:** [Endlyz](https://www.endlyz.com/)

### Aspen Neuroscience (Scripps Research)
**University Origin:** Scripps Research Institute (La Jolla, CA)
**Founded:** 2018
**Scientific Founders:**
- Jeanne Loring, PhD (Scripps Professor Emeritus, Center for Regenerative Medicine founder)
- Andres Bratt-Leal, PhD (Loring lab postdoc)
**Technology:** Autologous iPSC-derived dopaminergic neuron replacement therapy

**Lead Program:** ANPD001
- FDA IND clearance: August 2023
- ASPIRO Phase 1/2a: Cohort 3 initiated September 2025
- Approach: Patient's own skin cells -> iPSCs -> dopaminergic neurons

**Funding:**
- Series C (November 2025): $115M
- Series B: $147.5M
- Total: $272M

**Differentiation:** Autologous (patient-specific) approach avoids immune rejection
**Source:** [Aspen Neuroscience](https://aspenneuroscience.com/)
