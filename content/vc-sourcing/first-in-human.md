## Overview

## Cell Therapies

### Aspen Neuroscience - ANPD001 (ASPIRO Trial)

**First Dosing:** April 17, 2024 (first patient)

**Program:**
- Autologous iPSC-derived dopaminergic neuron replacement therapy
- First multi-patient, multi-center clinical trial of autologous cell therapy for PD
- FDA Fast Track designation

**Conference Presentations:**
- MDS 2024 (Philadelphia): Surgical safety data from Cohort 1
- IAPRD 2025: 6-month data from first three patients

**Key Data (May 2025):**
- Strong safety and tolerability
- Clinician- and patient-reported improvements
- No immunosuppression required (autologous approach)
- Cohort 3 initiated September 2025 with commercial formulation

**Trial Design:**
- Phase 1/2a open-label
- Levodopa-responsive patients 50-70 years old
- Primary endpoints at 12 months
- Long-term follow-up to 15 years

**Funding:** $115M Series C (November 2025)

**Investment Signal:**
- FIH completed, dose escalation ongoing
- Only autologous approach in late development
- No immunosuppression differentiator
- Commercial formulation milestone achieved

### BlueRock Therapeutics (Bayer) - Bemdaneprocel (exPDite Trials)

**First Dosing:** 2022 (Phase 1 exPDite)

**Program:**
- Allogeneic hESC-derived dopaminergic progenitor cell therapy
- First and most clinically advanced allogeneic cell therapy for PD

**Conference Presentations:**
- ISSCR 2024 (Hamburg): Phase 1 emerging data
- Nature publication (April 2025): 18-month data

**Key Data (18-month results):**
- Good tolerability at 24 months post-surgery
- No serious adverse events related to drug product
- Transplanted cells survived and engrafted
- Continued dopaminergic activity signal
- Both low-dose and high-dose showed motor improvement

**Current Status:**
- Phase 3 exPDite-2 initiated (first patient September 2025)
- SAKIGAKE designation in Japan (December 2025)

**Investment Signal:**
- Most advanced cell therapy program
- Bayer backing provides resources
- Phase 3 initiated - pivotal stage
- Multiple regulatory designations

## Gene Therapies

### Capsida Therapeutics - CAP-003

**First-in-Human Expected:** H1 2025

**Program:**
- IV-delivered gene therapy for GBA-associated PD
- Novel capsid technology for enhanced brain penetration

**Conference Presentations:**
- ASGCT 2025: Preclinical data

**Key Preclinical Data:**
- Brainwide RNA expression >200-fold vs IV AAV9
- Enables systemic delivery (vs intracranial)

**Investment Signal:**
- Differentiated delivery approach (IV vs surgical)
- Strong preclinical signal
- Clinical entry imminent
- GBA1 genetic stratification opportunity

### AAV2-GAD (STN Gene Therapy)

**Phase 1/2 Completed:** September 2024

**Program:**
- AAV-delivered glutamic acid decarboxylase to subthalamic nucleus
- Double-blind, placebo-controlled

**Conference Presentations:**
- ASGCT 2024: Results announcement

**Key Data:**
- 14 PD patients (sham, low-dose, high-dose groups)
- Safe and well tolerated
- Significant motor improvement in high-dose group

**Investment Signal:**
- Completed Phase 1/2 with efficacy signal
- Controlled study design validates findings
- Dose-response relationship established

## Small Molecules - NLRP3 Inhibitors

### NodThera - NT-0796

**First PD Dosing:** 2024

**Program:**
- Brain-penetrant NLRP3 inflammasome inhibitor

**Conference Presentations:**
- AD/PD 2024 (Lisbon): Phase 1b results

**Key Data:**
- Open-label study in elderly healthy volunteers and PD patients
- 300mg total daily dose
- Reduced neuroinflammatory biomarkers to healthy control levels
- 28-day sustained dosing demonstrated CNS activity
- Safe and well tolerated

**Investment Signal:**
- First demonstration of CNS NLRP3 inhibition in PD
- Biomarker evidence of target engagement
- Path to longer-term studies established

---

### Ventyx Biosciences - VTX3232

**First PD Dosing:** 2025

**Program:**
- Oral NLRP3 inhibitor

**Conference Presentations:**
- MDS 2025 (Honolulu): Phase 2a results

**Key Data:**
- 40mg oral daily, 28-day treatment
- Safe in early-stage PD patients
- Improved motor AND nonmotor symptoms
- CSF drug levels >3-fold over IC90
- Significant NLRP3 biomarker reduction (CSF and plasma)

**Investment Signal:**
- Clinical efficacy signals on motor/nonmotor
- Robust target engagement proven
- Ready for larger/longer studies

## Small Molecules - GCase Modulators

### Gain Therapeutics - GT-02287

**Phase 1 Initiated:** 2023
**Phase 1b (PD patients):** December 2024

**Program:**
- Allosteric modulator restoring GCase function
- Oral, brain-penetrant

**Conference Presentations:**
- WORLDSymposium 2024: Preclinical motor recovery data
- SfN 2025: Preclinical data
- Multiple 2024-2025 press releases

**Key Data:**
- Phase 1 (healthy volunteers): Safe, CSF penetration confirmed, peripheral target engagement
- Phase 1b (21 PD patients): First-ever reduction of GluSph substrate in PD patient CSF
- 90-day dosing well tolerated
- 79% elected to continue into 9-month extension

**Investment Signal:**
- First biomarker proof in PD patients
- High extension enrollment signals patient/investigator confidence
- MJFF funding support validates science
- Extension data expected September 2026

## Alpha-Synuclein PET Imaging

### Merck - MK-7337

**First-in-Human:** 2025

**Program:**
- Radiotracer for PET imaging of alpha-synuclein aggregates

**Conference Presentations:**
- AD/PD 2025 (Vienna): FIH results

**Key Data:**
- 8 patients (6 men, 2 women, ages 63-84)
- All had 3+ years idiopathic PD diagnosis
- Positive alpha-synuclein CSF test required for enrollment
- [11C]MK-7337 accumulated in substantia nigra (6/8 patients)
- Signal in brainstem (7/8 patients)
- Binds toxic fibrils, not monomers

**Investment Signal:**
- First tracer to show signal in idiopathic PD
- Merck resources behind development
- Transformative diagnostic potential
- Enables therapeutic trial patient selection
