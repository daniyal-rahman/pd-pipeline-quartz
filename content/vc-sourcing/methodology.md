## CRITICAL CAVEAT

> **Patent Filing Does NOT Equal Active Development**
>
> Patents are filed to protect intellectual property and may represent:
> - Early-stage research that will never advance
> - Abandoned programs
> - Defensive filings to block competitors
> - Academic inventions without commercial intent
> - Failed programs that were discontinued
>
> **Use patent data to IDENTIFY potential players, then VERIFY development status through:**
> - ClinicalTrials.gov registrations
> - SEC filings (public companies)
> - Company press releases and websites
> - Conference presentations (MDS, AAN, AD/PD)
> - MJFF and Parkinson's UK funding announcements
> - PitchBook/Crunchbase (private company funding)

## Limitations of This Research

### 1. Google Patents Search Limitations
- Site-specific operators (site:, filed:, assignee:) did not work consistently in web searches
- Recent patent filings may not yet be indexed
- Publication lag: PCT applications publish 18 months after priority date
- Non-English patents (Chinese, Japanese, Korean) may be underrepresented

### 2. Information Gaps
- Private company patent portfolios not fully disclosed
- Provisional applications not published until conversion
- Some patent assignments may not be updated in databases
- Method-only patents may not appear in therapeutic searches

### 3. Development Status Uncertainty
- Cannot determine if patented technologies are actively developed
- Company announcements may not reflect current status
- Failed programs may still have active patents

### 4. Geographic Coverage
- Focus was primarily on USPTO, EPO, and WIPO filings
- May have missed important filings in China (CNIPA), Japan (JPO), Korea (KIPO)

## Verification Recommendations

### For Each Identified Company/Patent:

1. **ClinicalTrials.gov Check**
   - Search company name + "Parkinson"
   - Search asset name
   - Check study status (recruiting, active, completed, terminated)

2. **SEC Filings (Public Companies)**
   - 10-K annual reports (pipeline section)
   - 10-Q quarterly reports
   - 8-K material events
   - S-1/S-4 (IPO/merger filings)

3. **Company Website**
   - Pipeline page
   - Press releases
   - Investor presentations
   - Scientific publications

4. **Conference Abstracts**
   - MDS (Movement Disorder Society) - September annually
   - AAN (American Academy of Neurology) - April annually
   - AD/PD - March annually
   - MJFF Scientific Symposium

5. **Funding Databases**
   - PitchBook
   - Crunchbase
   - CB Insights
   - BioCentury

6. **Patent Status Check**
   - USPTO PAIR (for US applications)
   - Espacenet (for European)
   - PatSnap or Synapse for comprehensive analysis
   - Check maintenance fee status (abandoned patents)
