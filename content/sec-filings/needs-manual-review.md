## Purpose

This document flags SEC filings and disclosures that:
1. Are too dense for automated extraction
2. Contain vague or incomplete pipeline information
3. Require human judgment to interpret
4. May contain buried PD-relevant information

## 1. Complex Partnership Structures Needing Review

### Biogen/Denali LRRK2 Collaboration

**Why Manual Review Needed:**
- Complex cost-sharing arrangements (60/40, 50/50 varying by geography)
- Multiple concurrent trials with different sponsors
- Third-party funding agreements adding complexity
- Need to trace milestone payments and obligations

**SEC Filing to Review:**
- Biogen 10-K FY2024: Collaboration agreements section
- Denali 10-K FY2024: Revenue recognition notes
- Exhibit 10.x: LRRK2 Collaboration Agreement amendments

**Questions to Answer:**
1. What are the trigger events for milestone payments?
2. Who controls development decisions?
3. What happens if trials fail?

## Recommended Review Process

### For Each Flagged Filing:

1. **Download full 10-K PDF** from SEC EDGAR
2. **Search for keywords:** "Parkinson," "LRRK2," "synuclein," "GBA1," "dopamin"
3. **Review these sections:**
   - Business (pages 1-30 typically)
   - Risk Factors (extensive, but valuable)
   - Management Discussion & Analysis
   - Notes to Financial Statements (collaborations, contingencies)
4. **Cross-reference** with clinical trial registries
5. **Note any contradictions** between press releases and 10-K language
