## 1. Alpha-Synuclein Targeting Programs

### Prothena Corporation (NASDAQ: PRTA)

**SEC Filing Reference:** Form 10-K, Form 10-Q filings available on EDGAR

**Lead Asset: Prasinezumab (PRX002/RG7935)**
- **Mechanism:** Monoclonal antibody targeting aggregated alpha-synuclein
- **Partner:** Roche/Genentech (December 2013 license agreement)
- **Stage:** Phase 2b completed; advancing to Phase 3
- **Clinical Trials:**
  - PASADENA (Phase 2): 4-year open-label extension data published in Nature Medicine (October 2024)
  - PADOVA (Phase 2b): 586 patients, 18+ months treatment; primary endpoint (HR=0.84, p=0.0657) missed statistical significance but showed trends
- **Key Data:**
  - Subgroup on levodopa showed HR=0.79 [0.63-0.99]
  - Safety database: 900+ patients treated, 750+ remain in open-label extension
  - Roche announced Phase 3 advancement (June 2025)
- **Milestones Earned:** $135M of potential $755M from Roche

### Vaxxinity, Inc. (NASDAQ: VAXX)

**SEC Filing Reference:** S-1 (2021), 10-Q filings

**Lead Asset: UB-312**
- **Mechanism:** Synthetic peptide vaccine targeting C-terminus of alpha-synuclein
- **Stage:** Phase 1 completed; Phase 2 status
- **Clinical Data:**
  - 92% (12/13) patients developed anti-aSyn antibodies
  - 20% decrease in CSF aggregated alpha-synuclein vs 3% increase in placebo (p<0.05)
  - Preferential binding to aggregated (not monomeric) alpha-synuclein
- **Regulatory:** EMA orphan designation for MSA
- **Cash Position (Dec 2023):** $30.4M in liquid assets

### AC Immune SA (NASDAQ: ACIU)

**SEC Filing Reference:** Form 20-F (Swiss company filing)
**2024 Annual Report:** Filed March 2025 for FY2024

**Lead Asset: ACI-7104.056**
- **Mechanism:** Active immunotherapy (vaccine) targeting pathological alpha-synuclein
- **Stage:** Phase 2 (VacSYn trial)
- **Clinical Data:**
  - 100% responder rate against target antigen
  - Antibody levels 16-fold higher than placebo after 3 immunizations
  - Well tolerated; injection site reactions (49%), headaches (18%)
- **Partnerships:** $100M upfront from Takeda (2024); CHF 24.6M milestone from Janssen

## 2. LRRK2 Inhibitor Programs

### Denali Therapeutics (NASDAQ: DNLI) / Biogen (NASDAQ: BIIB)

**SEC Filing Reference:**
- Denali: FY2024 Annual Report (April 2025), 10-K forms
- Biogen: Form 10-K FY2024 (filed 2025)

**Lead Asset: BIIB122/DNL151**
- **Mechanism:** Small molecule LRRK2 kinase inhibitor (oral, brain-penetrant)
- **Stage:** Phase 2b/3
- **Clinical Trials:**
  - LUMA Study (Phase 2b): ~640 participants with early-stage PD; enrollment completed 2025, readout expected 2026
  - LIGHTHOUSE Study (Phase 3): ~400 participants with LRRK2-PD
  - BEACON Study (Phase 2a): Initiated December 2024; ~50 participants with LRRK2 mutations
- **Partnership Structure:**
  - Global development: 60% Biogen / 40% Denali
  - US commercialization: 50%/50%
  - China commercialization: 60% Biogen / 40% Denali
- **Funding:** $75M third-party funding agreement (January 2024); $12.5M received

**Scientific Rationale from SEC Filings:**
> "LRRK2 is the most common genetic risk factor for Parkinson's disease, and is involved in maintaining a healthy cellular environment by regulating lysosomal function. Increased levels of LRRK2 lead to lysosomal dysfunction, which contributes to neurodegeneration and the formation of Lewy bodies."

## 3. GBA1/GCase Targeting Programs

### Gain Therapeutics (NASDAQ: GANX)

**SEC Filing Reference:** Form 10-K FY2024, press releases filed as exhibits

**Lead Asset: GT-02287**
- **Mechanism:** Allosteric enzyme modulator restoring GCase function
- **Stage:** Phase 1b (ongoing)
- **Target Population:** PD with or without GBA1 mutation
- **Clinical Data (Phase 1):**
  - Safe and well tolerated at all dose levels
  - CNS penetration confirmed (present in CSF)
  - Peripheral target engagement demonstrated
- **Timeline:** Phase 1b first analysis expected Q2 2025
- **Partnerships:** Michael J. Fox Foundation support

### Prevail Therapeutics (acquired by Eli Lilly, 2021)

**SEC Filing Reference:** Form 10-K (2019), S-1 IPO prospectus, merger filings

**Lead Asset: PR001**
- **Mechanism:** AAV-based gene therapy delivering GBA1 gene
- **Target:** PD with GBA1 mutation (PD-GBA) and neuronopathic Gaucher disease
- **Route:** Single intra-cisterna magna (ICM) injection
- **Stage:** Phase 1/2 (PROPEL trial)
- **Regulatory:** FDA Fast Track designation
- **Market Size:** PD-GBA affects 7-10% of all PD patients; ~90,000 in US
- **Acquisition:** Eli Lilly acquired for $22.50/share + $4.00 CVR (up to $1.04B total)
- **License:** REGENXBIO AAV9 license agreement

### Sanofi/Genzyme - Venglustat (DISCONTINUED)

**SEC Filing Reference:** Historical 10-K disclosures

**Asset: Venglustat (GZ/SAR402671)**
- **Mechanism:** Glucosylceramide synthase inhibitor
- **Stage:** Discontinued for PD (February 2021)
- **MOVES-PD Trial Results:**
  - Failed primary endpoint
  - Venglustat group showed GREATER deterioration vs placebo
  - More psychiatric and GI adverse events
  - Despite 75% reduction in glucosylceramide (CSF and plasma)
- **Status:** Development continues for GD type 3 and late-onset GM2

## 4. Multi-Target / Novel Mechanism Programs

### Annovis Bio (NYSE: ANVS)

**SEC Filing Reference:** Form 10-K FY2024 (filed March 2025)
**SEC Link:** https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1477845/000155837025003482/anvs-20241231x10k.htm

**Lead Asset: Buntanetap**
- **Mechanism:** Inhibits synthesis of multiple neurotoxic proteins (APP/Abeta, tau, alpha-synuclein)
- **Stage:** Phase 3 completed (PD); FDA clearance for pivotal Phase 3
- **Clinical Data (Phase 3 PD):**
  - 471 patients completed study
  - Clinically meaningful improvements across MDS-UPDRS Parts II, III, IV
  - Halted cognitive decline (MMSE stable vs worsening on placebo)
  - Particularly effective in PIGD subtype
- **Regulatory:**
  - FDA Type C meeting scheduled January 2026 for PD dementia pathway
  - Open-label extension starting January 2026
- **Formulation:** FDA approved new crystal form (July 2024)

### Inhibikase Therapeutics (NASDAQ: IKT)

**SEC Filing Reference:** Form 10-K, 424B4 IPO prospectus

**Lead Asset: IkT-148009**
- **Mechanism:** c-Abl tyrosine kinase inhibitor (oral, brain-penetrant)
- **Stage:** Phase 2 (Study 201)
- **Target:** Treatment of PD in brain and GI tract
- **Clinical Design:**
  - 120 patients with untreated PD (Hoehn & Yahr <3.0)
  - Doses: 50, 100, 200 mg daily vs placebo
  - 12-week double-blind treatment
- **Scientific Rationale:**
  > "Internalization of misfolded alpha-synuclein within affected neurons activates c-Abl, which then modifies the protein to create a toxic form of alpha-synuclein triggering the neurodegenerative disease process."
- **Preclinical Data:** Clears pathologic alpha-synuclein aggregates; blocks neurodegeneration
- **Regulatory:** FDA lifted clinical hold for MSA (March 2023)

### BioVie (NYSE: BIVI)

**SEC Filing Reference:** Form 10-K, 8-K filings

**Lead Asset: Bezisterim (NE3107)**
- **Mechanism:** ERK/NFkB inflammatory signaling inhibitor; reduces brain insulin resistance
- **Stage:** Phase 2 (SUNRISE-PD trial)
- **Clinical Design:**
  - 60 patients, newly diagnosed PD
  - 20 mg twice daily vs placebo
  - 12-week double-blind, 20-week total study
- **Phase 2a Results:**
  - Significant improvement in sleep/fatigue domain (-2.4 vs +1.0 placebo, p=0.0159)
  - Correlation with motor improvements (r=0.51, p=0.0259)
- **Funding:** $15M+ raised; trial fully funded
- **Timeline:** Start early 2025; topline results late 2025

## 6. GLP-1 Receptor Agonist Programs

### Neuraly (Private - D&D Pharmatech subsidiary)

**Note:** No SEC filings (private company)

**Lead Asset: NLY01**
- **Mechanism:** Long-acting GLP-1R agonist (exendin-4 analogue); brain-penetrant
- **Stage:** Phase 2 completed
- **Phase 2 Results (Published December 2023):**
  - 255 participants randomized
  - 36-week treatment
  - Primary endpoint NOT MET: No difference vs placebo on MDS-UPDRS II+III
  - Subgroup finding: Slowing of motor decline in young (<60) treatment-naive patients (~1/3 of subjects)
- **Current Status:** Developing Phase 2B for Alzheimer's disease

## Key Observations

1. **Alpha-synuclein remains primary target** - Multiple modalities (antibodies, vaccines) with prasinezumab most advanced
2. **LRRK2 inhibition gaining traction** - Biogen/Denali collaboration well-funded with multiple Phase 2/3 trials
3. **GBA1/GCase approaches proliferating** - Gene therapy, small molecule activators, and modulators all in development
4. **Symptomatic therapies still advancing** - Tavapadon represents next-gen dopamine targeting
5. **Failed programs inform field** - Sanofi's venglustat failure shows substrate reduction may not be viable for PD
