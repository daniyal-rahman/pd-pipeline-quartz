## 1. Emerging Companies with Active PD Programs

### Gain Therapeutics (NASDAQ: GANX)

**Why Notable:** Small-cap company with novel GCase modulation approach distinct from gene therapy or substrate reduction

**Asset: GT-02287**
- **Mechanism:** STAR (Site Targeted Allosteric Regulation) platform-derived GCase activator
- **Differentiation:** Works on both wild-type and mutant GCase; applicable to PD with OR without GBA1 mutations
- **Stage:** Phase 1b (2025)
- **Funding:** Michael J. Fox Foundation support
- **SEC Disclosure:** 10-K references ongoing collaboration and Phase 1b enrollment

**Key Insight from SEC Filings:**
> GT-02287 has potential beyond GBA1-PD, including Gaucher's disease, dementia with Lewy bodies, and Alzheimer's disease.

---

### Inhibikase Therapeutics (NASDAQ: IKT)

**Why Notable:** Unique c-Abl kinase approach linking alpha-synuclein phosphorylation to disease mechanism

**Asset: IkT-148009**
- **Mechanism:** c-Abl tyrosine kinase inhibitor
- **Novel Insight:** c-Abl modifies alpha-synuclein into toxic form; inhibition may restore neurons
- **Dual Target:** Brain AND gastrointestinal tract pathology
- **Stage:** Phase 2 (Study 201)

**SEC Filing Highlight (S-1 IPO Prospectus):**
> "In validated preclinical animal models, IkT-148009 was able to drive functional recovery in the brain and gastrointestinal tract, clear pathologic alpha-synuclein aggregates, and block neurodegeneration and neuroinflammation."

---

### BioVie (NYSE: BIVI)

**Why Notable:** Repurposed/repositioned anti-inflammatory mechanism for early PD

**Asset: Bezisterim (NE3107)**
- **Mechanism:** Selective inhibitor of inflammation-driven ERK/NFkB signaling
- **Unique Feature:** Preserves homeostatic signaling (insulin, neuron growth) while blocking pathological inflammation
- **Target Population:** Newly diagnosed, treatment-naive PD
- **Data Signal:** Sleep/fatigue improvement correlating with motor improvement

**SEC/Press Release Note:**
The SUNRISE-PD trial uses a hybrid decentralized design, potentially enabling faster enrollment and broader geographic reach.

## 3. Novel Therapeutic Modalities

### BlueRock Therapeutics (Bayer subsidiary)

**Why Notable:** Most advanced cell therapy for PD

**Asset: Bemdaneprocel**
- **Mechanism:** iPSC-derived dopaminergic neuron transplantation
- **Stage:** Phase 3 initiated (January 2025)
- **Regulatory Path:** Likely to seek regenerative medicine advanced therapy (RMAT) designation

**SEC Access:** Via Bayer AG filings (German company)

---

### Arvinas

**Why Notable:** First PROTAC degrader approach for PD

**Asset: ARV-102**
- **Mechanism:** Proteolysis-targeting chimera (PROTAC) for LRRK2 degradation
- **Differentiation:** Degrades entire LRRK2 protein rather than just inhibiting kinase activity
- **Stage:** Early clinical development

**Potential Advantage:** May provide more complete LRRK2 modulation than small molecule inhibitors

## 5. Discontinued Programs Worth Noting

### Sanofi/Genzyme - Venglustat

**Why Important:** Failure informs field about substrate reduction viability

**Key Lessons from SEC-disclosed trial results:**
1. Target engagement achieved (75% glucosylceramide reduction) but no clinical benefit
2. Treated patients actually deteriorated faster than placebo
3. Substrate reduction may not be viable monotherapy for PD

**Implication:** GCase activation/restoration approaches (Gain, BIAL, Prevail) may be more promising than substrate reduction.

## 7. Unexpected Findings

### Boston Scientific (NYSE: BSX)

**SEC Filing:** 10-K FY2024

**Why Included:** Medical device company with PD relevance

**Product: Vercise Genus DBS System**
- Deep brain stimulation for PD, tremor, dystonia
- Stimview XT visualization software (Brainlab collaboration)
- Market cap: ~$113 billion

**Insight:** Device therapies represent significant PD market but often overlooked in drug pipeline discussions.
