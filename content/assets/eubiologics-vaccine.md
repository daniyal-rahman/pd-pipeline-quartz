# EuBiologics PD Vaccine

## Summary

EuBiologics (biotech, KOSDAQ: 206650) formally launched its Parkinson's disease vaccine program in January 2026 through EuPOP Life Sciences, a joint venture with US-based POP Biotechnologies (62.5% EuBiologics ownership). The program combines POP Bio's SNAP nanoliposome antigen display platform with EuBiologics' proprietary EcML adjuvant to target pathological alpha-synuclein -- the same technology stack validated through Phase 3 for the companies' COVID-19 vaccine (EuCorVac-19). The program is preclinical with no drug name, no IND filing, and no clinical data, making this the earliest-stage alpha-synuclein vaccine in the pipeline alongside more advanced competitors [[aci-7104|ACI-7104.056]] (Phase 2) and [[ub-312|UB-312]] (Phase 1b). If the parallel Alzheimer's vaccine Padivax (IND filing planned April 2026) demonstrates the SNAP/EcML platform can generate therapeutically meaningful anti-neurodegeneration immune responses, EuBiologics' PD vaccine gains credibility. If Padivax fails or the IND is rejected, the platform thesis for CNS vaccines weakens substantially and this PD program likely stalls.

## Notes

### Science
- Active immunotherapy vaccine designed to induce the patient's own polyclonal antibody response against pathological alpha-synuclein aggregates, targeting disease modification rather than symptomatic relief
- Built on two complementary platform technologies: **SNAP** (Spontaneous Nanoliposome Antigen Particleization) from POP Biotechnologies, which uses cobalt-porphyrin-phospholipid (CoPoP) liposomes to stably display His-tagged peptide antigens on nanoparticle surfaces, dramatically enhancing immunogenicity; and **EcML** from EuBiologics, a genetically engineered monophosphoryl lipid A (MLA) TLR4 agonist adjuvant derived from E. coli, transferred from the Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST) in 2017
- The SNAP+EcML combination was validated in EuCorVac-19 (COVID-19 vaccine), which demonstrated superior antibody immunogenicity vs. a WHO Emergency Use Authorized adenoviral vector vaccine and met the primary endpoint of a Phase 3 trial -- proving the platform can generate strong humoral immune responses in humans
- The specific alpha-synuclein peptide epitope(s) for the PD vaccine have not been publicly disclosed. A 2023 preclinical study from Gyeongsang National University (Park et al., Vaccines) demonstrated that a 10-amino-acid alpha-synuclein peptide epitope (VAEKTKEQVT) conjugated to carrier proteins reversed alpha-synuclein pathology and improved motor function in PFF-injected mice -- this represents the type of approach EuBiologics may pursue, though no direct link to EuBiologics has been confirmed
- Key scientific challenge shared with all active alpha-synuclein vaccines: generating antibodies that selectively bind pathological aggregates (oligomeric/fibrillar) over physiological monomeric alpha-synuclein, and achieving sufficient CNS penetration (~0.1% of serum levels for standard antibodies) for meaningful target engagement
- Differentiation from [[aci-7104|ACI-7104.056]] (synthetic mimotope + KLH + alum adjuvant) and [[ub-312|UB-312]] (native epitope + UBITh T-helper peptide): EuBiologics' SNAP nanoliposome display may offer enhanced antigen presentation and adjuvanting via the EcML TLR4 agonist, potentially generating stronger and more durable antibody titers -- though this is theoretical until PD-specific immunogenicity data exist

### Clinical

No clinical trials initiated. The program was formally announced at the JP Morgan Healthcare Conference on January 14, 2026. EuBiologics has stated it plans to lead Phase 1 clinical trials directly, then transition subsequent phases to EuPOP Life Sciences for global expansion in the US and Europe.

**Padivax (Alzheimer's vaccine, related platform)** — IND filing planned April 2026
- Four-antigen peptide vaccine targeting both amyloid-beta and tau proteins
- Uses the same SNAP/EcML platform as the planned PD vaccine
- Preclinical data reportedly showed BBB permeability and target binding
- IND originally scheduled for late 2025, delayed to add primate toxicology studies
- Padivax IND outcome will serve as the first clinical validation gate for the platform in neurodegeneration

### Financial
- **EuBiologics (KOSDAQ: 206650):** Market cap ~470B KRW (~$340M USD); 2024 revenue 96B KRW (~$70M USD) with 38.4% growth, primarily from oral cholera vaccine supply to UNICEF/global public health market
- **Cholera vaccine franchise:** World's largest supplier of oral cholera vaccine; current capacity 33M doses/year, expanding to 90M doses/year by 2026 via Chuncheon plant expansion. This generates the cash flow funding the CNS pivot
- **JV economics:** EuBiologics holds 62.5% of EuPOP Life Sciences; invested $3M for 25% stake in POP Biotechnologies (2020) and $1.25M for JV formation. Total committed capital to date is modest (~$4.25M), reflecting early-stage risk management
- **POP Bio NIH grant:** POP Biotechnologies received a $2.46M NIH grant (October 2025) for Alzheimer's mosaic active immunotherapy development -- validates the platform in the eyes of US federal funders, though grant was for Alzheimer's not PD
- **Strategic optionality:** CEO Baik Yeong-ok has indicated willingness to license-out or co-develop rather than pursue full independent development, suggesting EuBiologics views the PD vaccine as a platform demonstration asset rather than a must-win proprietary franchise
- **No PD-specific R&D spend disclosed;** the preclinical program is likely embedded within EuPOP Life Sciences operating costs

- EuBiologics enters a competitive alpha-synuclein active vaccine field well behind [[aci-7104|ACI-7104.056]] (AC Immune, Phase 2 with 100% immunogenicity and early biomarker signals) and [[ub-312|UB-312]] (Vaxxinity, Phase 1b with published Nature Medicine target engagement data)
- The SNAP/EcML platform is the key differentiator -- if it generates stronger or more durable antibody responses than KLH-conjugate ([[aci-7104|ACI-7104.056]]) or UBITh-conjugate ([[ub-312|UB-312]]) approaches, there could be a meaningful immunogenicity advantage. But this is entirely unproven for alpha-synuclein
- If [[prasinezumab]] succeeds in PARAISO Phase 3, it validates extracellular alpha-synuclein as a target and creates strong pull-through for active vaccines as the scalable, low-COGS follow-on -- EuBiologics would benefit from this but would still be years behind [[aci-7104|ACI-7104.056]]
- If both [[aci-7104|ACI-7104.056]] and [[ub-312|UB-312]] fail in mid-stage trials, EuBiologics' PD vaccine likely gets deprioritized regardless of platform quality, as the active vaccine thesis for alpha-synuclein would be severely damaged
- EuBiologics' strongest strategic advantage is its manufacturing infrastructure and global vaccine distribution network (cholera vaccine supply to 30+ countries via UNICEF), which could enable rapid scale-up and emerging market access if the science works

## Analysis

EuBiologics' PD vaccine is a preclinical-stage program with no disclosed epitope, no animal efficacy data, no IND, and no clinical timeline beyond vague statements about leading Phase 1. The program's entire value proposition rests on two things: the SNAP/EcML platform's clinical track record in infectious disease (EuCorVac-19 Phase 3 success), and the strategic optionality of being a vaccine manufacturer with global distribution capability.

**Analytical estimate -- Probability of reaching Phase 1 initiation by 2028: 50-60%.** This is our assessment, not from a published source. The reasoning: the Padivax Alzheimer's IND filing (planned April 2026) will be the key gating event -- if accepted, it validates the platform for neurodegenerative targets and provides a template for PD IND preparation (+20%); EuBiologics has stable cash flow from cholera vaccine business to fund preclinical work (+10%); the JV structure with POP Bio provides US regulatory expertise (+5%); however, no disclosed PD-specific preclinical data suggest the program may still be in early target selection/epitope design (-15%); the company's core competency is infectious disease vaccines, not neurodegeneration (-10%).

**Analytical estimate -- Conditional probability of eventual approval (if Phase 1 initiated): 5-10%.** This is our assessment, not from a published source. The reasoning: base rate for preclinical neuro vaccines is extremely low (~2-3%); SNAP/EcML platform validation in infectious disease provides modest uplift (+3%); no alpha-synuclein-specific immunogenicity or selectivity data exist to assess; the field leaders ([[aci-7104|ACI-7104.056]], [[ub-312|UB-312]]) will generate years of clinical data before EuBiologics reaches Phase 2, creating a "fast follower" dynamic where success or failure of predecessors will heavily influence this program's trajectory.

**Signal analysis:** The January 2026 JP Morgan Healthcare Conference announcement is strategically timed -- EuBiologics is signaling its CNS ambitions to potential partners and investors while the alpha-synuclein vaccine space is generating positive headlines from [[aci-7104|ACI-7104.056]]'s VacSYn data. The MOU structure (not a binding agreement) and modest capital commitment ($4.25M total) indicate this is an option-like bet rather than a full commitment. CEO Baik's openness to license-out suggests EuBiologics may be positioning the PD vaccine as a partnering asset -- if Padivax demonstrates the platform in Alzheimer's, the PD vaccine becomes a logical extension that a larger pharma partner could advance. The most likely path to clinical development is not independent advancement but rather a licensing deal to a company with neuroscience clinical expertise, triggered by Padivax clinical data.
