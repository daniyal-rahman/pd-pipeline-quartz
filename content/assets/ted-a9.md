# TED-A9

## Summary

TED-A9 (A9-DPC) is an hESC-derived dopaminergic progenitor cell therapy from S.BIOMEDICS (biotech, KOSDAQ: 304360) that published the first peer-reviewed Phase 1/2a results in *Cell* (Oct 2025), showing clean safety, dose-dependent motor improvement (12.7-point and 15.5-point MDS-UPDRS Part III off-medication reductions in low- and high-dose groups), and PET-confirmed dopaminergic engraftment in the posterior putamen at 12 months across 12 patients. This places TED-A9 alongside [[raguneprocel]] (iPSC-derived, NDA filed in Japan) and bemdaneprocel (ESC-derived, Phase 3) as one of three cell therapies with published human engraftment data in PD. If 24-month data holds and a larger trial replicates the dose-response pattern, S.BIOMEDICS becomes a credible cell therapy competitor from Korea with a differentiated small-molecule-only manufacturing process. If longer follow-up reveals graft overgrowth, waning efficacy, or the open-label placebo effect accounts for the signal, the program stalls in a crowded field where bemdaneprocel's Phase 3 trial will generate definitive data first.

## Notes

### Science
- A9-DPC consists of high-purity ventral midbrain dopaminergic progenitor cells derived from a clinical-grade hESC line, differentiated using exclusively small molecules (no biological factors such as recombinant proteins or growth factors) -- a manufacturing distinction that S.BIOMEDICS claims improves reproducibility and purity
- Cells are transplanted via stereotactic surgery into three segments of the putamen (anterior, middle, posterior) with three tracks per putamen, bilaterally -- targeting the region of greatest dopaminergic denervation
- The biological rationale is direct: PD motor symptoms result from loss of A9-type dopaminergic neurons in the substantia nigra that project to the dorsal putamen. Transplanting committed DA progenitors into the putamen bypasses the need for long-distance axonal regrowth -- the graft produces dopamine locally
- Key distinction vs. [[raguneprocel]]: TED-A9 uses hESCs (embryonic), while raguneprocel uses iPSCs (reprogrammed adult cells). ESC-derived cells avoid reprogramming-associated epigenetic concerns but raise ethical sourcing questions and require immunosuppression (allogeneic graft)
- Key distinction vs. bemdaneprocel (BlueRock/Bayer): both are hESC-derived DA progenitors, but TED-A9 uses a small-molecule-only differentiation protocol while bemdaneprocel uses a more conventional growth factor-based approach. Whether this translates to clinical differences in purity or engraftment is unknown
- Open questions: (1) What is the immunosuppression protocol and can it be tapered without graft rejection? (2) Will engrafted cells continue to mature and produce dopamine beyond 12 months, or will efficacy plateau? (3) Does the high-dose group's greater PET signal translate to durable clinical superiority?
- Correlation between increased DAT signal in the posterior dorsal putamen and MDS-UPDRS Part III improvement was statistically significant -- the strongest mechanistic evidence that motor benefit is graft-derived rather than placebo

### Clinical

**Phase 1/2a (S.BIOMEDICS-A9-001)** | NCT05887466 | N=12 | Moderate-to-severe PD, >5 years, age 50-75
- **Primary endpoint:** Safety and tolerability at 12 months → No dose-limiting toxicities or graft-related adverse events. No tumorigenesis, cell overgrowth, ectopic migration, or immune-mediated inflammation. One asymptomatic mild hemorrhage at a non-transplant site.
- **MDS-UPDRS Part III (off-medication):** Low-dose (3.15M cells, n=6): mean decrease of 12.7 points (~22.7% improvement). High-dose (6.30M cells, n=6): mean decrease of 15.5 points (~25.3% improvement).
- **MDS-UPDRS Total (off-medication):** Low-dose: 29.0 points improvement. High-dose: 34.7 points improvement.
- **Hoehn and Yahr:** Low-dose: 3.7 to 2.7 (~27% improvement). High-dose: 3.8 to 2.2 (~42% improvement).
- **Non-motor (NMSS):** Low-dose: 31.7 point improvement. High-dose: 35.8 point improvement.
- **PET imaging:** Overall increase in putamen DAT signals; greater increases in high-dose group. Statistically significant correlation between posterior dorsal putamen DAT uptake and motor improvement.
- **Status:** Active; 12-month data published in *Cell* (Oct 2025). Primary trial continues for 2 years post-transplant with 3 additional years of safety follow-up. Trial expected to conclude early 2026.
- **Interpretation:** Clean safety and consistent dose-response across motor, non-motor, and imaging endpoints are encouraging. However, N=12 open-label with no placebo/sham surgery arm limits causal inference. The MDS-UPDRS Part III improvements (12.7-15.5 points) are clinically meaningful (MCID is ~3.25 points) but must be interpreted cautiously given the known placebo effect of surgical interventions in PD, which historically accounts for ~10-15 points of MDS-UPDRS improvement in sham-controlled trials.
- **Site:** Yonsei University Severance Hospital, Seoul, South Korea

### Financial
- **Market listing:** KOSDAQ (304360.KQ) since May 2023; IPO raised KRW 39.6 billion (~$30M USD) at 18,000 won/share with 994.8:1 oversubscription
- **Market cap:** ~KRW 356 billion (~$270M USD) as of recent trading
- **Revenue:** KRW 13.1 billion (2023), primarily from non-PD products (cosmetic/regenerative medicine); the company is not yet generating revenue from TED-A9
- **No disclosed partnerships** for TED-A9 with big pharma -- the program is internally funded. A licensing deal for ex-Korea rights would be the key financial catalyst
- **Comparison:** bemdaneprocel was acquired by Bayer when they bought BlueRock Therapeutics for $600M upfront + $1B milestones in 2019 (pre-clinical stage). [[raguneprocel]] was developed through an academic collaboration with CiRA/Kyoto University. TED-A9 remains unpartnered, which is both a risk (limited resources for global trials) and an opportunity (full economics retained)
- **Key investors:** Shinhan Venture Investment participated pre-IPO; broader investor base post-KOSDAQ listing

- [[raguneprocel]] (Sumitomo/CiRA, iPSC-derived, NDA filed Japan) is the most advanced cell therapy for PD globally. If approved in H2 2026, it validates the neuron replacement paradigm and creates regulatory precedent that TED-A9 could leverage -- but also demonstrates the bar TED-A9 must clear
- Bemdaneprocel (BlueRock/Bayer, hESC-derived, Phase 3) is the most direct competitor: same cell source (hESC), same target tissue (putamen), same modality. BlueRock's Phase 1 showed 17.9-point MDS-UPDRS Part III improvement at 36 months in the high-dose cohort. Their Phase 3 (exPDite-2, ~102 patients) dosed first patient September 2025 and will generate registrational data before TED-A9 can reach that stage
- TED-A9's potential differentiator is the small-molecule-only manufacturing protocol, which could offer manufacturing cost and scalability advantages if validated -- relevant for a therapy requiring neurosurgical delivery
- The cell therapy field is bifurcating: ESC-derived (TED-A9, bemdaneprocel) vs. iPSC-derived ([[raguneprocel]]) vs. autologous iPSC approaches. ESC approaches share a common risk of requiring chronic immunosuppression; iPSC allogeneic approaches may use HLA-matched banks to reduce this burden
- If bemdaneprocel's Phase 3 succeeds with sham-surgery control, it establishes the definitive evidence base for ESC-derived DA cell therapy -- TED-A9 would need to demonstrate manufacturing or efficacy advantages to compete. If bemdaneprocel fails, TED-A9's differentiated manufacturing may become the next best hope for the ESC approach

## Analysis

TED-A9's *Cell* publication represents a meaningful milestone -- it is one of only three PD cell therapy programs with published human engraftment data (alongside [[raguneprocel]] and bemdaneprocel). The statistically significant correlation between posterior putamen DAT signal and motor improvement is the strongest mechanistic finding, providing biological plausibility that the clinical benefit is graft-derived. The dose-response pattern across motor, non-motor, and imaging endpoints adds further weight.

**Analytical estimate -- Probability of TED-A9 reaching Phase 3 with positive data: 25-30%.** This is our assessment, not from a published source. The reasoning: Base rate for cell therapy programs advancing from Phase 1/2 to Phase 3 in neurodegeneration is low (~15-20%). Adjustments upward: published peer-reviewed data in a top-tier journal (+5%), PET-confirmed engraftment with motor correlation (+10%), dose-response signal (+5%), clean safety at 12 months (+5%). Adjustments downward: no sham-surgery control means placebo effect cannot be excluded (-10%), N=12 is very small (-5%), S.BIOMEDICS has limited resources for global registrational trials without a partner (-5%), bemdaneprocel will generate Phase 3 data first, potentially making TED-A9 redundant if positive (-5%). Net: ~25-30%.

**Signal analysis:** S.BIOMEDICS is a small Korean biotech with a diversified pipeline (5 programs in clinic across PD, spinal cord injury, ischemia, cosmetics). This is not a single-asset company -- TED-A9 is the flagship but the company has revenue from other products. The KOSDAQ listing provides access to Korean capital markets, and the strong IPO oversubscription (994.8:1) signals domestic investor enthusiasm. However, the absence of any big pharma partnership for TED-A9 is notable in a landscape where both major competitors are backed by large pharma (Bayer for bemdaneprocel, Sumitomo for [[raguneprocel]]). The most important near-term catalyst is whether S.BIOMEDICS secures an ex-Korea licensing deal -- this would signal external validation and provide the resources needed for larger, sham-controlled trials. Without a partner, TED-A9 risks remaining a Korea-only program that cannot generate the sham-controlled data needed for FDA or EMA registration.

The decision tree is clear: if 24-month data confirms durability and a partner emerges, TED-A9 becomes a legitimate third entrant in the cell therapy race with differentiated manufacturing. If the signal attenuates at 24 months or no partner materializes, the program likely remains regional while bemdaneprocel and [[raguneprocel]] define the global market.
