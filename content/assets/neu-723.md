# NEU-411

## Summary

Neuron23 (startup, $310M raised) is running the precision medicine counterpart to [[biib122|BIIB122]]'s broad-population LRRK2 program. Phase 1 in >100 healthy volunteers showed clean safety and dramatic pS935-LRRK2 and pRab10 reductions in blood and CSF. The NEULARK Phase 2 trial (NCT06680830, N=~150, 52 weeks) uses a QIAGEN NGS companion diagnostic to select "LRRK2-driven" patients — claimed to be up to 30% of idiopathic PD — and a Roche digital biomarker as primary endpoint. NEU-411 was designed using Neuron23's Origenis AI/ML platform. The March 2026 [[biib122|LUMA]] readout is the existential dependency: if positive, Neuron23 becomes a high-value acquisition target with the only companion diagnostic in the space; if negative, the LRRK2 field crashes and NEULARK's 2027 readout becomes dead money.

## Notes

### Science
- Brain-penetrant, selective LRRK2 kinase inhibitor designed via Origenis AI/ML drug discovery platform — company claims "best-in-class" but no head-to-head selectivity data vs. [[biib122|BIIB122]] is publicly available
- Mechanism: LRRK2 gain-of-function kinase activity drives Rab10 hyperphosphorylation, disrupting endolysosomal trafficking, impairing autophagy, and promoting alpha-synuclein accumulation. Inhibiting LRRK2 kinase reverses this cascade
- Wild-type LRRK2 is activated in dopaminergic neurons in idiopathic PD (not just mutation carriers), published in *Science Translational Medicine* — this is the scientific basis for expanding beyond the 1-2% G2019S population
- Elevated pS1292-LRRK2 in urine exosomes from idiopathic PD patients vs. controls supports pathway activity beyond mutation carriers
- Polygenic risk modulation: G2019S carriers with high polygenic risk score have 27-fold PD risk vs. 10-fold from G2019S alone — supports the thesis that SNP burden predicts LRRK2 pathway activity
- LRRK2 inhibition restores GCase activity in GBA1-mutant neurons (published preclinical data) — convergence with [[pariceract|GBA1 targets]] on lysosomal biology
- Key distinction vs. [[biib122|BIIB122]]: differentiation is NOT molecular (no comparative data) but strategic — companion diagnostic-guided patient selection, digital biomarker endpoint, 52-week fast-failure trial design
- Class-effect safety concern: LRRK2 inhibitors cause reversible lung (vacuolated type II pneumocytes) and kidney (tubular pigmentation) changes in primates. No clinical signals in Phase 1 for either NEU-411 or [[biib122|BIIB122]], but Phase 1 was only 28 days — chronic dosing in Phase 2 (52 weeks) is the real safety test
- Open question: is LRRK2 activation in idiopathic PD causal or a downstream consequence of alpha-synuclein/mitochondrial dysfunction? If downstream, inhibition will not slow disease in non-mutation carriers
- 40% of LRRK2 mutation carriers lack Lewy body pathology at autopsy (they have tau or TDP-43 instead) — LRRK2-PD may not be a synucleinopathy in all cases, complicating endpoint selection

### Clinical

**Phase 1 (Healthy Volunteers)** | NCT TBD | N=>100 | Healthy volunteers, 28 days
- **Primary endpoint:** Safety/tolerability -> Clean safety profile, no serious AEs, no lung/kidney signals
- **Key secondary:** Dramatic reductions in pS935-LRRK2 in blood and CSF; modulation of pRab10 and BMP (bis(monoacylglycero)phosphate)
- **Status:** Completed (data presented AD/PD 2025)
- **Interpretation:** Robust target engagement in periphery and CNS. Comparable safety and PK/PD profile to [[biib122|BIIB122]] Phase 1/2a. Enabled Phase 2 progression.

**NEULARK (Phase 2)** | NCT06680830 | N=~150 | Early PD with LRRK2-driven disease (identified via QIAGEN NGS companion diagnostic targeting 50+ SNPs)
- **Primary endpoint:** Roche digital biomarker score (smartphone-based movement/tremor/cognition monitoring) at 52 weeks
- **Key secondary:** MDS-UPDRS, pRab10, BMP, clinical global impression
- **Patient selection:** QIAGEN NGS companion diagnostic + algorithmic model identifying "elevated LRRK2 pathway activity" — claimed to capture up to 30% of idiopathic PD patients
- **Status:** First patient dosed June 2025; topline data expected 2027
- **Interpretation:** Deliberately designed as the anti-LIGHTHOUSE: 52 weeks (vs. 180), 150 patients (vs. 400), precision-selected (vs. broad enrollment), digital biomarker (vs. MDS-UPDRS alone). If QIAGEN enrichment works, this trial has higher statistical power per patient than [[biib122|LUMA]] despite being 4x smaller. If enrichment fails, the trial is underpowered.

## Analysis

NEU-411 is a strategy bet, not a molecule bet. There is no published evidence that NEU-411 is a superior LRRK2 inhibitor to [[biib122|BIIB122]] — both are potent, selective, brain-penetrant oral small molecules with comparable Phase 1 safety and target engagement. The differentiation is entirely in trial design and commercial infrastructure: companion diagnostic (QIAGEN), digital biomarker endpoint (Roche), precision patient selection (50+ SNP panel + algorithmic model), and fast-failure trial architecture (52 weeks vs. LIGHTHOUSE's 180).

**Analytical estimate — Phase 2 success probability: 35-45%.** This is our assessment, not from a published source. The reasoning:
- Base rate: LRRK2 inhibitors have 0/0 human efficacy readouts -> no direct base rate; using Phase 2 success rate for neuroscience small molecules (~25%) as starting point
- Adjustments upward: precision patient selection enriches for responders (+10%), digital biomarker more sensitive than MDS-UPDRS (+5%), robust Phase 1 target engagement in CSF (+5%), published biological rationale for LRRK2 pathway activation in idiopathic PD (+5%)
- Adjustments downward: 30% addressable population claim is unvalidated (-5%), digital biomarker as regulatory endpoint is unprecedented in PD (-5%), small trial size (150 pts) increases variance (-5%), LRRK2 causality in non-mutation carriers unproven (-5%)
- Net: ~30-40% intrinsic; conditional on LUMA being positive or equivocal (+5-10%), yielding 35-45% in the favorable scenario

**Signal analysis:**
- $310M raised from sophisticated investors (SoftBank, Kleiner Perkins, Redmile) who have seen internal preclinical and Phase 1 data. These are not passive LPs — Redmile is a $6B healthcare hedge fund with deep diligence capability. The investor quality is a positive signal on undisclosed data.
- The QIAGEN/Roche/Quest/Sano partnership web is unusual for a startup at this stage. Each partner has conducted independent technical diligence on the CDx and digital biomarker approach. Four independent institutional validations reduce execution risk.
- The 30% addressable population claim is the critical unvalidated assumption. The biology is plausible — LRRK2 pathway activation in idiopathic PD is published science, not just a company narrative. But translating pathway activation into a prospective diagnostic that predicts drug response is a leap. The actual enrichment may be 10-20%, which would still be commercially viable but reduces trial power.
- The decision tree is asymmetric and favorable: Neuron23 has value in 3 of 4 LUMA scenarios (positive, equivocal in subgroups, equivocal overall) and is only destroyed in the LUMA-fails-outright scenario (~25% probability). This makes it a structurally sound option position on LRRK2.
- Neuron23's TYK2 pipeline and Origenis platform provide a $100M+ floor value even in total LRRK2 failure — the company is not single-asset dependent in the way that the PD narrative implies.
