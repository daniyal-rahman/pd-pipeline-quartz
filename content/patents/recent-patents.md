## LRRK2 Inhibitors

### WO2024108116A1 - LRRK2 Inhibitors and Uses Thereof
- **Assignee**: Neuron23 Inc
- **Filing Date**: November 17, 2023
- **Publication Date**: May 23, 2024
- **Mechanism**: Brain-penetrant small molecule LRRK2 kinase inhibitor targeting gain-of-function mutations (especially G2019S) associated with familial Parkinson's disease
- **Notes**: Neuron23's NEU-411 is in Phase 2 trials (NEULARK study) as of 2025

### WO2024218288A1 - Leucine-Rich Repeat Kinase 2 (LRRK2) Inhibitors
- **Assignee**: H. Lundbeck A/S and Vernalis (R&D) Limited
- **Filing Date**: 2024
- **Publication Date**: October 24, 2024
- **Mechanism**: Novel 2,4,12,13-tetrahydro-11H-5,7-(azenometheno)dipyrazolo scaffold compounds for LRRK2 inhibition
- **Notes**: Lundbeck is a major CNS-focused pharmaceutical company based in Denmark

### WO2024158745A1 - Inhibitors of LRRK2
- **Assignee**: Vanderbilt University
- **Filing Date**: January 23, 2024
- **Mechanism**: Indazole and azaindazole-substituted cyclopenta[c]pyrrole compounds; selective for G2019S mutant LRRK2 over wild-type
- **Notes**: Academic patent; Craig W. Lindsley is a prominent medicinal chemist

### WO2023220247A1 - LRRK2 Inhibitors
- **Assignee**: [Company TBD - US priority filing US18/942,025 in November 2024]
- **Priority Filing**: November 8, 2024
- **Mechanism**: Brain-penetrant, selective kinase inhibitor for LRRK2

## Alpha-Synuclein Targeting

### WO2024184179A1 - Minzasolmin for Use in the Treatment of Parkinson's Disease
- **Assignee**: UCB Biopharma SRL
- **Filing Date**: February 29, 2024
- **Mechanism**: Orally available inhibitor of alpha-synuclein misfolding and downstream aggregation
- **Dosing**: 180 mg twice daily for early-to-moderate PD (Hoehn and Yahr stage <=2.5)
- **Notes**: UCB is a major biopharma; Phase 2a trial underway evaluating disease-modifying potential over 18 months

### US20240141027A1 - Bispecific Binding Molecule
- **Assignee**: BioArctic AB
- **Filing Date**: 2023
- **Publication Date**: May 2, 2024
- **Mechanism**: Bispecific antibodies targeting alpha-synuclein aggregates; includes elements from ABBV-0805 (mAb47)
- **Notes**: AbbVie terminated partnership in 2024, but BioArctic retains rights

### US11919947B2 - Antibody Binding Active Alpha-Synuclein
- **Assignee**: StressMarq Biosciences Inc
- **Grant Date**: March 5, 2024
- **Mechanism**: Monoclonal antibody 2F11 that binds active alpha-synuclein and tau fibrils
- **Notes**: StressMarq primarily provides research reagents

### US20240085435A1 - Alpha-Synuclein Seed Amplification Assay for Peripheral Matrices
- **Assignee**: Amprion Inc
- **Publication Date**: 2024
- **Mechanism**: Diagnostic assay for detecting misfolded alpha-synuclein aggregates in biological fluids (non-CSF)
- **Notes**: Diagnostic rather than therapeutic

## GBA/Glucocerebrosidase (GCase) Targeting

### US20240246952A1 - Small Molecule Modulators of Glucocerebrosidase Activity
- **Assignee**: Vanqua Bio Inc
- **Filing Date**: April 28, 2022
- **Publication Date**: July 25, 2024
- **Mechanism**: Small molecule GCase activators/allosteric modulators for GBA-associated PD and Gaucher disease
- **Notes**: Vanqua Bio's VQ-101 is a brain-penetrant GCase activator in development

### WO2024163012A1 - Compositions and Methods for Neurological Disorders Related to GCase Deficiency
- **Assignee**: Voyager Therapeutics Inc
- **Filing Date**: February 2, 2023
- **Mechanism**: AAV-based gene therapy delivering codon-optimized, CpG-reduced GBA gene to restore GCase activity
- **Target Conditions**: GBA-Parkinson's disease, Gaucher disease, Dementia with Lewy Bodies
- **Notes**: Voyager is a clinical-stage gene therapy company

### WO2024115612A1 - Polymer-Glucocerebrosidase (GBA) Conjugate
- **Assignee**: Fundacio Hospital Universitari Vall d'Hebron Institut de Recerca (VHIR)
- **Filing Date**: 2024
- **Mechanism**: Nanotechnology-based enzyme replacement therapy using polymer-enzyme nanoconjugation
- **Notes**: Academic/hospital patent from Barcelona, Spain

## Other Disease-Modifying Approaches

### US20250051775A1 - Therapeutics for Neurodegenerative Disorders (ASOs)
- **Assignee**: UCL Business Ltd and US Department of Health and Human Services
- **Filing Date**: October 18, 2024
- **Mechanism**: Antisense oligonucleotides (ASOs) targeting UNC13A cryptic exon inclusion; prevents TDP-43-related pathology
- **Target Conditions**: ALS, FTD; also mentions Parkinson's disease and Alzheimer's disease
- **Notes**: Collaboration between UCL and NIH

### US20240366648A1 - NAD-Augmentation Therapy for Parkinson's Disease
- **Assignee**: Vestlandets Innovasjonsselskap AS (VIS)
- **Filing Date**: June 24, 2022 (Priority date: June 24, 2021)
- **Publication Date**: 2024
- **Mechanism**: Oral nicotinamide riboside (NR) to increase neuronal NAD levels, improving mitochondrial function
- **Notes**: Norwegian innovation company; clinical trial data shows NAD augmentation and symptom improvement

### WO2024186658A1 - Human Adipose Stem Cell-Derived Exosomes for Parkinson's Disease
- **Assignee**: University of South Florida, US Department of Veterans Affairs, University of South Florida St Petersburg
- **Filing Date**: March 1, 2024
- **Mechanism**: Exosomes from adipose-derived stem cells containing MALAT1 lncRNA; neuroprotective via reduction of microglial inflammation
- **Administration**: Intranasal delivery
- **Notes**: Academic patent; early-stage research

### US20240207289A1 - Method to Identify and Alleviate Symptoms of Parkinsonism
- **Assignee**: Prepaire Labs Limited
- **Publication Date**: June 27, 2024
- **Mechanism**: Cannabinoid-based compounds with neuroprotective properties
- **Notes**: Less conventional approach; early stage
