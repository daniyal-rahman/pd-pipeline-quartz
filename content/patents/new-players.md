# New/Lesser-Known Players in Parkinson's Disease Patents

#claude


> **IMPORTANT CAVEAT**: A patent filing does NOT indicate active development or a viable drug program. Patents are filed to protect intellectual property and may represent early-stage research, abandoned programs, or defensive filings. These companies require further due diligence to confirm pipeline activity.

---

## Emerging Commercial Players

### Neuron23 Inc (South San Francisco, CA)
- **Focus**: LRRK2 inhibitors for Parkinson's disease
- **Lead Asset**: NEU-411 (brain-penetrant LRRK2 inhibitor)
- **Stage**: Phase 2 (NEULARK trial initiated 2025)
- **Patent**: WO2024108116A1 (Filed November 2023)
- **Funding**: $96.5M Series D (June 2025)
- **Notable**: Collaboration with QIAGEN for companion diagnostic; using machine learning for patient stratification to identify broader LRRK2-driven PD population beyond mutation carriers
- **Why Interesting**: Clinical-stage biotech specifically focused on LRRK2 with precision medicine approach

### Brenig Therapeutics (Founded 2021)
- **Focus**: LRRK2 inhibitors and NLRP3 inhibitors for neurodegeneration
- **Lead Asset**: BT-267 (LRRK2 inhibitor)
- **Stage**: Phase 1 (initiated November 2024)
- **Funding**: $65M Series A (July 2024)
- **Investors**: NEA, BioGeneration Ventures, OrbiMed, Torrey Pines
- **Notable**: Drug designed using AI in collaboration with Expert Systems; claims "best-in-class" CNS exposure with minimal peripheral effects
- **Why Interesting**: Recently funded, clinical entry, AI-driven drug design

### Vanqua Bio Inc
- **Focus**: GCase activators for GBA-Parkinson's disease
- **Lead Asset**: VQ-101 (brain-penetrant small molecule GCase activator)
- **Stage**: Preclinical/early clinical development
- **Patent**: US20240246952A1 (Published July 2024)
- **Notable**: Targets the largest genetically defined subset of PD (~5-15% of all PD patients have GBA mutations)
- **Why Interesting**: Novel mechanism (allosteric GCase activation) vs. gene therapy approaches

### Gain Therapeutics (Switzerland)
- **Focus**: GCase modulators using structure-based drug discovery
- **Lead Asset**: GT-02287 (small molecule GCase modulator)
- **Stage**: Phase 1b (ongoing as of 2025)
- **Notable**: Positive Phase 1 results in 2024 showing GCase activity increase in healthy volunteers; exploring both GBA1-mutation carriers and idiopathic PD
- **Why Interesting**: Advancing clinical data in GBA-PD space; competing with larger players

### Prepaire Labs Limited
- **Focus**: Cannabinoid-based neuroprotection
- **Patent**: US20240207289A1 (June 2024)
- **Stage**: Early research
- **Notable**: Exploring CBD pharmacology and cannabinoid compounds for motor disorders
- **Why Interesting**: Unconventional approach to PD; may appeal to certain patient populations

### Advanced Protein Technologies Corp
- **Focus**: Novel compositions for dopamine-related diseases
- **Patent**: US20240358729A1 (2024)
- **Stage**: Early development
- **Notable**: 2'-FL containing compositions
- **Why Interesting**: Asian company entering PD space with novel approach

## Academic/Hospital Patent Holders (Potential Spin-outs)

### Fundacio Hospital Universitari Vall d'Hebron Institut de Recerca (VHIR) - Barcelona, Spain
- **Patent**: WO2024115612A1 (2024)
- **Technology**: Nanotechnology-based enzyme replacement therapy using polymer-GBA nanoconjugation
- **Why Interesting**: Novel drug delivery approach for GCase; potential for spin-out or licensing

### Vestlandets Innovasjonsselskap AS (VIS) - Norway
- **Patent**: US20240366648A1 (2024)
- **Technology**: NAD-augmentation therapy using nicotinamide riboside (NR)
- **Inventors**: Charalampos Tzoulis, Christian Dölle
- **Why Interesting**: Clinical trial data showing brain NAD increase and symptom improvement; repurposing approach with established safety profile

### City University of Hong Kong
- **Patent**: US20240110200A1 (April 2024)
- **Technology**: Recombinant AAV vectors for brain diseases
- **Why Interesting**: Expanding gene therapy approaches in Asian markets

### University of South Florida / US Department of Veterans Affairs
- **Patent**: WO2024186658A1 (March 2024)
- **Technology**: Adipose stem cell-derived exosomes with MALAT1 lncRNA for neuroprotection
- **Notable**: Intranasal delivery route; demonstrated efficacy in young and aged animal models
- **Why Interesting**: Exosome therapeutics is a growing field; non-invasive delivery

### Vanderbilt University
- **Patent**: WO2024158745A1 (January 2024)
- **Technology**: Selective G2019S LRRK2 inhibitors (indazole/azaindazole compounds)
- **Lead Inventor**: Craig W. Lindsley (prominent medicinal chemist)
- **Why Interesting**: Academic group known for CNS drug discovery; potential licensing opportunities

### Qatar Foundation for Education, Science and Community Development
- **Patent**: WO2022060236A1
- **Technology**: Antibodies targeting non-phosphorylated alpha-synuclein aggregates
- **Why Interesting**: Middle Eastern institution entering PD research; potential for regional partnerships

### United Arab Emirates University
- **Patent**: EP2961774A4
- **Technology**: Alpha-synuclein antibodies
- **Why Interesting**: Another Gulf region academic institution with PD IP

## Companies That May Be Pivoting to PD

### Palomo Limited
- **Patent**: US10125363B2 (acquired February 2024)
- **Background**: Acquired through bankruptcy from NLife Therapeutics, S.L.
- **Technology**: Oligonucleotide delivery to specific neuron types for PD
- **Why Interesting**: Distressed asset acquisition may indicate opportunistic entry to PD space

### Amprion Inc
- **Patent**: US20240085435A1 (2024)
- **Technology**: Alpha-synuclein seed amplification assay (diagnostic)
- **Why Interesting**: Diagnostic company that could enable precision medicine approaches in PD; not a therapeutic player but important ecosystem participant

### StressMarq Biosciences Inc
- **Patent**: US11919947B2 (March 2024)
- **Background**: Primarily a research reagent company
- **Technology**: Monoclonal antibody 2F11 binding alpha-synuclein
- **Why Interesting**: Research tools company with therapeutic IP; may license or partner

## What to Watch For

1. **Neuron23** - Most advanced of the emerging LRRK2 players; Phase 2 data expected 2027
2. **Brenig Therapeutics** - Phase 1 data from BT-267 could differentiate vs. other LRRK2 inhibitors
3. **Vanqua Bio & Gain Therapeutics** - Competing GCase activator approaches; small molecule vs. small molecule
4. **Voyager Therapeutics** - Gene therapy approach to GCase; different mechanism than small molecules
5. **VIS (Norway) NAD therapy** - Clinical data from nicotinamide riboside could be near-term catalyst
