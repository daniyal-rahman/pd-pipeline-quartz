## Annovis Bio
**Website:** https://www.annovisbio.com/pipeline

### Parkinson's Disease Programs

| Asset | Mechanism | Stage | Indication |
|-------|-----------|-------|------------|
| **Buntanetap** | Targets neurotoxic proteins and inflammation; reduces key plasma biomarkers | Phase 3 (completed) | Parkinson's disease, Alzheimer's disease |

**Phase 3 Results (471 patients with early PD):**
- Clinically meaningful improvements across all key MDS-UPDRS measures
- Halted cognitive decline (placebo groups worsened)
- Greatest cognitive benefits in patients with mild dementia (MMSE >20) positive for amyloid and tau

**Phase 2a Results:**
- Statistically significant improvements in movement (MDS-UPDRS Part III)
- Improved cognitive processing speed (WAIS coding test)
- Optimal dosing: 10-20mg groups
