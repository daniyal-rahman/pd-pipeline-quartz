## Successfully Scraped - With PD Programs

### Biohaven
**Website:** https://www.biohaven.com/pipeline
**Status:** Active PD program
**Key Asset:** BHV-8000 (TYK2/JAK1 inhibitor) - Phase 3 for PD
**Mechanism:** Brain-penetrant neuroinflammation modulator
**Notes:** Targets neuroinflammation as driver of neurodegeneration

### Acadia Pharmaceuticals
**Website:** https://www.acadia.com/pipeline
**Status:** Approved PD treatment + pipeline
**Key Assets:**
- NUPLAZID (pimavanserin) - Approved for PD Psychosis
- Remlifanserin (ACP-204) - Phase 2 for LBD Psychosis (includes PD dementia)
**Notes:** 5HT2A inverse agonist platform

### Lundbeck
**Website:** https://www.lundbeck.com/global/our-science/pipeline
**Status:** Active PD program
**Key Assets:**
- Lu AF28996 (D1-D2 agonist) - Phase 1 for PD
- Amlenetug (anti-alpha-synuclein antibody) - Phase 3 for MSA
**Notes:** MSA is a related synucleinopathy

### NeuroDerm
**Website:** https://www.neuroderm.com
**Status:** Late-stage PD program (NDA submitted)
**Key Asset:** ND0612 (subcutaneous levodopa/carbidopa infusion)
**Mechanism:** Continuous drug delivery to reduce motor fluctuations
**Notes:** Drug-device combination; Phase 3 completed successfully

### Alector
**Website:** https://www.alector.com/pipeline
**Status:** Multiple PD programs
**Key Assets:**
- AL101 (progranulin antibody) - Phase 2 for AD/PD
- AL050-ABC (GCase-ERT with brain carrier) - Preclinical for PD
- ADP062-ABC (alpha-synuclein siRNA) - Research
- ADP065-ABC (NLRP3 siRNA for neuroinflammation) - Research
**Notes:** Unique brain carrier technology for enzyme delivery

### Annovis Bio
**Website:** https://www.annovisbio.com/pipeline
**Status:** Phase 3 completed for PD
**Key Asset:** Buntanetap
**Mechanism:** Targets neurotoxic proteins and inflammation
**Results:** Clinically meaningful improvements in MDS-UPDRS; halted cognitive decline
