## Key Insights

### 1. The Biobucks Illusion
The ~$28B+ headline aggregate collapses to **~$3-4B in actual upfront cash**. The remaining $24B+ is milestone-contingent with weighted-average payment probability of 15-25%. Expected total payout across all deals: **$6-8B, not $28B**.

### 2. Capital Allocation vs. Scientific Evidence

| Category | Capital Deployed | Evidence Strength | Assessment |
|----------|-----------------|-------------------|------------|
| BBB platforms | ~$7B biobucks | Zero PD efficacy data | **Over-invested for PD** |
| Symptomatic (tavapadon) | $8.7B | Phase 3 positive | Overpaid at deal level |
| Genetic PD (LRRK2 + GBA1) | $8.7B+ | Best-validated targets | **Appropriately invested** |
| Alpha-synuclein | $5B+ cumulative | 0-for-7 clinically | **Over-invested** |
| NLRP3 neuroinflammation | $1.5B+ | MR causality fails | Over-invested vs. evidence |
| PINK1/Mitophagy | ~$660M | Strong genetic validation | **Under-invested** — clearest gap |
| Cell therapy | $2B+ | Best motor data in field | Appropriately invested |
| AI discovery | $3B+ headline / ~$100M real | Zero clinical proof | Headline inflation; actual spend modest |

### 3. Conviction Tiers (by upfront/total ratio)
- **Full conviction (100%):** AbbVie/Cerevel ($8.7B), Lilly/Ventyx ($1.2B), Lilly/Prevail ($1.04B) — all acquisitions
- **High conviction (>30%):** Biogen/Denali (47.7%), MeiraGTx/Hologen (46.5%), AbbVie/Capsida (~30%) — cash-heavy or acquisition structures
- **Moderate conviction (10-30%):** Novartis/UCB (10.0%), AbbVie/Mitokinin (16.8%), Insilico/Hygtia (15.2%), GSK/Vesalius (12.3%)
- **Low conviction / optionality (<10%):** Novartis/Arrowhead (9.1%), Sanofi/ABL Bio (7.1%), Capsida/Lilly (7.4%), Roche/Prothena (6.0%), Biogen/Alectos (2.1%), Lilly/ABL Bio (2.1%), GSK/ABL Bio (2.0%), Biohaven/Highlightll (2.1%), Neurocrine/Voyager (4.0%)

### 4. Landscape-Reshaping Catalysts (2026-2028)

| Catalyst | Timing | If Positive | If Negative |
|----------|--------|-------------|-------------|
| **LUMA** (LRRK2 in sporadic PD) | Mar 2026 | Validates LRRK2 broadly; Neuron23 reprices | LRRK2 field crashes; capital → GBA1/mitophagy |
| **ACTIVATE** (pariceract in GBA-PD) | Q2 2026 | Oral disease modification validated; BIAL acquisition target; gene therapy pressured | Small molecule GCase dead; gene therapy sole GBA path |
| **Prasinezumab Phase 3** (PARAISO) | 2027-2028 | First PD DMT; alpha-synuclein validated | 0-for-8 antibodies; aSyn antibodies permanently dead |
| **BlueRock Phase 3 interim** | ~2027 | Accelerated cell therapy approval | Cell therapy for PD set back a decade |
| **Tavapadon NDA** | Mid 2026 | D1/D5 agonist class established | Unlikely to fail at this stage |

### 5. Biggest Strategic Gaps
1. **Combination therapy** — Zero programs combining validated targets (LRRK2i + GCase activator has preclinical synergy data, no one is developing it)
2. **PD companion diagnostics** — Neuron23/QIAGEN only dedicated CDx play; comprehensive multi-gene panel is white space
3. **PINK1/mitophagy** — Strongest genetic evidence outside GBA1/LRRK2, weakest capital commitment (~$660M vs. $1.5B for NLRP3)
4. **Non-motor symptom therapeutics** — Cause more disability than motor symptoms in advanced PD; almost no investment
