## Phase 1: Deep Dive

### 1. Scientific Foundation: What Makes Capsida's Capsids Different?

#### The Problem with Standard AAV9

AAV9 is the current gold standard for CNS gene therapy, but it has **crippling limitations**:

**Poor CNS Transduction:**
- IV delivery requires **doses ≥1×10¹⁴ vg/kg** to achieve therapeutic CNS levels
- At this dose, a 10kg child receives **>1 quadrillion viral particles** (more than total cells in the body)
- Vast majority (~95%) ends up in **liver, not brain**

**Hepatotoxicity:**
- **~90% of IV AAV trials** report elevated liver enzymes (ALT/AST)
- **~33% of patients at 1×10¹⁴ vg/kg** experience hepatotoxicity requiring immunosuppression
- Multiple cases of **acute liver failure** (some fatal) in recent CNS AAV trials

**Peripheral Neurotoxicity:**
- High-dose AAV9 causes **dorsal root ganglia (DRG) sensory neuron degeneration**
- Observed in NHP/piglet studies: proprioceptive deficits, ataxia, severe DRG lesions
- This is **direct AAV transduction toxicity**, not immune-mediated

**The Fundamental Challenge:** To get enough AAV into the brain via IV, you must systemically dose so high that you poison the liver and peripheral nervous system.

#### Capsida's Solution: Combinatorial Loop Engineering + NHP Screening

**Platform Overview:**

Capsida's approach is **NOT incremental improvement** — it's **systematic capsid redesign**:

1. **Sequential engineering of multiple surface-exposed loops** on the AAV capsid
2. **Positive selection** for brain enrichment (using Cre-transgenic models)
3. **Negative selection** against liver tropism (detargeting mutations)
4. **Direct screening in non-human primates** (bypasses mouse-to-human translation failure)

**Why NHP Screening Matters:**
- Rodent-selected capsids **fail to translate to primates** (different receptor expression)
- Capsida screens **billions of variants directly in marmosets/macaques**
- Identified **novel human BBB receptors** conserved across primates (not mice)

**Published Validation:**

**Nature Neuroscience (2021):** "AAV capsid variants with brain-wide transgene expression and decreased liver targeting after intravenous delivery in mouse and marmoset"
- Combinatorial AAV engineering of multiple loops
- Capsids enriched in brain by positive selection
- Targeted away from liver by negative selection in Cre-transgenic mice

**Nature Nanotechnology (2023):** "Adeno-associated viral vectors for functional intravenous gene transfer throughout the non-human primate brain"
- **AAV.CAP-Mac** variant identified via multi-species NHP screening
- Increased transduction over AAV9 in NHPs
