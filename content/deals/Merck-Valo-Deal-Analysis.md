## PHASE 2: THESIS EXTRACTION & EVALUATION

### THE THESIS: What Must Be True for This to Work?

**Core Bet:** AI + longitudinal real-world patient data (17M records, 20-30 year spans) can identify NOVEL, CAUSAL PD targets that traditional genetics/GWAS/proteomics have missed.

**Chain of Assumptions:**

1. **Data → Insight:** 17M patient records contain hidden patterns revealing PD subtypes and causal mechanisms
   - **Requires:** Data quality, PD representation, causal inference algorithms that work
   - **Risk:** EHR data is noisy, biased (socioeconomic, racial, diagnostic), incomplete

2. **Insight → Target:** AI can translate patterns into druggable biological targets
   - **Requires:** Targets must be (a) causal not correlative, (b) druggable, (c) validated in orthogonal models
   - **Risk:** False discoveries, undruggable mechanisms, species/model translation failure

3. **Target → Molecule:** Closed-loop AI chemistry (Logica) can rapidly design small molecules
   - **Requires:** Predictive models for BBB penetration, target engagement, safety in humans
   - **Risk:** In silico predictions ≠ in vivo reality (PK/PD, off-target effects)

4. **Molecule → Clinic:** PD-relevant models predict human efficacy
   - **Requires:** Better preclinical models than current (rodent, NHP, iPSC)
   - **Risk:** PD preclinical models notoriously poor at predicting human outcomes

5. **Clinic → Approval:** Novel target shows disease modification in heterogeneous PD population
   - **Requires:** Right patient subtype, biomarkers for target engagement/efficacy, trial design for slow progression
   - **Risk:** PD heterogeneity, long trial durations (3-5 years), high placebo response, lack of biomarkers

**What Makes PD Especially Hard?**

From research literature:
- **Translational failure rate >90%** in PD neuroprotection trials
- **Root causes:**
  - Poor preclinical model predictive validity (immortalized cell lines, animal models don't capture complexity)
  - Disease heterogeneity (clinical subtypes unstable over time; "PD" is likely 10+ diseases)
  - Late diagnosis (profound neurodegeneration already present; too late to modify?)
  - Lack of biomarkers for target engagement, patient stratification, efficacy measurement
  - CNS-specific challenges: BBB, tissue access, non-invasive target engagement measurement
  - $1B+ per drug, longer dev timelines = high patent expiration risk

**Counter-Thesis: Why AI MIGHT Work Where Traditional Failed:**

1. **Subtype Discovery:** AI can identify molecular subtypes within "PD" that share causal biology (Vesalius thesis)
   - Traditional: Clinical symptom-based subtypes (tremor-dominant vs PIGD) are unstable
   - AI: Molecular clustering (proteomics, metabolomics, genomics) could reveal true endotypes

2. **Longitudinal Causality:** 20-30 year patient records capture disease evolution, not just snapshots
   - Can identify which biomarkers PRECEDE symptoms (causal) vs appear later (consequence)
   - Enables early intervention target discovery

3. **Human Data First:** Bypass animal model translation failure by starting with human biology
   - Traditional: Target from mouse → fail in human
   - AI: Target from human data → validate in models → back to human

4. **Repurposing + Novel Targets:** AI can scan both new mechanisms AND reposition existing drugs for PD subtypes
   - Lower risk, faster timelines for repurposing
   - Novel targets for unmet biology

### EVIDENCE FOR THE THESIS

**Supporting Data:**

1. **Insilico ISM001-055 (IPF):** Proves AI can discover + design clinically effective drugs for chronic disease (Phase IIa positive)

2. **SandboxAQ/UCSF (PD):** 30x hit rate improvement shows AI can accelerate molecular optimization in neuro

3. **Genetic Validation Gap:** Despite GWAS identifying 90+ PD risk loci, <5 have led to drugs (LRRK2, GBA1 are exceptions). AI might find the "missing druggable targets"

4. **MJFF Endorsement:** Valo selected for Targets to Therapies Initiative → expert validation of approach

5. **nference Data Quality:** 40M patient journeys, real-time access, physician notes + imaging = richer than typical EHR

6. **Novo Nordisk Expansion:** $4.6B deal (20 programs) in obesity/T2D suggests platform works in metabolic disease

**Weak Points in Evidence:**

1. **Valo Has 0 Clinical Validations in ANY Disease:**
   - OPL-0401 (in-licensed, not discovered) → Phase 2 fail
   - Lupus candidate → pre-IND, no timeline
   - NO published peer-reviewed validations of target discoveries

2. **Neurodegeneration ≠ Metabolic Disease:**
   - Novo deal is cardiometabolic (clearer endpoints, better models, faster trials)
   - PD is orders of magnitude harder (CNS, heterogeneity, lack of biomarkers)

3. **"17M Records" Is Marketing Until Proven:**
   - No disclosure of PD patient N
   - No published target discoveries
   - This deal is buying POTENTIAL, not validated output

4. **AI Drug Discovery Hype Cycle:**
   - BenevolentAI collapsed despite early promise (baricitinib for COVID was repurposing, not discovery)
   - 75+ AI molecules in clinic, but 0 approvals in neuro
   - Success rate hasn't demonstrably improved yet

### EVIDENCE AGAINST THE THESIS

**Structural Barriers AI Can't Solve:**

1. **Preclinical Model Failure:**
   - AI can optimize for in vitro or rodent models, but if models don't predict human PD, optimization is futile
   - PD animal models (MPTP, 6-OHDA, genetic) don't fully capture human disease
   - iPSC neurons show PD phenotypes but lack brain architecture/glia/immune context

2. **Disease Heterogeneity:**
   - If "PD" is 10+ distinct diseases, sample size required to find subtype-specific targets may exceed 17M records
   - Genetic PD (LRRK2, GBA1, SNCA) = <15% of cases; idiopathic PD mechanism unknown
   - AI might identify correlations that don't translate across subtypes

3. **EHR Data Limitations (Documented in Literature):**
   - **Bias:** Socioeconomic, racial, diagnostic (movement disorder specialist vs general neuro vs misdiagnosis)
   - **Incompleteness:** Missing data, lost to follow-up, incomplete phenotyping
   - **Quality:** Free-text notes require NLP (error-prone); diagnostic codes are noisy
   - **FDA Guidance (Jan 2025):** Emphasizes bias assessment, data provenance, diverse datasets
   - **EMA (2024):** Calls for careful data quality checks, bias minimization

4. **Causal Inference ≠ Causality:**
   - Observational data (EHRs) can suggest associations, but can't prove causation
   - Genetic validation (Mendelian randomization) is gold standard; unclear if Valo is doing this

5. **Target Validation Fragmentation:**
   - Traditional validation is "inefficient and fragmented" per MJFF/literature
   - AI can propose targets faster, but validation bottleneck remains

**Historical Precedent:**

- **40+ years of PD neuroprotection failures** despite rational targets (MAO-B, coenzyme Q10, creatine, etc.)
- **Every promising target has failed in clinic:** Excitotoxicity, oxidative stress, mitochondrial, inflammation
- **Why would AI-discovered targets fare better if validation process is the same?**

- NOT for clinical assets, NOT for validated targets, but for TARGET IDENTIFICATION
- **Interpretation:** 40 years of PD drug development has exhausted traditional approaches
- Known targets (LRRK2, GBA1, alpha-synuclein) are crowded (10+ programs each)
- **Market gap:** Novel, differentiated PD targets with human genetic validation

**SIGNAL 3: Human Data is the New Moat**

- Valo's competitive advantage is NOT AI algorithms (those are commoditizing)
- Advantage is DATA: 17M + 40M patient records with longitudinal, multi-modal phenotypes
- nference partnership (March 2025) = data acquisition strategy
- **Trend:** AI drug discovery companies are becoming data aggregators
  - Recursion: Phenomic screening (biggest cell imaging dataset)
  - Valo: EHR + biobank + nference
  - Tempus: Cancer genomics + clinical outcomes
- **Implication:** Startups with proprietary human datasets (biobanks, health systems, registries) have M&A/partnership value

**SIGNAL 4: Discovery-Stage Deals are Back (With Caution)**

- 2020-2021: Pharma paid big upfronts for platforms (BioNTech, Moderna for COVID)
- 2023-2024: Retreat to late-stage, de-risked assets
- 2025: Return to discovery deals BUT with milestone-heavy structures
- **Shift:** Pharma wants innovation exposure without balance sheet risk
- **Implication:** Early-stage companies should expect:
  - Lower upfronts than 2020-2021
  - More milestones (pharma offloading risk)
  - Need for external validation (grants, KOL endorsement, peer-reviewed pubs)

### Sourcing Opportunities: Where to Look

**THESIS:** If Merck is paying $3B+ (in potential) for PD target discovery, there's a market for:

1. **Novel PD Target Validators**
   - Companies that can take GWAS/AI-identified targets → genetic validation (Mendelian randomization, functional genomics)
   - Example: Complement therapeutics space (C1q, C3, C5 in neuroinflammation)
   - **Gap:** MJFF Targets to Therapies Initiative exists because validation is fragmented
   - **Opportunity:** Platform for high-throughput target validation in human iPSC neurons, organoids, or animal models

2. **PD Subtype Stratifiers**
   - Vesalius thesis: "PD" is many diseases; AI can find molecular subtypes
   - **Gap:** Clinical subtypes (tremor-dominant vs PIGD) are unstable; no molecular taxonomy exists
   - **Opportunity:** Diagnostic companies using proteomics/metabolomics/imaging to define PD endotypes
   - Example: SomaLogic (proteomics), Metabolon (metabolomics), nference (EHR phenotyping)

3. **BBB Delivery Platforms for Neuro**
   - AI can identify targets, but delivery to CNS remains bottleneck
   - **Opportunity:**
     - Focused ultrasound (blood-brain barrier opening)
     - Brain-penetrant biologics (Denali's Enzyme Transport Vehicle, others)
     - Nanoparticle/exosome delivery for PD-relevant brain regions

4. **Human PD Models**
   - Valo acquired TARA for cardiac models; no equivalent for neuro
   - **Gap:** iPSC neurons don't recapitulate brain architecture, glia, immune cells
   - **Opportunity:**
     - Brain organoids with microglia + astrocytes (neuroinflammation modeling)
     - Microphysiological systems ("brain-on-a-chip")
     - Patient-derived iPSC programs with deep phenotyping

5. **Genetic PD Precision Medicine**
   - LRRK2, GBA1 mutations = 10-15% of PD, but genetically defined → easier trials
   - Gain Therapeutics (GBA1/GCase) is proof-of-concept
   - **Opportunity:**
     - LRRK2 inhibitors (multiple in clinic: Denali, Biogen, Abbvie)
     - GBA1 modulators (Gain, Sanofi/Prevail, others)
     - Other genetic PD targets: SNCA (alpha-synuclein), VPS35, PINK1/Parkin (mitophagy)

6. **AI Platforms with Differentiated Data Assets**
   - Merck/GSK are paying for data, not algorithms
   - **Sourcing filter:** AI drug discovery companies with:
     - Proprietary biobanks (esp neuro: brain tissue, CSF, longitudinal imaging)
     - Partnerships with national health systems (UK Biobank, All of Us, etc)
     - Disease-specific registries (PD, ALS, AD)
   - **Red flag:** Companies with only public data (PubMed mining, ChEMBL)

7. **Post-BenevolentAI Distressed Assets**
   - AI drug discovery shakeout → failed companies with valuable components
   - **Opportunity:**
     - Acqui-hire teams with AI + neuro expertise
     - License algorithms/platforms from defunct companies
     - In-license shelved assets (early-stage targets, datasets)

8. **PD Biomarker Companies**
   - Lack of biomarkers is #1 clinical trial failure cause in PD
   - **Need:**
     - Target engagement biomarkers (does drug hit target in brain?)
     - Progression biomarkers (is disease slowing?)
     - Subtype stratification biomarkers (which patients have which biology?)
   - **Opportunity:**
     - CSF/blood proteomics (alpha-synuclein seed amplification assays)
     - Neuroimaging (DAT-SPECT, MRI, PET for different targets)
     - Digital biomarkers (wearables for motor progression)

### Deal Sourcing Anti-Patterns (What to Avoid)

**RED FLAGS from Merck/Valo Analysis:**

1. **"$XB Deal" Headlines Without Upfront Disclosure**
   - If upfront is undisclosed, it's small
   - Milestone-heavy deals are PR, not validation
   - **Filter:** Ask "What's the upfront?" If answer is vague, discount headline by 90%

2. **AI Drug Discovery Companies with 0 Clinical Validations**
   - Valo is 6 years old with 1 failed Phase 2 (in-licensed) and 1 pre-IND asset
   - BenevolentAI had similar profile → collapse
   - **Filter:** Require at least 1 Phase 1 completion with positive data before investing/partnering

3. **Platform Companies That Don't Publish**
   - Valo: 0 Nature/Science/Cell papers
   - Contrast: Recursion publishes regularly (phenomic screening validation)
   - **Filter:** Peer-reviewed validation is minimum bar for platform credibility

4. **Discovery Companies in CNS Without Big Pharma Partners**
   - PD/AD/ALS require 10-15 year timelines, $500M+ to approval
   - No VC fund can finance this alone
   - **Filter:** CNS-focused AI/platform companies need pharma partnerships by Series B or are zombie companies

5. **Overfitted to Animal Models**
   - AI optimizing for rodent PD models is repeating historical mistakes
   - **Filter:** Prefer human data-first approaches (EHR, iPSC, human genetics)

### Emerging Sourcing Thesis: "PD Subtype Precision Medicine"

**INSIGHT:** Merck/Valo and GSK/Vesalius are BOTH betting on AI to identify PD subtypes → subtype-specific targets.

**Why This Matters:**
- Traditional PD trials: Enroll heterogeneous patients, dilute signal, fail
- Genetic PD (LRRK2, GBA1): Homogeneous biology, easier to show efficacy
- **Future:** Molecular PD endotypes (defined by proteomics, genomics, imaging) enable basket trials

**Sourcing Strategy:**
1. **Find companies defining PD molecular subtypes**
   - Proteomics: CSF/blood panels that classify PD biology
   - Genomics: Polygenic risk scores, rare variant burden
   - Imaging: Neuromelanin MRI, tau/alpha-synuclein PET tracers
   - Multi-omics integration: ML on genomics + proteomics + metabolomics + imaging

2. **Find drugs with subtype-specific mechanisms**
   - Example: GBA1 modulators work in GBA1-mutant PD (15% of patients)
   - Opportunity: Drugs for "neuroinflammatory PD" or "mitochondrial PD" or "lysosomal PD" endotypes

3. **Partner diagnostics + therapeutics**
   - "Companion diagnostic" model: Biomarker test → identifies patients for drug
   - PD needs this (heterogeneity is biggest barrier)

**Market Opportunity:**
- If PD is 10 diseases, each disease is smaller market ($200-500M vs $1-5B)
- BUT: Success rate is higher (enriched trials, clearer biology)
- **Math:** 5 drugs × $300M market × 20% success rate = better than 1 drug × $2B market × 5% success rate
