## PHASE 1: DEEP DIVE

### 1. Scientific Foundation

#### **GBA2 vs GBA1: Critical Distinction**

**GBA1 (Lysosomal):**
- Encodes glucocerebrosidase (GCase), a lysosomal enzyme breaking down glucocerebroside into glucose and ceramide
- Mutations are the **single largest genetic risk factor for PD** (8-12% of sporadic PD)
- Loss of function → lysosomal dysfunction → alpha-synuclein accumulation
- Well-established PD target with 12+ programs in development

**GBA2 (Non-Lysosomal):**
- β-glucosidase operating at ER/Golgi, NOT in lysosomes
- Functions at neutral pH in cytosol
- Hydrolyzes glucosylceramide (GlcCer) to ceramide + glucose
- **Paradox:** Loss-of-function GBA2 mutations cause hereditary spastic paraplegia (SPG46), cerebellar ataxia
- **Therapeutic hypothesis:** *Inhibiting* GBA2 paradoxically *improves* lysosomal function

#### **The GBA2 Inhibition Mechanism**

The counterintuitive rationale:

1. **GBA2 inhibition → increased cytosolic GlcCer**
2. **Increased cytosolic GlcCer → upregulation of vATPase V0a1 subunit**
3. **Increased vATPase → restored lysosomal acidification**
4. **Restored pH → proper lysosomal function → alpha-synuclein clearance**

**Published Evidence:**
- **Niemann-Pick C disease:** GBA2 inhibition reversed endolysosomal pH increases and restored disrupted lipid trafficking
- **Batten disease:** GBA2 inhibition showed beneficial effects in mouse models
- **Mouse PD models:** Preclinical studies showed re-acidifying lysosomes protects against neuronal loss
- **GBA2 knockout paradox:** In GBA1-deficient models, GBA2 deletion sometimes *worsened* pathology, suggesting complex sphingolipid metabolic interactions

**Key Scientific Concern:** The mechanism relies on indirect regulation via cytosolic lipid levels affecting vATPase expression—a multi-step pathway with unclear clinical translatability.
