# AB126

## Summary

AB126 is an unmodified neural stem cell-derived exosome and the first exosome to enter human clinical trials for a neurological indication (IND cleared January 2024). Aruna Bio (startup, private) is running a Phase 1b/2a dose-ascending trial in acute ischemic stroke patients with poor prognosis post-thrombectomy, with trial initiation expected in H2 2024. Unlike receptor-targeted BBB shuttles such as [[trontinemab]] (TfR1 transcytosis) or engineered exosome platforms like Evox's DeliverEX, AB126 exploits the natural tropism of neural-derived exosomes for CNS tissue — crossing the BBB via integrin-mediated transcytosis without surface engineering. The platform's therapeutic cargo is endogenous: anti-inflammatory and neuroprotective molecules inherent to neural stem cell exosomes, rather than an exogenous drug payload. If the stroke trial demonstrates clean safety and preliminary efficacy, the platform's broadest value is as a CNS delivery vehicle for neurodegenerative diseases including PD, ALS, and Alzheimer's, where neuroinflammation is a core pathological driver. If safety signals emerge or exosome manufacturing proves difficult to scale, it would set back the entire exosome therapeutics field for neurological indications given AB126's first-mover position.

## Notes

### Science

- **What it is:** AB126 is an unmodified exosome (30-150nm extracellular vesicle) derived from Aruna Bio's proprietary human neural stem cell line. "Unmodified" means no surface engineering or exogenous cargo loading — the therapeutic activity comes entirely from the exosome's native molecular contents ([GlobeNewswire, Jan 2024](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/01/16/2809846/0/en/Aruna-Bio-Announces-FDA-Clearance-of-IND-for-Lead-Program-AB126-Enabling-the-First-Exosome-to-Enter-in-Human-Clinical-Trials-for-a-Neurological-Indication.html))
- **BBB crossing mechanism:** Neural-derived exosomes cross the BBB primarily via transcytosis through brain microvascular endothelial cells. The mechanism involves integrin-mediated interactions, particularly LFA-1 on exosome surfaces binding ICAM-1 on endothelial cells, triggering internalization and transcellular transport. Neural stem cell-derived exosomes carry surface markers that confer inherent CNS tropism — they localize to hippocampal neurons after IV administration in preclinical models ([PMC: Insights into Exosome Transport through the BBB](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10144189/); [PMC: Transport of EVs across the BBB](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7352415/))
- **Proposed therapeutic mechanisms:** (1) Anti-inflammatory — modulates inflammasome activity in the CNS, reducing neuroinflammation; (2) Neuroprotective — promotes neuronal survival via endogenous cargo; (3) Neuro-regenerative — supports tissue repair pathways. These are the endogenous bioactivities of neural stem cell exosomes, not engineered functions ([NeurologyLive](https://www.neurologylive.com/view/fda-clears-aruna-bio-exosome-ab126-clinical-trials-neurological-indication))
- **Key distinction from engineered exosome platforms:** Evox Therapeutics (DeliverEX platform) and similar competitors engineer exosomes with surface-targeting ligands and load exogenous cargo (siRNA, ASO, mRNA, gene editors). Evox demonstrated 650-fold improvement in mRNA loading and outperformed LNPs at equal low doses in vivo ([GlobeNewswire, May 2023](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2023/05/12/2667740/0/en/Evox-Therapeutics-Presents-Progress-Across-its-DeliverEX-Platform-Highlighting-Advances-in-Exosome-Mediated-Delivery-of-Genetic-Medicines-Including-Gene-Editing-and-RNA-Therapeutic.html)). AB126 takes the opposite approach: no engineering, relying on the native biology of neural exosomes
- **Advantages over LNPs for CNS delivery:** Exosomes have inherent stability, low immunogenicity, and natural BBB-crossing ability. Standard LNPs distribute primarily to liver, spleen, and lungs due to passive biodistribution; crossing the BBB requires specialized functionalization (e.g., neurotransmitter-derived lipidoids, peptide functionalization). Exosomes carry surface proteins (integrins, tetraspanins, CAMs) that enable active targeting, and their lipid bilayer asymmetry facilitates membrane fusion with target cells ([ACS Nano: Exosomes — Nature's LNPs](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsnano.2c08774))
- **Manufacturing:** Aruna operates in-house GMP production facilities with proprietary purification techniques, established analytical methods, and batch consistency for clinical-grade production. Scalable exosome manufacturing remains one of the field's key challenges ([Pharma's Almanac, Jul 2025](https://www.pharmasalmanac.com/articles/five-exosome-biotechs-to-watch-july-2025))
- **Platform name:** ABEx (Aruna Bio Exosome) platform — combines proprietary neural exosome production with isolation, loading, and targeting methods. Beyond the unmodified AB126, the platform is designed to create enhanced exosomes loaded with small molecules, RNA, oligonucleotides, and antibodies for future pipeline candidates ([Pharma's Almanac, Jul 2025](https://www.pharmasalmanac.com/articles/five-exosome-biotechs-to-watch-july-2025))

### Clinical

**Phase 1b/2a (Acute Ischemic Stroke)** | NCT TBD | N=TBD | Post-thrombectomy patients with poor prognosis
- **Primary endpoint:** Safety, tolerability, and preliminary efficacy of IV AB126
- **Design:** Dose-ascending trial with 3 IV injections at low, medium, and high doses
- **Population:** Acute ischemic stroke patients who underwent thrombectomy with poor prognosis — enriching for severe cases where current standard of care (thrombectomy alone) leaves significant unmet need
- **Status:** IND cleared January 2024; trial initiation planned for H2 2024; as of early 2026, Aruna Bio was presenting at BIO International Convention 2025, suggesting the trial is active or nearing first enrollment
- **Interpretation:** This is a first-in-human study for exosomes in a neurological indication. The acute stroke setting provides a well-defined time window for intervention and measurable outcomes (NIHSS, mRS) within weeks to months. Poor-prognosis post-thrombectomy patients have large residual deficits, making even modest improvements clinically meaningful. However, stroke's acute pathophysiology (ischemia-reperfusion injury, rapid neuronal death) differs substantially from chronic neurodegeneration (PD, ALS), limiting direct translatability of efficacy signals

### Competitive

*No results*

- **Within exosome therapeutics:** Evox Therapeutics is the most direct competitor — their DeliverEX/ExoEdit platform engineers exosomes for targeted cargo loading and CNS delivery. The key technical difference: Evox demonstrated 650-fold mRNA loading improvement and 300-fold shRNA loading improvement, enabling exosomes as programmable delivery vehicles, while AB126 relies on unmodified endogenous cargo. This means Evox can deliver specific RNA therapeutics (e.g., for alpha-synuclein knockdown) while AB126 offers broad anti-inflammatory/neuroprotective effects without target specificity
- **Exosomes vs. receptor-targeted BBB shuttles:** [[trontinemab]] (Roche BrainShuttle, TfR1 transcytosis) and [[pabinafusp-alfa]] (JCR Pharmaceuticals, TfR1-binding fusion) represent the engineered biologic approach to BBB penetration — molecularly defined, quantifiable brain exposure (4-18x improvement for BrainShuttle), but requiring target-specific payloads. Exosomes offer broader biological activity but less molecular precision
- **Exosomes vs. LNPs:** LNPs face fundamental biodistribution challenges for CNS delivery — liver sequestration and minimal BBB penetration without specialized functionalization. Recent advances (neurotransmitter-derived lipidoids, peptide-functionalized LNPs) are addressing this gap, but exosomes retain advantages in immunogenicity and natural BBB tropism
- **Exosomes vs. AAV gene therapy:** AAV9 and AAV-PHP.eB cross the BBB but face immunogenicity, limited re-dosing, and manufacturing constraints. Exosomes can be administered repeatedly without anti-capsid immune responses
- **AB126 as platform vs. therapeutic:** If Aruna Bio can validate the exosome platform in stroke, the greater value may be as a CNS delivery vehicle (loaded with specific drugs) rather than as an unmodified therapeutic — positioning it to compete directly with Evox for partnering deals with pharma

## Analysis

The core question for AB126 is whether an unmodified neural exosome can demonstrate sufficient therapeutic activity to validate the platform. The biological rationale is sound: neural stem cell exosomes carry endogenous anti-inflammatory and neuroprotective cargo, and exosome transcytosis across the BBB is well-documented in the literature. The acute stroke indication is well-chosen — neuroinflammation is a major driver of secondary injury after ischemia-reperfusion, the post-thrombectomy poor-prognosis population has clear unmet need, and the acute time course allows relatively rapid readouts compared to chronic neurodegeneration trials.

**Analytical estimate — probability of Phase 1b/2a meeting primary safety endpoint: 65-70%; probability of showing preliminary efficacy signal: 20-30%.** This is our assessment, not from a published source. The reasoning: exosomes have a favorable safety profile in non-neurological clinical settings (MSC-derived exosomes in oncology, wound healing), and the unmodified nature avoids off-target concerns from engineered payloads, supporting the safety estimate. However, the preliminary efficacy estimate is lower because (1) stroke neuroprotection has a notoriously high preclinical-to-clinical failure rate (>100 neuroprotectants failed in stroke trials), (2) an unmodified exosome delivers a complex and poorly characterized cocktail of molecules rather than a defined therapeutic, making dose-response relationships unpredictable, and (3) the post-thrombectomy poor-prognosis population may have irreversible damage that anti-inflammatory intervention cannot rescue.

**PD relevance:** AB126's method-of-use patent explicitly covers Parkinson's disease, and Aruna Bio lists PD among future indications. The neuroinflammation thesis is relevant to PD — activated microglia, NLRP3 inflammasome activation, and pro-inflammatory cytokines are well-documented in PD pathology. However, PD neuroinflammation is chronic and cumulative, not acute like stroke, raising questions about whether periodic IV exosome dosing can achieve sustained CNS anti-inflammatory effects. The greater PD opportunity may be the ABEx platform as a delivery vehicle: neural exosomes loaded with anti-alpha-synuclein siRNA, GCase enzyme, or LRRK2-targeting ASOs could leverage the natural BBB crossing to deliver PD-specific therapeutics. No PD-specific exosome clinical program exists at any company as of February 2026 — preclinical work has shown exosome-delivered shRNA targeting alpha-synuclein reduced aggregation and dopaminergic neuron death in mouse models, but this remains academic.

**Signal analysis:** Aruna Bio's $33-38M total raise is modest for a clinical-stage biotech, suggesting either capital efficiency or capital constraints. The heavy reliance on NIH grants ($7M from SBIR alone) is characteristic of academic spinouts that have not yet attracted top-tier VC investment. The absence of a pharma partner is notable given the Evox/Lilly precedent ($1.2B deal) — this may reflect AB126's unmodified approach being less attractive to pharma than a programmable delivery platform. Conversely, the strong advisory board (Savitz, Wechsler, Nguyen) and first-mover IND position provide strategic leverage for partnering conversations if early clinical data is positive.
