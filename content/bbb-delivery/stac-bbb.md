# STAC-BBB

## Summary

Sangamo Therapeutics (biotech, SGMO) has developed STAC-BBB, an engineered AAV capsid that achieved 700-fold higher transgene expression in neurons versus benchmark AAV9 in cynomolgus macaques, with CNS enrichment and liver/DRG detargeting -- the strongest IV-to-brain transduction claim in the engineered AAV capsid space as of early 2026. The platform has attracted three major pharma licensees in rapid succession: Genentech/Roche ($50M upfront, up to $1.9B; August 2024), Astellas ($20M upfront, up to $1.3B; December 2024), and Eli Lilly ($18M upfront, up to $1.4B; April 2025). Sangamo's own lead internal program using STAC-BBB is ST-506 for prion disease (CTA expected mid-2026). There is no active PD-specific program, though Sangamo previously developed alpha-synuclein zinc finger repressors (ST-502) under a Biogen partnership that was terminated in 2023. Separately, Lilly/Prevail licensed SIFTER-derived capsids for intrathecal CSF delivery (up to $1.19B). If ST-506 CTA submission and early clinical data demonstrate safe IV-to-brain delivery in humans, STAC-BBB validates as the leading BBB-penetrant AAV capsid and becomes a potential backbone for PD gene therapy programs. If it fails on safety (immunogenicity, hepatotoxicity at human doses) or the 700x NHP result does not translate to primates/humans, the competitive advantage collapses and [[gba1-voyager|Voyager's TRACER/VCAP-102]] and [[cap-003|Capsida]] capsids fill the gap.

## Notes

### Science

- **SIFTER platform (Selecting In vivo For Transduction and Expression of RNA):** Sangamo's proprietary directed-evolution capsid engineering platform that screens tens of millions of unique AAV capsid variants through successive rounds of in vivo selection to identify capsids with superior CNS delivery properties ([Sangamo Delivery Platform](https://www.sangamo.com/science/technology-platforms/delivery-platform/))
- **STAC-BBB capsid performance:** In cynomolgus macaques (NHPs), IV-administered STAC-BBB at clinically relevant doses achieved 700-fold higher transgene expression in neurons versus benchmark AAV9, outperforming all other published neurotropic capsid variants evaluated in the study. Results were dose-dependent and consistent across all NHPs tested ([Sangamo Press Release, Mar 13, 2024](https://investor.sangamo.com/news-releases/news-release-details/sangamo-therapeutics-announces-data-novel-proprietary))
- **Brain coverage:** Widespread transgene expression across brain regions integral to neurodegenerative diseases including substantia nigra, cortex, hippocampus, and other regions relevant to PD, AD, ALS, HD, and prion disease
- **Detargeting:** STAC-BBB demonstrated enriched CNS targeting with de-targeting from liver, dorsal root ganglia (DRG), and other peripheral organs -- addressing the two primary safety concerns for IV AAV delivery
- **Zinc finger technology (ZF-TRs):** Sangamo's core competency. Zinc finger transcriptional regulators are compact, human-derived proteins composed of a DNA-binding zinc finger array fused to a functional domain. Zinc finger repressors (ZFRs) epigenetically silence target genes; zinc finger activators (ZFAs) upregulate them. The compact size of ZF-TRs makes them highly compatible with AAV packaging constraints ([Sangamo ZF Platform](https://www.sangamo.com/science/technology-platforms/zinc-finger-platform/))
- **Combination of capsid + payload:** The unique value proposition is STAC-BBB (delivery) + ZFR (payload) as an integrated system. Capsid-delivered ZFRs targeting prion protein and tau achieved robust, widespread target gene repression in NHP brains -- demonstrating functional cargo delivery, not just transgene expression
- **Second-generation variants:** At ASGCT 2025 (May 2025), Sangamo presented a poster titled "Fitness Maturation of STAC-BBB Yields Second-Generation Capsid Variants with Enhanced Delivery to the Central Nervous System" -- suggesting iterative improvement on the already-strong 700x baseline ([Sangamo ASGCT 2025 Announcement](https://investor.sangamo.com/news-releases/news-release-details/sangamo-therapeutics-present-neurology-pipeline-advances-28th))
- **PD relevance:** Sangamo has published preclinical data showing alpha-synuclein-targeted ZFRs (ST-502) achieved significant SNCA repression in vivo and were well tolerated. The combination of STAC-BBB + SNCA-ZFR is a theoretical PD gene therapy candidate, but no active PD program exists following the Biogen collaboration termination in 2023 ([Sangamo ASGCT 2021 Poster](https://www.sangamo.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/ASGCT-2021_Poster_Zinc_Finger_Protein_Transcription_Factors_Targeting_Alpha-synuclein.pdf))
- **Key open scientific questions:** (1) Will the 700x NHP transduction translate to humans? The NHP-to-human capsid translation gap is the central uncertainty for all engineered AAV platforms. (2) What is the immunogenicity profile of STAC-BBB in humans, particularly in patients with pre-existing anti-AAV antibodies? (3) Is the liver/DRG detargeting sufficient at human therapeutic doses? (4) What is the durability of transgene expression from a single IV dose? (5) Can STAC-BBB be re-dosed, or do anti-capsid antibodies after first dose preclude retreatment?

### Clinical

No clinical trials initiated for any STAC-BBB program as of February 2026.

- Zinc finger repressor targeting prion protein gene (PRNP), delivered via STAC-BBB capsid
- CTA-enabling activities advancing; CTA submission expected as early as mid-2026

- Note: ST-503 uses intrathecal delivery (NOT STAC-BBB); the first clinical validation of STAC-BBB as an IV-to-brain capsid awaits ST-506 or a partner program

**Competitive landscape for BBB-penetrant AAV capsids (IV-to-brain delivery):**

- **[[gba1-voyager|Voyager TRACER/VCAP-102]]:** The most direct competitor. Voyager claims 50-60x CNS transduction vs. AAV9 for TRACER capsids, and VCAP-102 (next-generation) has shown exceptional BBB penetration in marmosets. Voyager has identified ALPL as the BBB receptor mediating VCAP-102 transport. Voyager has more aggregate deal value ($7B+ across Neurocrine, Pfizer, Novartis) and a more specific mechanism story (known receptor), but STAC-BBB's 700x claim exceeds Voyager's published 50-60x. Neither has human data. The critical question is whether Sangamo's 700x and Voyager's claims were measured using comparable methodologies.
- **[[cap-003|Capsida Biotherapeutics]]:** IND cleared for CAP-003 (GBA1-PD) June 2025 -- the first engineered BBB-penetrant AAV capsid to reach the clinic with an IND. Claims >70% neuronal transduction brain-wide and 20-50x liver/DRG detargeting. Capsida is closest to human data and will likely generate the first clinical evidence for or against IV AAV BBB penetration in CNS, making CAP-003 Phase 1 safety data the most important near-term catalyst for the entire engineered AAV capsid field.
- **Affinia Therapeutics:** Developing BBB-penetrant capsids with Forge Biologics manufacturing partnership. Earlier-stage than Capsida/Voyager/Sangamo.
- **Apertura Gene Therapy:** TfR1-targeting CapX capsid. Early-stage.
- **4D Molecular Therapeutics:** A4 platform capsids for CNS delivery. Has clinical programs in ophthalmology but CNS pipeline is earlier.
- **Dyno Therapeutics:** AI-guided capsid engineering; partnered with Novartis, Roche, Sarepta. Broad platform but less specific BBB-penetrant data published.

**Key competitive differentiators for STAC-BBB:**
1. Highest published brain transduction claim (700x vs. AAV9) -- if validated, this is best-in-class
2. Integrated capsid + payload system (STAC-BBB + zinc finger regulators) -- competitors offer capsid only and rely on partners for payload
3. Three separate big pharma validations in 8 months (Aug 2024 - Apr 2025) -- rapid uptake signals platform attractiveness
4. Second-generation STAC-BBB variants already in development (ASGCT 2025) -- iterative improvement pipeline

**Key competitive vulnerabilities:**
1. No IND filed for any STAC-BBB program (Capsida and Voyager are ahead clinically)
2. Severe financial distress ($21M cash, CFO departure, going concern risk) threatens execution
3. History of failed partnerships (Biogen, Novartis, Pfizer, Sanofi all terminated)
4. 700x claim has not been independently replicated or peer-reviewed in a head-to-head comparison with competitor capsids
5. STAC-BBB receptor mechanism not publicly disclosed (Voyager has published ALPL; Capsida has described novel conserved receptors)

## Analysis

STAC-BBB represents an intriguing paradox: potentially the strongest preclinical BBB-penetrant AAV capsid data in the field, housed inside one of the most financially fragile companies in biotech. The 700x transduction improvement over AAV9 in NHPs, combined with liver/DRG detargeting, exceeds published claims from Voyager (50-60x for first-gen TRACER) and is competitive with Capsida's data. Three big pharma companies licensing the technology within 8 months -- Genentech, Astellas, and Lilly -- is meaningful external validation. The Genentech deal is particularly notable because Roche's internal BBB shuttle expertise (trontinemab, the only BBB platform with human AD efficacy data) means they chose STAC-BBB with full knowledge of the competitive landscape.

**Analytical estimate -- probability of STAC-BBB generating a clinical proof-of-concept in any indication by 2030: 25-35%.** This is our assessment, not from a published source. The reasoning:
- Base rate: Preclinical AAV program reaching clinical POC ~20-25%
- Adjustments upward: Best-in-class NHP transduction data (+5%), three independent pharma validations (+5%), zinc finger payload technology is differentiated and compact (+3%), ASGCT 2025 second-gen data suggests continued improvement (+2%)
- Adjustments downward: NHP-to-human capsid translation gap is the central risk for ALL AAV platforms (-8%), Sangamo's severe financial distress threatens ability to execute technology transfer and preclinical obligations (-5%), history of four terminated pharma partnerships raises execution concerns (-3%), no human safety data for STAC-BBB (-3%)
- Net: ~25-35% (wide range reflects high uncertainty)

**Signal analysis:**

The deal structure reveals important information. All three STAC-BBB deals have extremely low upfront/total ratios (1.3-2.6%), meaning partners are paying very little real money for very large theoretical milestones. Compare this to Capsida's AbbVie deal (~30% upfront/total) or MeiraGTx/Hologen (46.5%). The low conviction ratio suggests pharma views STAC-BBB as a cheap option worth holding, not a high-probability bet. The $88M in aggregate upfronts -- while a lifeline for Sangamo -- represents pocket change for Roche, Astellas, and Lilly individually.

The Genentech deal is the most informative: Roche already has the world's leading BBB shuttle (trontinemab) and deep AAV expertise, yet chose to license STAC-BBB specifically for tau repression + a second neurology target. This suggests Roche sees IV AAV + ZFR as complementary to antibody-based BBB shuttles, not competitive. Different modalities for different targets: antibodies for protein clearance (amyloid, alpha-synuclein), gene therapy for epigenetic silencing (tau, prion, potentially SNCA). This framing has important implications for PD -- if Roche's prasinezumab Phase 3 fails (antibody-based alpha-synuclein clearance), the door opens for ZFR-based SNCA silencing delivered via STAC-BBB.

**The PD gap is notable.** Sangamo has published preclinical data on alpha-synuclein ZFRs (ST-502) and possesses the delivery capsid (STAC-BBB) that could enable an IV gene therapy for synucleinopathies. But ST-502 was abandoned after Biogen terminated, and none of the three STAC-BBB partners have publicly disclosed PD as a target. The absence of a PD program despite having both the capsid and the payload suggests either: (a) Sangamo lacks the financial resources to advance an internal PD program, (b) partners are pursuing PD quietly under undisclosed target slots, or (c) the competitive landscape for PD gene therapy (Capsida CAP-003, Voyager/Neurocrine GBA1, Prevail PR001) is too crowded for a late entrant.

**Decision tree:**
- If ST-506 (prion) CTA submits mid-2026 and early clinical data shows safe IV-to-brain delivery with prion protein repression: STAC-BBB validates as the leading BBB-penetrant AAV capsid, partner programs accelerate, Sangamo's survival probability increases dramatically, and PD application becomes viable
- If ST-506 fails on safety or human translation: STAC-BBB joins the list of impressive preclinical platforms that did not survive the NHP-to-human gap, partner milestones do not materialize, and Sangamo's financial position becomes untenable
- Either outcome informs the broader competitive picture: [[gba1-voyager|Voyager]] and [[cap-003|Capsida]] are both pursuing the same NHP-to-human translation with their own capsids, and the first platform to generate positive human data will likely capture the majority of future CNS gene therapy partnerships

**The going-concern risk cannot be overstated.** With $21M cash, ~$35M quarterly burn, a recently departed CFO, and a dilutive $25M offering at $0.47/share, Sangamo may not survive to see its own capsid enter the clinic. The platform's value is increasingly being captured by partners (Roche, Lilly, Astellas) rather than by Sangamo shareholders. If Sangamo runs out of cash and must restructure, the IP could be acquired at distressed valuations -- presenting a potential opportunity for acquirers who believe in the capsid but not in Sangamo's ability to operate independently.
