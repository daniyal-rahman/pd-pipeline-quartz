# PD Revenue Landscape: Who's Making Money and How

The PD therapeutics market is a ~$6B global industry structured around a 500× price stratification: generic levodopa ($108–530/yr) anchors the volume foundation while a handful of specialty products capture most of the dollar economics at 100–1,000× that price. Three companies — AbbVie, Acadia, and Supernus — account for the majority of identifiable branded revenue from primary filings. AbbVie is executing a deliberate franchise consolidation — Duodopa declining, VYALEV ramping, tavapadon pending — that could make it the first PD company with a >$1B single-franchise year in 2026, with management guiding toward a $5B peak across all three assets.

---

## Market Overview

**Global market:** ~$5.9–6.6B in 2024 for therapeutics-focused estimates (treatment-only consensus across seven independent research firms: [Grand View $5.65B](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/parkinsons-disease-treatment-market), [DataM $5.92B](https://www.openpr.com/news/4303876/parkinson-s-disease-therapeutics-market-to-reach-usd-12-83), [GM Insights $6.2B](https://www.gminsights.com/industry-analysis/parkinsons-disease-therapeutics-market), [Precedence Research $6.59B](https://www.precedenceresearch.com/parkinsons-disease-therapeutics-market), [Fortune Business Insights $7.17B](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/industry-reports/parkinson-s-disease-drugs-market-100655)). The "$6–7B" range commonly cited is directionally accurate; treatment-only consensus centers at ~$6.1B. Upper estimates ($6.6B+) include diagnostics or use a broader drug-market definition.

**US branded revenue:** ~$1.7–2.1B is the best-supported bottom-up estimate. Three products verified directly from primary filings (Nuplazid $609M + Duopa US $96M + Gocovri $131M = $836M confirmed); adding plausible-but-unverified estimates for Rytary, Neupro US, Apokyn, and the long tail of branded dopamine agonists and COMT/MAO-B inhibitors likely reaches $1.7–2.1B. The "$2.3B" figure circulates but cannot be derived from the six commonly cited products alone — the math requires the full branded universe including products most analyses omit.

**Key structural insight — the market is bimodal.** Generic levodopa dominates by prescription volume (estimated 50–65% of all PD scripts; see Generic Levodopa section) at near-zero revenue per patient. Specialty products — novel mechanisms (pimavanserin), advanced delivery formulations (Duopa, VYALEV), and high-price reformulations (Gocovri, Rytary) — generate most dollar value at 50–1,000× the generic price. No disease-modifying therapy has reached market. The commercial landscape is entirely symptomatic.

## The 500× Pricing Spread

The extremes of PD drug pricing span three orders of magnitude. This is not incidental — it is the core structural feature of the market.

**Generic carbidopa-levodopa cost:**
- GoodRx retail (no coupon): ~$44/30 tablets → **~$528/yr** at standard TID dosing ([GoodRx](https://www.goodrx.com/carbidopa-levodopa))
- GoodRx with discount coupon: as low as $9/30 tablets → **~$108/yr** ([GoodRx](https://www.goodrx.com/carbidopa-levodopa))
- ClinCalc 2023 data: average patient out-of-pocket ~$9.68/fill → **~$116/yr OOP** ([ClinCalc](https://clincalc.com/DrugStats/Drugs/CarbidopaLevodopa))
- The range $108–530/yr reflects pharmacy retail; Medicare Part D patients may pay well under $100/yr under the 2025 $2,000 annual OOP cap

**Specialty product WAC pricing:**
- [[nuplazid|Nuplazid]] WAC: **$4,995–5,009/30 capsules → ~$60K/yr WAC** ([GoodRx](https://www.goodrx.com/nuplazid); [Drugs.com](https://www.drugs.com/price-guide/nuplazid))
- [[vyalev|VYALEV]]: **$62,023/yr** per CADTH (Canada) pharmacoeconomic review (~1 vial/day standard dosing) ([NCBI/CADTH](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK595155/table/t03/)). US WAC not publicly disclosed.

**The 500× calculation:**

| Generic cost basis | Nuplazid WAC | Ratio |
|---|---|---|
| $108/yr (GoodRx coupon floor) | $60,000/yr | **556×** |
| ~$120/yr (midpoint) | $60,000/yr | **500×** |
| $528/yr (retail, no coupon) | $60,000/yr | 114× |

The 500× ratio is internally consistent using ~$120/yr as the generic midpoint. These are WAC-to-WAC list price comparisons — net realized prices (after PBM rebates, which are material for Nuplazid) compress the ratio somewhat, but the structural gap is real.

**What this means:** The market's willingness to pay $60K+/yr exists, but only for solving problems that levodopa can't. Nuplazid's monopoly on PD psychosis (a condition where dopamine D2-blocking antipsychotics are contraindicated) justifies the pricing. VYALEV's $62K/yr justifies itself against the alternative of PEG-J surgery for Duopa. The pricing spread is not arbitrary — it maps precisely to the severity of unmet need and the degree of competitive insulation at each tier.

## AbbVie's PD Consolidation

AbbVie is the only company executing a coordinated multi-product PD franchise strategy. Three assets, three time horizons.

**Current franchise state (FY2024):**

| Product | FY2024 Revenue | Trajectory | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[duopa\|Duodopa/Duopa]] | $447M total ($96M US) | Declining: -4.7% YoY from $468M in 2023 | Legacy; cash cow being cannibalized |
| [[vyalev\|VYALEV]] | ~$20–50M est. (minimal; US launch Oct 2024) | Rapid ramp: FY2025 ~$482M globally; Q4 2025: $183M | Growth engine |
| Tavapadon | $0 | NDA filed Sep 26, 2025; PDUFA mid-to-late 2026 | Pipeline optionality |

**VYALEV's trajectory makes the math work.** The drug ramped from ~$20–50M in 2024 (partial-year US launch; EU only for most of 2024) to $482M globally in FY2025. Q4 2025 alone was $183M. AbbVie management (Q4 2025 earnings, February 4, 2026): ["we now expect Vyalev to achieve blockbuster revenue in 2026"](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/02/04/abbvie-abbv-q4-2025-earnings-call-transcript/) — defined as $1B+. Combined with Duodopa at ~$420–430M (declining ~5%/yr), the combined franchise reaches $1.4–1.5B in 2026 before tavapadon contributes anything.

**Tavapadon's significance is structural, not 2026 revenue.** Filed as ["the first novel drug treatment for Parkinson's disease submitted to the FDA in more than half a century"](https://news.abbvie.com/2025-09-26-AbbVie-Submits-New-Drug-Application-to-U-S-FDA-for-Tavapadon-for-the-Treatment-of-Parkinsons-Disease), it is a D1/D5-selective partial agonist — a different receptor pharmacology than anything currently approved. If approved in 2026, it generates minimal 2026 revenue (launch ramp), but it gives AbbVie coverage across the patient journey: tavapadon for early/moderate PD, VYALEV for advanced, Duodopa for the surgically managed transition. AbbVie management guidance: **"peak sales in excess of $5B"** across all three assets.

**Duodopa's managed decline** is the funding mechanism. At $447M in 2024, declining ~5%/yr, it still generates substantial cash flow through ~2028, by which time VYALEV should be at or near blockbuster territory. The transition mirrors AbbVie's Humira-to-Skyrizi/Rinvoq playbook in immunology: own both legacy and successor, control prescriber relationships through the franchise transition.

**What this means for competitors:** AbbVie is consolidating the advanced PD infusion market (Duodopa's installed base converting to VYALEV) while simultaneously attempting to enter the early/moderate PD dopamine agonist market (tavapadon). No other company has this breadth. The risk is NeuroDerm/Mitsubishi Tanabe's ND0612 (SC levodopa/carbidopa, NDA resubmitted May 2025, PDUFA Q4 2025) — if approved, it splits the SC infusion market from day one, compressing VYALEV's peak.

## The Generic Levodopa Invisible Economy

The largest single component of the PD drug market is invisible in company financial reports. No public company discloses generic carbidopa-levodopa revenue as a segment.

**Why it's invisible:** Teva buries it in $16.5B generics revenue; Amneal puts it in "Affordable Medicines" alongside hundreds of other generics; Viatris and Sun Pharma similarly. At ~$10–30 net cost per fill, even 65M+ annual US prescriptions generates revenue that's structurally below any SEC disclosure materiality threshold for large manufacturers. ([Amneal FY2024 PR](https://investors.amneal.com/news/press-releases/press-release-details/2025/Amneal-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2024-Financial-Results/default.aspx); [Teva 10-K generic segment](https://www.tevausa.com/our-products/tevagenerics/teva-generics-catalog/vision-product-page/carbidopaandlevodopatabletsusp))

**Scale estimate (LOW confidence — no IQVIA/CMS primary data available):**
- US: ~$520M/yr from Grand View Research's model (26.77% of their $1.94B US PD market estimate) ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/parkinsons-disease-treatment-market))
- Global: ~$1.5–2.0B from secondary market research ([Cognitive Market Research](https://www.cognitivemarketresearch.com/levodopa-market-report))
- Prescription volume: "levodopa/carbidopa segment captured 50.93% market share in 2024" ([Precedence Research](https://www.precedenceresearch.com/parkinsons-disease-therapeutics-market)); "12 generic CD/LD products supplying >60% of total levodopa dispensation" ([Market Growth Reports](https://www.marketgrowthreports.com/market-reports/parkinson-disease-drug-market-109272)); best estimate for generic CD/LD as a share of all PD prescriptions: **50–65%** (unverifiable from public sources alone; directional inference only; verified sources confirm 50% is the conservative floor, not the ceiling)

These estimates are directionally plausible but should not be used for precision analysis without IQVIA subscription data or CMS pharmacy claims.

**Why it matters for the pipeline:**
1. Generic levodopa is the universal background therapy. Every PD patient is either on it or will be. The patient flow starts here.
2. The downstream symptomatic pipeline exists to solve levodopa's limitations — motor fluctuations, dyskinesia, psychosis — not to replace it.
3. The $1.5–2.0B generic market is a floor, not a ceiling, for total PD spend. Specialty products are additive, not substitutive.
4. Generic levodopa's existence as cheap, highly effective first-line therapy is the primary reason PD's specialty tier is so structurally defensible: you can only justify $60K/yr if $120/yr is already adequate for the baseline need, and the specialty product addresses something categorically different.

## What This Means for the Pipeline

**Which assets target existing revenue pools vs. unmet needs:**

*Competing for existing dollars (high clinical risk already de-risked by approved products):*
- CREXONT targets Rytary's base directly — same mechanism, same patients, lifecycle management play. Commercial risk is payer friction. Revenue ceiling is Rytary's ~$150–200M peak, not additive.
- ND0612 (NeuroDerm/Mitsubishi Tanabe) competes directly with VYALEV for the SC levodopa infusion market. A two-product SC infusion market caps VYALEV's peak vs. a monopoly scenario; AbbVie's first-mover advantage and sales infrastructure are significant but not insurmountable moats.

*Creating new revenue pools (unmet need + new price point):*
- Nuplazid established that a new indication in an existing patient population (PD psychosis within the larger PD universe) can command $60K/yr pricing with no competitive pressure for 7+ years — the blueprint for next-generation molecules in underserved PD sub-populations.
- VYALEV demonstrates that delivery innovation on a 50-year-old molecule can access $1B+ peak sales if the clinical problem (advanced motor fluctuations without surgical access) is large and the solution is clearly better than the predecessor.

**Where's the next Nuplazid?**

The template: new mechanism, new indication within PD, no D2-related contraindication concern, small enough starting patient population that manufacturers can charge specialty pricing, but large enough that it becomes a $500M+ franchise. Candidates that fit this structural profile:
- Apomorphine continuous infusion (ONAPGO, Supernus, launched April 2025) — different mechanism from levodopa infusion; captures patients who fail or cannot tolerate SC levodopa
- LY03017 (Luye Pharma, dual 5-HT2A/5-HT2C) if it proves differentiated from pimavanserin
- Any approved agent for PD dementia, autonomic dysfunction, or the cognitive/non-motor symptom cluster that currently has no approved treatment and is addressed only off-label

The next Nuplazid is not a better levodopa formulation — the formulation space is crowded (VYALEV, CREXONT, ND0612) and pricing power is constrained by levodopa's commodity generic floor. It's an agent that creates a new clinical definition of unmet need, positions itself as the only approved therapy in that definition, and prices accordingly.

**What would disrupt the current revenue structure:**
- A disease-modifying therapy that demonstrably slows progression would reduce the fraction of patients who develop advanced disease, compressing the advanced-delivery market (VYALEV, Duopa) over a 10–15 year horizon. No DMT is currently close to market.
- A D1/D5-selective agonist (tavapadon, if approved) that delays or reduces the motor fluctuation progression rate would shrink the addressable population for Rytary/CREXONT/VYALEV — but this would require the TEMPO Phase 3 data to show not just efficacy vs. placebo but a demonstrable disease-course modification vs. standard care.
- A non-drug intervention (focused ultrasound, DBS iteration) that costs less and works better for advanced PD motor fluctuations would undermine the SC infusion market. Not near-term given current surgical complexity and cost thresholds.
