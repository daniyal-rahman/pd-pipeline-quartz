## Paywalled News Sources

### BioCentury
- **URL:** https://www.biocentury.com/
- **Example article:** "Swedish CNS play IRLAB finds partner in Ipsen to carry Parkinson's program into Phase III testing"
- **Content type:** In-depth deal analysis, pipeline intelligence
- **Subscription required:** Yes (premium biotech intelligence)

### Endpoints News
- **URL:** https://endpts.com/
- **Content type:** Biotech funding rounds, deal announcements
- **Subscription required:** Premium content requires subscription

### STAT News (Premium)
- **URL:** https://www.statnews.com/
- **Content type:** Some articles freely accessible; premium content paywalled
- **Note:** Some Kenai Therapeutics launch coverage was accessible

### Bloomberg
- **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/
- **Example article:** "Merck KGaA, Valo to Use AI for Parkinson's in $3 Billion Deal"
- **Content type:** Deal financial details, executive quotes
- **Subscription required:** Yes (limited free articles)

## Clinical Trial Databases

### ClinicalTrials.gov

- **Used for:** Trial verification, enrollment data

### Citeline/Pharmaprojects

## Alternative Sources Used

When primary sources were paywalled, the following freely accessible alternatives were used:

1. **SEC filings** (for US public companies) - EDGAR database
2. **Press releases** - GlobeNewswire, PR Newswire, Business Wire
3. **Company websites** - Investor relations sections
4. **Foundation announcements** - MJFF, Parkinson's Foundation
5. **BioPharma Dive** - Generally accessible industry news
6. **Fierce Biotech** - Most articles accessible
7. **Parkinson's News Today** - Patient-focused coverage
8. **ClinicalTrials.gov** - Trial data verification

## Recommendations for Deeper Research

### Subscription Services to Consider
1. **BioCentury** - Best for deal analysis and pipeline intelligence
2. **Evaluate Pharma** - Pipeline valuations and forecasts
3. **GlobalData** - Comprehensive industry databases
4. **PitchBook** - Detailed private company funding data

### Free Resources to Leverage
1. SEC EDGAR filings for US public companies
2. Company press releases and investor presentations
3. MJFF grant announcements
4. ClinicalTrials.gov for trial status
5. PubMed for scientific publications

---

## Data Quality Notes

The funding amounts and deal terms reported in this research are based on publicly disclosed information. Actual deal values may differ due to:

- Undisclosed milestone structures
- Equity components not publicly valued
- Subsequent amendments not announced
- Confidential side agreements

For the most accurate financial data, consider cross-referencing with:
- SEC filings (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K)
- Company annual reports
- Subscription intelligence services
