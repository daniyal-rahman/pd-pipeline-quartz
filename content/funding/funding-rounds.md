## Major Venture Capital Rounds

### Series C Rounds

#### Aspen Neuroscience - $115M Series C (2025)
- **Amount:** $115 million
- **Lead investors:** OrbiMed, ARCH Venture Partners, Frazier Life Sciences, Revelation Partners
- **Notable new investors:** Kite (Gilead subsidiary), Balyasny Asset Management, Cormorant Asset Management
- **Total raised to date:** $340 million
- **Program:** ANPD001 - autologous iPSC-derived dopamine neuron cell therapy
- **Stage:** Phase 1/2 (14 patients dosed as of late 2025)
- **FDA designation:** Fast Track
- **Use of funds:** Power ongoing Phase 1/2 study, scale manufacturing, advance preclinical pipeline
- **Next milestone:** Phase 3 expected to begin 2026
- **Source:** [BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/aspen-neuroscience-stem-cell-therapy-parkinsons-series-c/806048/)

---

### Series B Rounds

#### NRG Therapeutics - £50M (~$67M) Series B (2025)
- **Amount:** £50 million (~$67 million USD)
- **Investors:** Merck KGaA Ventures, Novartis Venture Fund, SV Health Investors' Dementia Discovery Fund, Omega Funds
- **Previous funding:** £16 million Series A (2022) led by Omega Funds
- **Program:** NRG-5051 - oral small molecule mPTP inhibitor
- **Mechanism:** Blocks mitochondrial permeability transition pore (mPTP) to protect mitochondria from toxic proteins
- **Target indications:** Parkinson's disease, ALS
- **Use of funds:** Advance NRG-5051 into clinical trials (expected 2026)
- **Notable:** Supported by Parkinson's Virtual Biotech initiative
- **Source:** [BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/nrg-therapeutics-series-b-parkinsons-als-mitochondria-mptp/759403/)

---

### Series A Rounds

#### Kenai Therapeutics - $82M Series A (February 2024)
- **Amount:** $82 million
- **Lead investors:** Alaska Permanent Fund Corporation, Cure Ventures, The Column Group
- **Additional investors:** Euclidean Capital, Saisei Ventures
- **Total raised including grants:** ~$90 million
- **Program:** RNDP-001 - allogeneic iPSC-derived dopamine progenitor cell therapy
- **Target:** Idiopathic and inherited forms of Parkinson's disease
- **Stage:** Phase 1 REPLACE trial (first patient dosed December 2025)
- **FDA designation:** Fast Track, IND cleared
- **Use of funds:** IND submission, complete Phase 1 trials
- **Company emerged from stealth February 2024** (previously known as Ryne Bio)
- **Source:** [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2024/02/29/kenai-therapeutics-parkinsons-disease-stem-cell-therapy/)

### Seed/Early Stage Rounds

#### Endlyz Therapeutics - Seed Round (2025)
- **Amount:** $6.15 million raised to date
- **Key funding:** $2.2 million from Michael J. Fox Foundation (MJFF)
- **Investors:** SV Health Investors' Dementia Discovery Fund (founder), Oxford Science Enterprises, AbbVie Ventures, Parkinson's UK, CD3
- **Program:** Small molecule potentiators of ATP13A2 (PARK9 gene) and ATP10B
- **Mechanism:** Restore lysosomal function via polyamine/lipid transporter modulation
- **Target indications:** Parkinson's disease, potentially ALS
- **Seeking:** $30M Series A to advance lead program into clinic
- **Company launched March 2025 from stealth**
- **Source:** [SV Health Investors](https://svhealthinvestors.com/news/endlyz-therapeutics-launches-and-unveils-its-portfolio-of-programmes-targeting-parkinsons-disease)
