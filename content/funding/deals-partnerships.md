## Mega-Deals (>$1B potential value)

### Merck KGaA - Valo Health ($3B+ Deal, November 2025)
- **Partners:** Merck KGaA (Germany), Valo Health (Flagship Pioneering portfolio)
- **Deal value:** Up to $3 billion in upfront + milestones
- **Additional:** R&D funding + royalties on sales
- **Focus:** AI-driven drug discovery for Parkinson's disease
- **Mechanism:** Use Valo's Opal Computational Platform to identify novel PD targets and develop preclinical candidates
- **Notable:** Valo also received MJFF grant to study NOD2 (inflammation-linked PD target)
- **Stage:** Discovery/preclinical target identification
- **Source:** [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/3b-deal-merck-kgaa-becomes-latest-big-pharma-partner-drug-discoverer-valo-health)

### Novartis - Arrowhead Pharmaceuticals ($2.2B Deal, 2025)
- **Partners:** Novartis, Arrowhead Pharmaceuticals
- **Upfront:** $200 million
- **Milestones:** Up to $2 billion (development, regulatory, commercial)
- **Plus:** Tiered royalties on sales
- **Program:** ARO-SNCA - siRNA targeting alpha-synuclein
- **Target indications:** Parkinson's disease, other synucleinopathies
- **Stage:** Preclinical
- **Expected close:** Second half of 2025
- **Source:** [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/novartis-takes-another-shot-alpha-synuclein-22b-arrowhead-deal)

### Novartis - UCB ($1.65B potential, ongoing)
- **Partners:** Novartis, UCB
- **Upfront:** $150 million
- **Milestones:** Up to $1.5 billion (conditional on development, regulatory, sales)
- **Program:** UCB0599 (minzasolmin) - alpha-synuclein aggregation inhibitor
- **Structure:** Cost/responsibility sharing; UCB commercializes in Europe, Novartis in rest of world
- **Status:** ORCHESTRA study failed December 2024; UCB discontinued development
- **Source:** [BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/novartis-ucb-parkinsons-licensing-deal/610854/)

### Biogen - Denali Therapeutics (~$1B LRRK2 Deal)
- **Partners:** Biogen, Denali Therapeutics
- **Upfront:** $560 million
- **Equity investment:** $465 million
- **Program:** BIIB122 - LRRK2 inhibitor
- **Stage:** Phase 2b LUMA trial ongoing
- **Significance:** Expanded Biogen's LRRK2 portfolio with rights to Denali's drugs
- **Source:** [Michael J. Fox Foundation](https://www.michaeljfox.org/news/news-context-lrrk2-therapies-speeding-forward-after-1b-biotech-deal)

### MeiraGTx - Hologen JV ($430M total, March 2025)
- **Partners:** MeiraGTx, Hologen (generative AI startup)
- **Structure:** Joint venture - Hologen Neuro AI
- **Committed capital:** Up to $230 million from Hologen

### Roche - Neurocrine Biosciences (~$1B+ potential, 2025)

- **Upfront:** $125 million
- **Milestones:** Potentially reaching $1 billion
- **Program:** Undisclosed early-stage Parkinson's compound (NOT prasinezumab)

- **Note:** Prasinezumab remains with Roche/Prothena, advancing to Phase 3
- **Source:** [Licensing Trends 2025](https://www.laboratoriosrubio.com/en/licensing-trends-2025-pharma/)

## Strategic Alliances & Consortia

### LRRK2 Investigative Therapeutics Exchange (LITE)
- **Organizer:** Michael J. Fox Foundation
- **Implementer:** University of Dundee (UK)
- **Duration:** 3-year initial funding period (launched 2024)
- **Participants:** 20+ academic KOLs, 20+ biotech/pharma KOLs, 10+ clinical advisors
- **Member companies:** SciNeuro (joined November 2025), others
- **Purpose:** De-risk LRRK2 therapy development, establish best practices, connect stakeholders
- **Source:** [MJFF](https://www.michaeljfox.org/grant/lrrk2-investigative-therapeutics-exchange-lite)

### Parkinson's Virtual Biotech
- **Partners:** Parkinson's Foundation, Parkinson's UK
- **Committed:** >£30 million to portfolio projects
- **Model:** Investment fund accelerating early-stage treatments to clinical trials
- **2025 portfolio highlights:**
  - NRG Therapeutics (mPTP inhibitor)
  - ASPro-PD/ambroxol (GCase, Phase 3)
  - HER-096 (CDNF pathway, Phase 1b complete)
- **Source:** [Parkinson's Foundation](https://www.parkinson.org/advancing-research/parkinsons-virtual-biotech)

## Failed/Discontinued Deals (2024-2025)

### UCB - Minzasolmin (December 2024)
- **Status:** Development discontinued
- **Reason:** ORCHESTRA Phase 2 study failed primary and secondary endpoints
- **Impact:** Novartis partnership for UCB0599 effectively terminated

### AbbVie - Alpha-Synuclein Partnership (2025)
- **Status:** Partnership terminated
- **Timing:** On cusp of Phase 2
- **Impact:** Thinned the alpha-synuclein antibody field
- **Source:** [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/abbvie-shreds-alpha-synuclein-parkinsons-pact-cusp-phase-2-thinning-field-led-roche)

### Ipsen - IRLAB (Mesdopetam)
- **Original deal (2021):** $28M upfront + $335M milestones
- **Program:** Mesdopetam - D3 receptor antagonist for levodopa-induced dyskinesia
- **Status:** Phase 2b failed (January 2023) - missed primary endpoint
- **Current:** IRLAB exploring secondary endpoint signals; unclear if program continues
- **Source:** [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/irlabs-shares-plummet-60-after-ipsen-partnered-parkinsons-drug-flunks-phase-2-trial)

## Key Observations

1. **Alpha-synuclein remains hot:** Despite UCB/minzasolmin failure, Novartis doubled down with $2.2B Arrowhead deal for siRNA approach

2. **AI entering PD drug discovery:** Merck/Valo deal signals AI platform value for target ID in neurodegeneration

3. **LRRK2 field maturing:** Multiple programs advancing to Phase 2/3 with significant pharma backing

4. **GBA1 crowded space:** BIAL, Biogen, Voyager/Neurocrine, Gain all competing with different mechanisms

5. **Gene/cell therapy advancing:** MeiraGTx, BlueRock, Kenai, Aspen all moving toward pivotal trials

6. **Deal structures favoring biotechs:** Higher upfronts, larger milestone pools as pharma competes for assets
