## Companies Emerging from Stealth

### Kenai Therapeutics (February 2024)
- **Location:** San Diego, CA
- **Founded:** 2022 (as Ryne Bio)
- **Public launch:** February 29, 2024
- **Funding:** $82M Series A
- **Investors:** Alaska Permanent Fund Corporation, Cure Ventures, The Column Group, Euclidean Capital, Saisei Ventures
- **Technology:** iPSC-derived allogeneic neuron replacement cell therapy
- **Lead program:** RNDP-001 - dopamine progenitor cell therapy
- **Target:** Idiopathic and inherited forms of Parkinson's disease
- **Progress:**
  - IND cleared by FDA (2025)
  - Fast Track designation received
  - First patient dosed in Phase 1 REPLACE trial (December 2025)
- **Differentiation:** "Off-the-shelf" allogeneic approach (vs. autologous competitors)
- **Leadership:** Team with experience from prior stem cell companies
- **Source:** [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2024/02/29/kenai-therapeutics-parkinsons-disease-stem-cell-therapy/)

### Endlyz Therapeutics (March 2025)
- **Location:** Belgium/UK
- **Founded:** 2021 by SV Health Investors' Dementia Discovery Fund
- **Public launch:** March 31, 2025
- **Funding:** $6.15M raised (including $2.2M MJFF grant)
- **Seeking:** $30M Series A
- **Investors:** Dementia Discovery Fund, Oxford Science Enterprises, AbbVie Ventures, Parkinson's UK, CD3
- **Technology:** Small molecule modulators of lysosomal function
- **Targets:** ATP13A2 (PARK9 gene) and ATP10B polyamine/lipid transporters
- **Mechanism:** Restore lysosomal recycling to address neurodegeneration
- **Scientific founders:** Prof. Peter Vangheluwe, Prof. Richard Wade-Martins (Oxford), Prof. John Davis (Oxford)
- **CEO:** Dr. Joanna Wolak
- **Differentiation:** Novel target class (lysosomal transporters vs. GCase, LRRK2)
- **Validation:** Loss-of-function mutations in ATP13A2 cause early-onset Parkinson's
- **Source:** [Endlyz](https://www.endlyz.com/news/endlyz-therapeutics-launches-and-unveils-its-portfolio-of-programmes-targeting-parkinsons-disease)

## Established Companies Entering/Re-entering PD Space

### Valo Health (via Merck KGaA partnership, November 2025)
- **Background:** Flagship Pioneering portfolio company, AI drug discovery platform
- **PD entry:** $3B deal with Merck KGaA to identify novel PD targets
- **Technology:** Opal Computational Platform - AI for target identification and drug design
- **Specific focus:** NOD2 (inflammation-linked PD target) - received MJFF grant
- **Approach:** Use human biology datasets to identify druggable targets, rapidly develop preclinical candidates
- **Significance:** Major AI player entering neurodegeneration, could accelerate target-to-clinic timelines
- **Source:** [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-11-20/merck-kgaa-valo-to-use-ai-for-parkinson-s-in-3-billion-deal)

### Hologen (via MeiraGTx JV, March 2025)
- **Background:** London-based generative AI startup
- **PD entry:** $230M commitment to Hologen Neuro AI joint venture with MeiraGTx
- **Focus:** Apply generative AI to gene therapy clinical trials for PD
- **Lead asset:** AAV-GAD gene therapy advancing to Phase 3
- **Significance:** AI company bringing capital and technology to PD gene therapy space
- **Source:** [MedCity News](https://medcitynews.com/2025/03/gene-therapy-meiragtx-parkinsons-generative-ai-to-hologen-joint-venture-mgtx/)

### Kite Pharma (Gilead) - Investment in Aspen Neuroscience (2025)
- **Background:** Cell therapy leader (CAR-T)
- **PD entry:** Notable new investor in Aspen Neuroscience's $115M Series C
- **Significance:** Signals Kite exploring cell therapy beyond oncology into neurodegeneration
- **Potential implications:** Could bring manufacturing expertise to iPSC-derived therapies
- **Source:** [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/aspen-enriches-parkinsons-cell-therapy-trial-kite-backed-115m-series-c)

## Recently Funded Preclinical/Early Companies

### Vincere Biosciences
- **Location:** USA
- **Funding:** $5M MJFF grant (November 2025); previously $8M+ from NIH and MJFF
- **Investors:** Draper Associates, Beiersdorf
- **Technology:** USP30 inhibitors to promote mitophagy
- **Lead program:** VB-23 - first-in-class USP30 inhibitor
- **Mechanism:** Enhance mitochondrial quality control by removing damaged mitochondria
- **Stage:** IND-enabling studies; Phase 1 planned 2026
- **Differentiation:** Novel target (USP30) addressing mitochondrial dysfunction
- **Source:** [Vincere Bio](https://vincerebio.com/)

### SciNeuro Pharmaceuticals
- **Location:** China/USA
- **Founded:** Recent years, backed by ARCH Venture Partners
- **Funding:** $53M equity (December 2025) + $5M MJFF grant
- **Lead PD program:** SNP614 - next-gen antisense oligonucleotide targeting LRRK2
- **Mechanism:** mRNA knockdown to reduce LRRK2 levels
- **Stage:** Preclinical, advancing toward IND
- **Other programs:** Lp-PLA2 inhibitor (Phase 2), beta amyloid
- **Differentiation:** ASO approach (vs. small molecule LRRK2 inhibitors)
- **Joined:** MJFF LITE consortium (November 2025)
- **Source:** [SciNeuro](https://scineuro.com/)

### Stealth BioTherapeutics
- **Background:** Mitochondria-focused biotech with FDA-approved product (FORZINITY for Barth syndrome, September 2025)
- **PD program:** SBT-272 - mitochondria-targeted peptide
- **Funding:** MJFF grant for PD evaluation
- **Mechanism:** Target mitochondria to provide neuroprotection
- **Collaborator:** Dr. Laurie Sanders, Duke University
- **Stage:** Preclinical (2-year research study)
- **Source:** [Stealth BioTherapeutics](https://stealthbt.com/)

## Companies with New/Expanded PD Focus

### AC Immune (expanded PD focus, 2025)
- **Background:** Swiss biotech with Alzheimer's programs
- **PD program:** ACI-7104.056 - alpha-synuclein vaccine
- **Stage:** Phase 2 VacSYn trial (positive interim data December 2025)
- **Results:** First evidence that active immunotherapy could slow PD progression
- **Funded into Q3 2027** (without milestone payments)
- **Significance:** Represents expansion of anti-aggregation vaccine approach from Alzheimer's to PD
- **Source:** [AC Immune](https://ir.acimmune.com/news-releases/news-release-details/ac-immune-positive-interim-phase-2-data-aci-7104056-support)

### Herantis Pharma (advancing to Phase 2, 2025)
- **Background:** Finnish clinical-stage biotech
- **PD program:** HER-096 - small peptide CDNF mimetic
- **Stage:** Phase 1b completed (October 2025); Phase 2 planned 2026
- **Results:** All primary/secondary endpoints met; BBB penetration confirmed
- **Funded by:** MJFF, Parkinson's UK
- **Mechanism:** Multimodal - UPR modulation, alpha-synuclein reduction, anti-inflammation
- **Differentiation:** Novel neurotrophic factor approach via subcutaneous peptide
- **Source:** [Herantis Pharma](https://herantis.com/)

## Key Themes in New Entrants

1. **Cell therapy acceleration:** Multiple iPSC-derived therapies entering/advancing in clinical trials (Kenai, Aspen, BlueRock)

2. **Novel targets beyond LRRK2/GBA1:** ATP13A2 (Endlyz), USP30 (Vincere), mPTP (NRG) gaining traction

3. **AI integration:** Valo, Hologen bringing AI/computational approaches to PD drug discovery

4. **Imaging enabling trials:** SynuSight's alpha-synuclein PET tracer could transform patient selection

5. **Neurotrophic approaches:** Herantis' CDNF mimetic represents underexplored mechanism

6. **Geographic diversification:** Chinese biotechs (SynuSight, ABLi) emerging as players

7. **Pharma venture activity:** AbbVie Ventures, Merck KGaA Ventures actively investing in early-stage PD companies
