# Michael J. Fox Foundation Funded Research and Companies

#claude


**Last Updated:** January 2026

**IMPORTANT CAVEAT:** MJFF grants often fund early-stage research. The Therapeutics Pipeline Program specifically targets industry and industry-academia collaborations with more advanced programs, making these the most relevant for pipeline tracking.

---

## Overview

The Michael J. Fox Foundation (MJFF) is the largest non-profit funder of Parkinson's research globally. In 2024-2025 alone, MJFF awarded over $135M across multiple funding rounds.

---

## Therapeutics Pipeline Program - Company Grants (Most Relevant)

The Therapeutics Pipeline Program is only open to industry and industry-academia collaborations. Awards range from $250,000 for smaller programs to upwards of $2M+ for larger multi-stage preclinical and clinical programs.

### Major Company Grants (2024-2025)

#### Vincere Biosciences (Cambridge, MA) - $5M (November 2025)
- **Target:** USP30 (deubiquitinating enzyme)
- **Mechanism:** Small molecule USP30 inhibitor to restore mitophagy
- **Stage:** IND-enabling studies toward 2026 clinical trial
- **Use of Funds:** Pharmacology, toxicology, regulatory workstreams, biomarker development
- **Previous MJFF Support:** 2019, 2022 preclinical work
- **Total MJFF Funding:** Over $8M combined

#### Booster Therapeutics (Berlin, Germany) - $5M (November 2025)
- **Target:** 20S Proteasome activation
- **Mechanism:** Small molecule proteasome activators (DGRADX platform)
- **Stage:** Advancing toward Phase 1 first-in-human studies
- **Approach:** Activate cellular quality control to degrade misfolded proteins
- **Founded by:** Dr. Diogo Feleciano, Prof. Dr. Darci Trader, Apollo Health Ventures
- **Investors:** Novo Holdings support

#### Lys Therapeutics (France) - $5M (September 2025)
- **Target:** Immunotherapy approach
- **Product:** LYS241 (first-in-class monoclonal antibody)
- **Stage:** Completing preclinical, launching first-in-human trials
- **Use of Funds:** Preclinical completion and Phase 1 trial launch

#### SciNeuro Pharmaceuticals - $5M (November 2025)
- **Target:** LRRK2 (antisense oligonucleotide approach)
- **Product:** SNP614 (next-generation ASO targeting LRRK2)
- **Stage:** Preclinical development acceleration
- **Note:** Different approach from small molecule LRRK2 inhibitors

#### NRG Therapeutics Ltd (UK) - $5M (October 2024)
- **Target:** Mitochondrial permeability transition pore (mPTP)
- **Product:** NRG5051 (first-in-class oral mPTP inhibitor)
- **Stage:** IND-enabling studies, targeting first-in-human in 2025
- **September 2025 Update:** Raised funding to take drug into clinical trials

#### Montara Therapeutics - $3.3M (May 2025)
- **Target:** LRRK2 (brain-selective approach)
- **Platform:** BrainOnly platform for CNS-selective delivery
- **Goal:** Next-generation brain-selective LRRK2 inhibitor avoiding peripheral toxicity

#### SandboxAQ - $25M (October 2024)
- **Program:** LRRK2 Investigative Therapeutics Exchange (LITE)
- **Focus:** AI-driven drug discovery for LRRK2-targeting therapies
- **Also:** Development of LRRK2-relevant clinical biomarkers

## Targets to Therapies (T2T) Initiative - $7.5M Initial Grants (December 2025)

A major target validation effort evaluating 280+ nominated targets and prioritizing 21 for near-term validation.

### First Funded Targets (5 high-potential targets across 3 teams):

| Target | Type | Mechanism |
|--------|------|-----------|
| NOD2 | Gene | Inflammatory activity regulation |
| TMEM175 | Gene | Cellular recycling/lysosomal function |
| ATP13A2 | Gene | Lysosomal function |
| TRPML1 | Protein | Toxic protein clearance |
| OGA | Protein | Protein regulation |

### T2T Target Explorer Platform
- 59 high-potential target profiles publicly released
- Digital open-science resource with curated evidence and validation plans
