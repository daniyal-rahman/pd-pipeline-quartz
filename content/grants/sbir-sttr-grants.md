**Last Updated:** January 2026

**IMPORTANT CAVEAT:** SBIR/STTR grants represent early-stage company research. Legislative authority for the NIH SBIR/STTR programs expired on October 1, 2025, which has disrupted ongoing funding. The grants below were funded prior to this expiration.

---

## Program Status

As of October 2025, NIH is not accepting new SBIR/STTR applications because legislative authority for the programs expired. Active awardees should contact their Program Officer for guidance.

---

## NINDS SBIR/STTR Programs (Parkinson's-Relevant)

The National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke (NINDS) has been a primary funder of Parkinson's disease small business research.

### Funding Mechanisms

| Mechanism | Purpose | Typical Award |
|-----------|---------|---------------|
| R43 (Phase I) | Establish technical merit and feasibility | Up to $314,363 |
| R44 (Phase II) | Continue R&D toward commercialization | Up to $2,095,748 |
| Phase IIB | Bridge "Valley of Death" to clinical development | Larger awards |

### Key NINDS Programs (2023-2024)

1. **PAR-23-311**: Exploratory Clinical Trials for Small Business
   - Supports trials evaluating drugs, biologics, devices, or diagnostics
   - Explicitly mentions Parkinson's Disease as a priority area

2. **PAR-24-222**: Phase IIB Pre-Clinical Research Renewals
   - Designed to help projects reach commercialization stage
   - Addresses the funding gap before clinical trials

3. **PAR-24-221**: Phase IIB Clinical Trials/Research Renewals
   - Supports Phase 1/2 clinical studies
   - First-in-human studies for neurological therapeutics

## Known SBIR/STTR Companies in Parkinson's Space

### Vincere Biosciences (Cambridge, MA)
- **Focus:** USP30 inhibitors for mitophagy restoration
- **Stage:** IND-enabling studies (as of 2025)
- **Funding:** Over $8M in NIH and MJFF grant funding combined
- **NIA Showcase Company**
- **Note:** Also received $5M MJFF Therapeutics Pipeline grant (2025)

### Neurodon, LLC
- **Focus:** ER stress-targeting small molecules
- **Targets:** Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, ALS, diabetes
- **Funding:** NIA SBIR support
- **Platform:** Novel discovery platform for disease-modifying medicines

### Creative Bio-Peptides, Inc.
- **Focus:** Chemokine receptor antagonist peptides
- **Target:** Synapse protection in neurodegeneration
- **Funding:** NIA SBIR grant

### Cognition Therapeutics
- **Focus:** CT1812 for neurodegeneration
- **Achievement:** FDA Fast Track designation (2017) via SBIR funding
- **Funding:** $1M Fast Track SBIR award
- **Note:** Primarily focused on Alzheimer's, but platform relevant to PD

## NIA Small Business Funding for Neurodegeneration

The National Institute on Aging provides over $140M annually in non-dilutive funding through SBIR/STTR programs, with significant focus on Alzheimer's and related dementias (which can include Parkinson's-related dementia).

### AD/ADRD-Specific Funding Levels
- **Phase I:** Up to 2 years, awards up to $500,000
- **Phase II:** Up to 3 years, awards up to $2.5M

### Research Priorities Include:
- Novel interventions for neurodegeneration
- Brain connectivity and neuroplasticity
- Drug and non-drug interventions for cognitive decline
- CNS dysfunction treatments

## How to Search for More SBIR/STTR Awards

The NIH Reporter database (reporter.nih.gov) and SBIR.gov awards database allow searching by:
- Activity codes (R43, R44 for SBIR)
- Disease area (Parkinson's disease)
- Fiscal year
- Institution/company name

**Note:** Direct database searches were not accessible during this research due to JavaScript requirements and the program expiration situation.
