# Amneal Pharmaceuticals

Mid-size specialty/generics pharma (NYSE: AMRX) with the deepest branded oral levodopa franchise in the US. Three commercialized PD products: **[[rytary|Rytary]]** (IPX066, ER carbidopa/levodopa, approved 2015, LOE Q3 2025), **[[crexont|CREXONT]]** (IPX203, next-gen mucoadhesive ER CD/LD, approved August 2024, launched September 2024), and **Ongentys** (opicapone, COMT inhibitor adjunct, acquired from BIAL). CREXONT is the designated Rytary successor; 2025 guidance ~$50M (February 2025) with JPMorgan January 2026 suggesting possible upward revision. Non-PD portfolio includes a large generics division ("Affordable Medicines") — generic carbidopa/levodopa is part of this segment but not separately reported.
