# Bristol-Myers Squibb

## Overview

Bristol-Myers Squibb (NYSE: BMY) is a global biopharmaceutical company headquartered in Princeton, NJ, with a market cap of ~$114B (early 2026). BMS has re-entered neuroscience R&D with a focus on neurodegeneration, including Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease, and neuropsychiatry. Key neuroscience programs include Cobenfy (KarXT, approved for schizophrenia), an anti-MTBR-Tau antibody in Phase 2 for AD, and the BioArctic BAN1503/BAN2803 anti-PyroGlu-amyloid-beta program licensed in December 2024. BMS also has an ongoing strategic neuroscience collaboration with Evotec (since 2016) focused on disease-modifying therapies for neurodegeneration. [Source: BMS neuroscience page](https://www.bms.com/researchers-and-partners/areas-of-focus/our-research-in-neuroscience.html)

## PD Pipeline Relevance

BMS does not currently have disclosed Parkinson's disease clinical assets, but the company's neuroscience strategy page lists PD as a focus area, including "cell therapies for CNS neuron replacement for conditions such as Parkinson's disease." The BioArctic BAN2803 deal is primarily Alzheimer's-focused (anti-amyloid-beta), but the BrainTransporter platform it validates has potential applications to alpha-synuclein antibodies in PD. [Source: BMS areas of interest](https://www.bms.com/business-development/science-and-technology-areas-of-interest.html)
