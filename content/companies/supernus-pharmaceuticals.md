# Supernus Pharmaceuticals

Mid-cap CNS-focused biotech with the broadest PD symptomatic portfolio among non-big-pharma companies. Three commercialized PD products: **Gocovri** (amantadine ER, dyskinesia/OFF), **APOKYN** (apomorphine SC injection, acute OFF rescue), and **ONAPGO** (apomorphine SC infusion pump, advanced PD motor fluctuations — launched April 2025). Gocovri acquired via $400M Adamas Pharmaceuticals buyout (closed November 2021); APOKYN/ONAPGO acquired from US WorldMeds in 2020. Non-PD portfolio includes Qelbree (ADHD), Oxtellar XR and Trokendi XR (epilepsy). FY2024 Gocovri net sales: $130.8M; growing mid-teens.
