# BioArctic

## Overview

BioArctic is a Swedish biotech (Nasdaq Stockholm Large Cap, BIOA-B) founded in 2003 by Professor Lars Lannfelt based on his discovery of the Arctic mutation in Alzheimer's disease at Uppsala University. The company originated lecanemab (Leqembi), the first approved drug shown to slow AD progression, through a collaboration with Eisai dating to 2005. Lecanemab royalties (~SEK 117M/quarter) provide substantial non-dilutive funding. BioArctic's proprietary BrainTransporter platform (TfR-mediated BBB shuttle) has attracted three pharma partnerships: BMS ($1.35B total biobucks), Novartis ($802M), and Eisai (BAN2802 research evaluation). Market cap ~SEK 29.5B (~$2.7B) as of early 2026; company expected to reach profitability in 2025.

## Platform: BrainTransporter

BioArctic's BrainTransporter platform uses transferrin receptor (TfR)-mediated transcytosis to shuttle antibodies across the BBB, achieving up to 70x higher brain exposure in preclinical models without hematological toxicity. Three pharma deals validate the platform:
- **BMS** (Dec 2024): BAN1503/BAN2803 anti-PyroGlu-Abeta -- $100M upfront, $1.35B total
- **Novartis** (Aug 2025): Undisclosed neurodegeneration target -- $30M upfront, $802M total
- **Eisai** (Apr 2024): BAN2802 research evaluation
