# InSightec

## Overview

InSightec is an Israeli medical device company that developed and commercializes the [[bbb-delivery/exablate-neuro|Exablate]] platform for MRI-guided focused ultrasound (MRgFUS). Founded in 1999 by Kobi Vortman and Oded Tamir, the company is headquartered in Tirat Carmel, Israel with operations in Miami, FL. InSightec's Exablate Neuro system is FDA-approved for thermal ablation in essential tremor (2016) and tremor-dominant Parkinson's disease (2018), and is in clinical development for non-thermal blood-brain barrier opening in neuro-oncology (GBM), Alzheimer's disease, and Parkinson's disease. In December 2025, InSightec spun out Lotus Neuro to advance therapeutic BBB-opening programs while InSightec continues to develop the core device platform.
