# Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai

## Overview

The Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai is a major academic medical center in New York City. It houses the Friedman Brain Institute, one of the top neuroscience research institutes in the US, founded and directed by Eric J. Nestler (2008-2025) and focused on neurological and psychiatric disease mechanisms. Mount Sinai does not have PD-specific drug candidates in its clinical pipeline, but the institution's platform technologies -- particularly the MK16 BLNP brain-targeted lipid nanoparticle system developed by Yizhou Dong's lab -- have direct relevance to PD drug delivery ([Mount Sinai Newsroom, Feb 2025](https://www.mountsinai.org/about/newsroom/2025/new-lipid-nanoparticle-platform-delivers-mrna-to-the-brain-through-the-blood-brain-barrier)).

## Key PD-Relevant Programs

### MK16 BLNP (Brain-Targeted LNP)
- **Asset Note:** [[bbb-delivery/mk16-blnp]]
- **Stage:** Preclinical
- **PIs:** Yizhou Dong, PhD (nanomedicine); Eric J. Nestler, MD, PhD (neuroscience)
- **Relevance:** IV-to-brain mRNA delivery platform; 8.3-fold improvement over FDA-approved LNPs in mouse brain delivery; potential applications in GBA1 mRNA replacement, GDNF delivery, SNCA silencing, and CRISPR-based gene editing for PD
- **Publication:** *Nature Materials* (Feb 2025). DOI: [10.1038/s41563-024-02114-5](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41563-024-02114-5)

## Key Researchers

### Yizhou Dong, PhD
- **Title:** Mount Sinai Endowed Professor in Nanomedicine; Icahn Genomics Institute and Marc and Jennifer Lipschultz Precision Immunology Institute; Department of Immunology and Immunotherapy; Department of Oncological Sciences ([Mount Sinai Profile](https://profiles.mountsinai.org/yizhou-dong))
- **Career:** BS and MS in China; postdoc with Robert Langer and Daniel Anderson at MIT; Assistant Professor at Ohio State University (2014); Associate Professor at OSU (2018); joined Mount Sinai as tenured professor (2022) ([Dong Lab](https://donglab.net/members/yizhou-dong-2/))
- **Focus:** Design and development of lipid nanoparticle delivery systems and RNA therapeutics for cancer, genetic disorders, and infectious diseases
- **Output:** >100 papers, >100 patents; AIMBE College of Fellows ([AIMBE](https://aimbe.org/college-of-fellows/COF-7029/))
- **Lab:** [Dong Research Group (Drug Discovery & Delivery Laboratory)](https://donglab.net/)

### Eric J. Nestler, MD, PhD
- **Title:** Anne and Joel Ehrenkranz Dean of the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai (2025-present); formerly Nash Family Professor of Neuroscience; Founding Director of the Friedman Brain Institute (2008-2025); Dean for Academic Affairs ([Mount Sinai Profile](https://profiles.mountsinai.org/eric-j-nestler))
- **Career:** BA, PhD, and MD from Yale University; Chair of Neuroscience at Mount Sinai (2008-2016); elected to National Academy of Sciences (2025), American Academy of Arts and Sciences (2005), National Academy of Medicine (1998) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_J._Nestler))
- **Focus:** Molecular mechanisms of drug addiction and depression; discovered DeltaFosB as molecular switch in reward circuitry; epigenetic regulation of gene expression in brain reward circuits ([Nestler Lab](https://labs.neuroscience.mssm.edu/project/nestler-lab/))
