# JCR Pharmaceuticals

## Overview

JCR Pharmaceuticals Co., Ltd. (TSE: 4552) is a Japanese specialty biopharmaceutical company founded in 1975, headquartered in Ashiya, Hyogo Prefecture. Originally a domestic pharmaceutical manufacturer, JCR has evolved into a global biotech with subsidiaries in the US, Europe, and Brazil, focused on rare and genetic diseases with two proprietary technology platforms:

1. **J-Brain Cargo** -- Anti-TfR1 antibody fusion platform enabling receptor-mediated transcytosis of therapeutic proteins across the blood-brain barrier. Validated by the approval of IZCARGO ([[bbb-delivery/pabinafusp-alfa|pabinafusp alfa]]) in Japan in March 2021 -- the first BBB-shuttled drug approved anywhere in the world.

2. **JUST-AAV** -- Engineered AAV capsid platform using miniaturized antibodies on capsid surfaces for tissue-targeted gene therapy delivery (brain-targeting, muscle-targeting, liver-sparing variants). Licensed to Alexion/AstraZeneca in July 2025 for up to 5 programs ($825M total deal value).

## PD Relevance

JCR does not have direct PD programs, but the J-Brain Cargo platform has been licensed to Alexion/AstraZeneca for an undisclosed neurodegenerative disease target (first research milestone achieved March 2024). The platform's proof-of-concept for TfR1-mediated BBB crossing is foundational for the broader field of CNS drug delivery in neurodegeneration, including PD-relevant programs at Denali (Transport Vehicle), Roche (Brain Shuttle), and BioArctic (BrainTransporter).
