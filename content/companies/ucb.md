## PD Context

Brussels-based neurology/immunology company (EUR 6.4B revenue, 2024). PD is a legacy franchise for UCB built on the 2006 acquisition of Schwarz Pharma (EUR 4.4B). Key PD assets:

- **[[neupro|Neupro]] (rotigotine patch)** — Approved symptomatic DA agonist. Mature/declining product (~EUR 300-400M peak sales). Key '589 patent invalidated in 2023; generic entry from Teva/Viatris expected but timing uncertain. Retains niche for continuous dopaminergic stimulation (nocturnal symptoms, morning akinesia)
- **[[minzasolmin]] (UCB0599)** — Alpha-synuclein small molecule inhibitor. Was the subject of a $1.5B deal with Novartis (terminated 2025 after Phase 2 failure)
- **[[ucb7853]]** — Anti-alpha-synuclein antibody. Phase 1 completed
- **[[glovadalen]]** — Partnered with Bial for advanced PD (opicapone combination)

UCB's strategic focus has shifted from PD toward immunology (Bimzelx for psoriasis/PsA, Rystiggo for myasthenia gravis) and epilepsy (Vimpat, Briviact). PD pipeline is not featured in forward-looking communications, suggesting diminishing investment.
