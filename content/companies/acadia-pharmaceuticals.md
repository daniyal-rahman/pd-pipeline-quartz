# Acadia Pharmaceuticals

CNS-focused biotech with two approved products: **Nuplazid** (pimavanserin) for PD psychosis ($609M FY2024, only FDA-approved PDP drug) and **DAYBUE** (trofinetide) for Rett syndrome ($340M FY2024). Combined 2028 revenue target ~$1.7B. Nine disclosed pipeline programs with seven Phase 2/3 studies initiating 2025-2026. The key near-term catalyst is the Phase 2 RADIANT readout for **remlifanserin** (ACP-204, next-gen 5-HT2A inverse agonist) in Alzheimer's disease psychosis, expected Aug-Oct 2026 — this is the lifecycle management successor to Nuplazid ahead of its ~Q1 2030 patent expiry.

Acadia remains publicly traded on NASDAQ (ACAD). Not acquired. The company was **not** acquired by Noven/Hisamitsu — that was a different transaction in the CNS space.
