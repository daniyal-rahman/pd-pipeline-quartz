# Carthera

French clinical-stage medtech company developing implantable ultrasound devices for transient blood-brain barrier opening. Founded in 2010 by Prof. Alexandre Carpentier (neurosurgeon, Sorbonne University) based on research at the Brain Institute (Institut du Cerveau, ICM) in Paris. CEO Frederic Sottilini. CTO Francois Lacoste (25+ years in ultrasound device development). CCO Carole Desseaux.

## Key Technology

SonoCloud platform: implantable ultrasound devices using Low-Intensity Pulsed Ultrasound (LIPU) at 1 MHz combined with IV microbubbles to transiently and reversibly open the BBB, increasing brain drug concentrations 4-6x. Devices are MRI-compatible and activated via transdermal needle connection without requiring MRI guidance or general anesthesia.

## PD Relevance

No current PD programs. However, the BBB-opening platform is directly applicable to PD therapeutics that struggle with brain penetration (anti-alpha-synuclein antibodies, GCase enzyme replacement, growth factors). The surgical implantation requirement is a barrier for PD expansion -- most relevant scenario is combination with DBS implantation. See [[bbb-delivery/sonocloud-9]] for full analysis.
