# Sangamo Therapeutics

## Overview

Sangamo Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on genomic medicines for neurology, leveraging two core technology platforms: (1) zinc finger protein transcriptional regulators (ZF-TRs) for epigenetic gene regulation, and (2) the SIFTER-derived AAV capsid platform, including the BBB-penetrant STAC-BBB capsid for IV-to-brain gene therapy delivery. Founded in 1995 and headquartered in Brisbane, California. Publicly traded on NASDAQ (SGMO).

**Financial status (as of Q4 2025):** Severely cash-constrained with ~$21M cash, going concern risk, CFO departed February 2026, and recent dilutive $25M offering. Market cap ~$144M. The company's survival depends on partner milestone payments and additional financing.

**PD relevance:** No active PD program. Previously developed ST-502 (alpha-synuclein zinc finger repressor) under a Biogen partnership that was terminated in 2023. The STAC-BBB capsid platform has been licensed to Genentech/Roche, Astellas, and Eli Lilly for CNS applications that could theoretically include PD targets. The combination of STAC-BBB + alpha-synuclein ZFR remains a viable PD gene therapy concept, but no partner has publicly disclosed a PD program.
