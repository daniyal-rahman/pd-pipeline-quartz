# Sironax

## Company Overview

Sironax is a clinical-stage biotechnology company founded in 2017 by Xiaodong Wang (founding Director of NIBS Beijing, co-founder of BeiGene, member of US National Academy of Sciences, Howard Hughes Medical Institute Investigator 1997-2010) and Zhiyuan Zhang (CSO). The company focuses on age-related degenerative diseases by targeting three core biological drivers: dysregulated cell death (necroptosis/apoptosis), uncontrolled inflammation, and disrupted energy homeostasis ([Sironax website](https://www.sironax.com/about-us/)).

Headquartered in Waltham, Massachusetts, with research centers in Beijing and Shanghai, European operations in Basel, Switzerland, and a presence in Sydney, Australia. Led by President and CEO Shefali Agarwal, MD (former CEO of Onxeo, former CMO of Epizyme) ([Sironax Leadership](https://www.sironax.com/about-us/corporate-leaders/)).

Named to the 2025 Endpoints 11 list of the most promising private biotech companies, honored at an awards gala in Boston on September 18, 2025 ([GlobeNewsWire, September 19, 2025](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/09/19/3153269/0/en/Sironax-Named-to-2025-Endpoints-11-List-of-the-Most-Promising-Biotech-Startups.html)).

## Key Relevance to PD Pipeline

Sironax's primary relevance is through the **Brain Delivery Module (BDM)** platform — a proprietary BBB crossing technology licensed to Novartis in July 2025 for up to $175M. See [[bbb-delivery/sironax]] for full analysis.

Secondary relevance: Sironax's RIPK1 inhibitor (SIR9900) and SARM1 inhibitor (SIR2501) programs target neurodegeneration-relevant pathways. RIPK1-mediated necroptosis and SARM1-driven axon degeneration are implicated in PD pathology, though Sironax has not disclosed PD-specific indications for any program.
