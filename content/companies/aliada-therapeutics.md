# Aliada Therapeutics

**Status: Acquired by [[companies/abbvie|AbbVie]] on December 11, 2024 for $1.4B all-cash.**

## Overview

Aliada Therapeutics was founded in 2021 to advance the MODEL (Modular Delivery) platform, a BBB-crossing technology originally created by Janssen/J&J scientists and licensed to Aliada at inception. The platform targets transferrin receptor (TfR1) and CD98 heavy chain (CD98hc) on brain endothelial cells to deliver large molecule therapeutics across the blood-brain barrier via receptor-mediated transcytosis.
