# Aruna Bio

## Overview

Aruna Bio is a privately held clinical-stage biotechnology company developing neural-derived exosome therapeutics for neurological diseases. The company originated as ArunA Biomedical in 2005, licensing neural stem cell technology from co-founder Steven Stice's lab at the University of Georgia. It initially manufactured stem cells for researchers before pivoting to exosome development in 2016, shortening its name to ArunA Bio in 2017-2018. The company operates GMP manufacturing facilities and holds worldwide exclusive rights from the University of Georgia for an extensive patent portfolio covering neural exosome-based therapeutics. ([Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bio-conversation-aruna-bio-ceo-mark-sirgo-and-cso-steve-stice); [NC Biotech](https://www.ncbiotech.org/news/aruna-bio-adds-13m-coffers-research-triangle-presence))

## Platform

The ABEx (Aruna Bio Exosome) platform produces neural-derived exosomes from proprietary human neural stem cells. These exosomes naturally cross the blood-brain barrier via integrin-mediated transcytosis and carry endogenous anti-inflammatory and neuroprotective cargo. The platform is designed for both unmodified exosome therapeutics (like AB126) and engineered exosomes loaded with small molecules, RNA, oligonucleotides, or antibodies. ([Pharma's Almanac, Jul 2025](https://www.pharmasalmanac.com/articles/five-exosome-biotechs-to-watch-july-2025))

## PD Relevance

Aruna Bio does not currently have a PD-specific program, but the platform has explicit PD applicability:
- Method-of-use patent covers Parkinson's disease as a target indication
- Neuroinflammation (AB126's mechanism) is a validated pathological component of PD
- The ABEx platform could serve as a CNS delivery vehicle for PD-specific payloads (anti-alpha-synuclein siRNA, GCase enzyme, LRRK2 ASOs)
- Company publicly lists PD among planned future indications alongside ALS, Alzheimer's, and MS
