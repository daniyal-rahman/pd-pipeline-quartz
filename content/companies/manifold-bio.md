# Manifold Bio

## Overview

Manifold Biotechnologies, Inc. ("Manifold Bio") is a platform therapeutics company founded in 2018 by George Church (Harvard Medical School / Wyss Institute), Gleb Kuznetsov (CEO), Pierce Ogden (CTO), and Shane Lofgren (Head of Strategy). The company combines massively parallel in vivo protein screening via proprietary mCode barcoding technology with AI-guided protein design (the mDesign engine) to discover tissue-targeted biologics. Headquartered at 201 Brookline Avenue, Boston, MA, with approximately 44 employees as of September 2025.

**Key deal:** In November 2025, Roche signed a strategic collaboration worth $55M upfront and over $2B in total milestones plus tiered royalties for Manifold to discover next-generation BBB shuttles for neurological and neurodegenerative diseases. See [[bbb-delivery/manifold-bio]] for full platform analysis.

**Funding:** $5.4M seed (2020, Playground Global) + $40M Series A (2022, Triatomic Capital) + $55M Roche upfront (2025).
