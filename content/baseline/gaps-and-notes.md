## Areas for Deeper Research

### High Priority

1. **Failed trials analysis**: Document why specific programs failed (MOVES-PD venglustat, cinpanemab, exenatide Phase 3) - important for understanding mechanism limitations

2. **GBA precision medicine**: Map which GBA variants each therapy targets (some may only work in specific mutations)

3. **Alpha-synuclein biomarkers**: Understand SAA/PMCA assay use across trials for patient stratification

4. **Combination therapy approaches**: Identify any trials testing mechanism combinations (e.g., alpha-syn + anti-inflammatory)

### Medium Priority

1. **Device-based treatments**: DBS, focused ultrasound, TMS - not covered in this drug pipeline review
2. **Nutrition/supplement interventions**: Thiamine, vitamin D, creatine trials
3. **Exercise/physical therapy trials**: Growing evidence base not captured
4. **Digital therapeutics**: Apps, monitoring devices in development

### Low Priority (for drug pipeline baseline)

1. **Biomarker development programs**: Alpha-syn SAA, NfL, imaging tracers
2. **Diagnostic tools**: PARS, PREDICT-PD cohorts
3. **Natural history studies**: PPMI, Parkinson's Progression Markers Initiative

## Recommendations for Deduplication

When integrating findings from other agents:

1. **Use standardized drug names**: Prefer INN names over company codes where available
2. **Track company name changes**: Cerevel -> AbbVie, NeuroDerm -> Mitsubishi Tanabe
3. **Note trial name mappings**: LUMA/LIGHTHOUSE/BEACON all for BIIB122
4. **Distinguish mechanism variants**: Different alpha-syn antibodies have different epitopes
5. **Flag combination products**: P2B001 contains two known drugs

- Total active Phase 1-3 trials: 136
- DMT vs ST split: 60 DMTs (44%), 76 STs (56%)
- Phase distribution: 41 Phase 1, 79 Phase 2, 16 Phase 3
- Novel vs repurposed: ~38% each
- Target enrollment: 16,649 participants
- Average trial duration: 29 months
- Trial completion rate: 17% (past year)
- Delay rate: 41% with average 10-month delays
