## Key Mechanism Categories

### Disease-Modifying Approaches
- **Alpha-synuclein targeting**: Prasinezumab (Roche), UB-312 (Vaxxinity), ACI-7104.056 (AC Immune)
- **LRRK2 inhibition/degradation**: BIIB122 (Biogen/Denali), NEU-411 (Neuron23), ARV-102 (Arvinas)
- **GCase activation/gene therapy**: GT-02287 (Gain), VQ-101 (Vanqua Bio), BIA 28-6156 (BIAL), CAP-003 (Capsida)
- **NLRP3 inhibition**: NT-0796 (NodThera), VENT-02 (Ventus), Dapansutrile (Olatec)
- **Mitochondrial protection**: NRG5051 (NRG Therapeutics), MTX325 (Mission Therapeutics)
- **Neurotrophic factors**: AB-1005 (AskBio), HER-096 (Herantis)

### Cell/Gene Therapies
- **ESC-derived**: Bemdaneprocel (BlueRock), TED-A9 (S.BIOMEDICS)
- **iPSC-derived (autologous)**: ANPD001 (Aspen), UX-DA001 (Unixell)
- **iPSC-derived (allogeneic)**: Kyoto University program
- **Gene therapy (AAV)**: AB-1005 (AskBio), CAP-003 (Capsida), PR001 (Prevail)

### Symptomatic Therapies
- **Tavapadon**: D1/D5 partial agonist (AbbVie/Cerevel)
- **ND0612**: Continuous levodopa/carbidopa infusion (Mitsubishi Tanabe)
- **SER-252**: Extended-release apomorphine (Serina)
- **Adaptive DBS**: BrainSense (Medtronic)
