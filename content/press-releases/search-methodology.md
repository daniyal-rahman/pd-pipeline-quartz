## Limitations

### Sources Not Fully Accessed
1. **Endpoints News**: Paywall limited access; site-specific search returned no results
2. **Peer-reviewed journals**: Full-text access limited (Lancet, Nature, Cell, etc.)
3. **Proprietary databases**: Evaluate, Citeline, GlobalData not accessed
4. **Company confidential pipelines**: Stealth programs not captured

### Geographic Gaps
- **Asia-Pacific**: Some Chinese, Japanese, Korean programs may be underrepresented
- **European biotechs**: Smaller companies may have limited English-language press

### Temporal Gaps
- Research conducted January 2026; announcements after search date not captured
- Some 2024 announcements may be underrepresented if not referenced in 2025 articles

### Mechanism Gaps
- **Device therapies**: Limited search (Medtronic DBS captured)
- **Digital therapeutics**: Not specifically searched
- **Dietary/supplement approaches**: Not searched

## Recommendations for Future Research

### Additional Searches Recommended
1. **ClinicalTrials.gov**: Direct search for newly registered PD trials
2. **Conference proceedings**: MDS, AAN, AD/PD congress abstracts
3. **SEC filings**: 10-K, 8-K filings for public companies
4. **Patent filings**: USPTO/EPO for early-stage programs
5. **Academic publications**: PubMed systematic search

### Sources to Monitor
1. **Company investor calls**: Quarterly earnings for pipeline updates
2. **FDA databases**: IND/NDA submissions, breakthrough designations
3. **EMA**: European regulatory filings
4. **PMDA (Japan)**: Asian regulatory activity
5. **Biotech conferences**: JPM, BIO, sector-specific events

### Suggested Search Frequency
- **Weekly**: Press release monitoring (PRNewswire, BusinessWire, GlobeNewswire)
- **Monthly**: Industry news synthesis (FierceBiotech, BioSpace, Endpoints)
- **Quarterly**: Comprehensive pipeline review
- **Annually**: Full mechanism-by-mechanism landscape analysis
