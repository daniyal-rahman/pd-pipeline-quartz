# New and Lesser-Known Parkinson's Disease Companies

#claude


## Companies Not Typically Featured in Major Pipeline Reviews

This document highlights companies that are making PD pipeline announcements but may not appear in mainstream pharmaceutical industry coverage. Many are emerging biotechs, international companies, or companies working on novel mechanisms.

## Tier 1: Clinical-Stage Emerging Companies

### Unixell Biotech (China/US)
- **Founded**: Clinical-stage company
- **Location**: China with US operations
- **Asset**: UX-DA001
- **Stage**: Phase 1 (IND approved in both China and US, 2024-2025)
- **Mechanism**: Autologous iPSC-derived dopaminergic neurons
- **Significance**: First autologous cell therapy product for PD with dual China/US IND approval
- **Funding**: Unknown
- **Watch for**: This is the world's first autologous cell therapy for PD approved in both jurisdictions
- **Source**: [PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/unixell-biotech-reported-a-case-study-of-ux-da001-an-ipsc-derived-autologous-cell-therapy-for-parkinson-diseases-at-mds-congress-2025-302586177.html)

### S.BIOMEDICS (South Korea)
- **Location**: South Korea
- **Asset**: TED-A9 (A9-DPC)
- **Stage**: Phase 1/2a (12-month positive data Nov 2024)
- **Mechanism**: hESC-derived dopamine progenitor cells
- **Significance**: One of the most advanced hESC cell therapy programs globally
- **Results**: 44.4% improvement on Hoehn & Yahr scale in high-dose patients
- **Source**: [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20241126606645/en/S.BIOMEDICS-Investigational-Cell-Therapy-for-Parkinson%E2%80%99s-Disease-With-TED-A9-Shows-Positive-Data-at-12-months-in-Phase-12a-Clinical-Trial)

### Herantis Pharma (Finland)
- **Location**: Turku, Finland
- **Asset**: HER-096
- **Stage**: Phase 1b completed (2025), Phase 2 planned for 2026
- **Mechanism**: CDNF (cerebral dopamine neurotrophic factor)-derived peptide
- **Significance**: First-in-class neuroprotective peptide with subcutaneous administration
- **Funding**: Michael J. Fox Foundation, Parkinson's UK
- **Public**: Listed on Nasdaq Helsinki
- **Source**: [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/10/15/Herantis-Pharma-presents-positive-topline-data-for-HER-096-in-Phase-1b-trial-for-people-living-with-Parkinsons-disease.html)

### Vanqua Bio (US)
- **Location**: United States
- **Asset**: VQ-101
- **Stage**: Phase 1b (positive interim data Oct 2025)
- **Mechanism**: Oral, brain-penetrant allosteric GCase activator
- **Target Population**: GBA-PD patients
- **Significance**: Robust GCase activation demonstrated at well-tolerated doses
- **Source**: [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/10/02/3160240/0/en/Vanqua-Bio-Highlights-Positive-Interim-Data-from-its-Ongoing-Phase-1b-Clinical-Trial-of-VQ-101-an-Allosteric-Activator-of-GCase-for-the-Treatment-of-GBA-Parkinson-s-and-Related-Dis.html)

### Serina Therapeutics (US)
- **Location**: Huntsville, Alabama
- **Asset**: SER-252 (POZ-apomorphine)
- **Stage**: Phase 1b starting Q4 2025
- **Mechanism**: POZ-conjugated apomorphine for extended release
- **Target**: 180,000 advanced PD patients in US
- **Funding**: $20M (Dec 2024 - Feb 2025)
- **Public**: NYSE American (SER)
- **Significance**: Novel polymer technology for sustained drug delivery
- **Source**: [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/12/02/2990192/0/en/Serina-Therapeutics-Secures-10-Million-Financing-to-Continue-Advancing-Lead-IND-Candidate-into-Phase-1-Clinical-Trial-in-Advanced-Parkinson-s-Disease-Patients.html)

### InBrain Pharma (Spain)
- **Location**: Spain
- **Asset**: DIVE-I device therapy
- **Stage**: Phase I/II (positive results Sept 2024)
- **Mechanism**: Direct intracerebral dopamine infusion device
- **Significance**: Novel device-assisted approach for advanced PD
- **Source**: [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/9/25/2952974/0/en/InBrain-Pharma-to-Present-Positive-Results-from-Its-DIVE-I-Phase-I-II-Clinical-Trial-for-Parkinson-s-Disease-at-the-Congress-of-Parkinson-s-Disease-and-Movement-Disorders-from-Sept.html)

## Tier 2: Preclinical/Early Clinical Emerging Companies

### Endlyz Therapeutics (Belgium/UK)
- **Founded**: April 2025
- **Location**: Belgium/UK
- **Asset**: Discovery-stage small molecules
- **Stage**: Preclinical
- **Mechanism**: ATP13A2/ATP10B pathway modulators (lysosomal recycling)
- **Funding**: €16M seed (SV Health Investors lead)
- **Significance**: Novel target (lysosomal transport proteins linked to familial PD)
- **Watch for**: First-in-class mechanism; recently launched
- **Source**: [Labiotech](https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/parkinsons-treatments-european-biotech/)

### NRG Therapeutics (UK)
- **Founded**: UK-based
- **Location**: United Kingdom
- **Asset**: NRG5051
- **Stage**: Phase 1 expected Dec 2025
- **Mechanism**: mPTP (mitochondrial permeability transition pore) inhibitor
- **Funding**: £50M ($67M) Series B (Sept 2025)
- **Investors**: SV Health (Dementia Discovery Fund), British Business Bank, M Ventures, Novartis Venture Fund, Criteria Bio Ventures, Omega Funds, Brandon Capital, Parkinson's UK
- **Significance**: First-in-class mitochondrial protection mechanism
- **Additional Target**: ALS/motor neuron disease
- **Source**: [BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/nrg-therapeutics-series-b-parkinsons-als-mitochondria-mptp/759403/)

### Mission Therapeutics (UK)
- **Location**: United Kingdom
- **Asset**: MTX325
- **Stage**: Phase 1 (expanded to 50 patients)
- **Mechanism**: Mitophagy modulator (DUB inhibitor for faulty mitochondria clearance)
- **Funding**: ~£10M (Oct 2025)
- **Significance**: Novel approach to clearing damaged mitochondria
- **Source**: [Parkinson's UK](https://www.parkinsons.org.uk/research/research-blog/research-explained/why-fixing-mitochondria-could-be-key-parkinsons)

### Kenai Therapeutics (US)
- **Founded**: 2024 (formerly Ryne Bio)
- **Location**: San Diego, California
- **Asset**: Off-the-shelf cell therapy
- **Stage**: Pre-clinical/IND-enabling
- **Mechanism**: Genetically reprogrammed stem cell-derived neurons
- **Funding**: $82M Series A (Feb 2024)
- **Investors**: Cure Ventures, The Column Group, Alaska Permanent Fund Corp.
- **Significance**: Major investment in next-gen allogeneic cell therapy
- **Source**: [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2024/02/29/kenai-therapeutics-parkinsons-disease-stem-cell-therapy/)

### ABLi Therapeutics (US)
- **Founded**: 2024 (spinout from Inhibikase)
- **Location**: United States
- **Asset**: Risvodetinib (IkT-148009)
- **Stage**: Phase 1b/2
- **Mechanism**: c-Abl tyrosine kinase inhibitor
- **Background**: Milton Werner (former Inhibikase CEO) founded ABLi to advance this troubled asset
- **Significance**: Attempting to rescue a promising c-Abl program
- **Source**: [FierceBiotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/former-inhibikase-leader-launches-new-biotech-advance-troubled-parkinsons-disease-asset)

### Capsida Biotherapeutics (US)
- **Location**: United States
- **Asset**: CAP-003
- **Stage**: Phase 1/2 (IND cleared June 2025, first patient Q3 2025)
- **Mechanism**: IV-administered AAV gene therapy for GBA1
- **Significance**: Novel IV-delivered, BBB-crossing AAV capsid; potential best-in-class profile
- **Differentiation**: 200-fold greater brain expression than AAV9 in NHP studies
- **Source**: [Capsida](https://capsida.com/capsida-receives-fda-ind-clearance-for-its-iv-administered-gene-therapy-for-parkinsons-disease-associated-with-gba-mutations/)

- **Funding**: $53M + $5M MJFF grant (2025)

- **Source**: [BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/scineuro-biotech-arch-lav-capital-raise-neuro-drug/807076/)

## Notable International Academic Programs

### Kyoto University (Japan)
- **Asset**: iPSC-derived dopaminergic progenitors
- **Stage**: Phase I/II completed (7 patients, published 2025 in Nature)
- **Significance**: First completed iPSC trial with 24-month follow-up data
- **Approach**: Allogeneic iPSCs with HLA-matched bank
- **Source**: [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08700-0)

### hESC-DPC Trial (South Korea/China consortium)
- **Asset**: A9-DPC
- **Stage**: Phase 1/2a (12 patients, published 2025 in Cell)
- **Results**: Up to 35% improvement in MDS-UPDRS Part III at 12 months
- **Source**: [Cell](https://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/S0092-8674(25)01041-4)

## Companies With Hints of Stealth-Mode Activity

Based on recent partnership announcements and funding patterns, the following areas suggest undisclosed/stealth-mode activity:

1. **Flagship Pioneering portfolio**: Multiple companies beyond Vesalius likely have PD programs (pattern of large pharma partnerships)

2. **Chinese iPSC companies**: Multiple undisclosed companies pursuing autologous iPSC approaches following Unixell's success

3. **AI-driven drug discovery**: Multiple AI biotechs likely have undisclosed PD programs given Valo Health/Merck KGaA and Hologen/MeiraGTx deals

4. **LRRK2 PROTAC space**: Arvinas success may indicate other undisclosed PROTAC programs

5. **GCase activation**: Competitive landscape suggests additional undisclosed programs beyond Gain, Vanqua, BIAL

## Investment Themes

### Hot Mechanisms (2024-2026)
1. **GCase activation/GBA gene therapy** - Multiple well-funded programs
2. **NLRP3 inhibition** - NodThera, Ventus, Olatec all advancing
3. **Cell therapies** - Both ESC and iPSC approaches progressing
4. **LRRK2 targeting** - PROTACs emerging as differentiated approach
5. **Mitochondrial protection** - NRG, Mission, Stealth BioTherapeutics

### Funding Trends
- **Cure Ventures** investing heavily in cell therapy (Kenai)
- **Parkinson's Virtual Biotech** (UK/US partnership) funding novel mechanisms
- **Michael J. Fox Foundation** supporting multiple early-stage programs
- **Large pharma** doing preclinical deals (GSK/Vesalius, Merck/Valo, AbbVie/Caraway)
