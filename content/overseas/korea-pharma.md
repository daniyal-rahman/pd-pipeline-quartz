# Major Korean Pharma Parkinson's Disease Programs

#claude


*Last updated: January 2026*

## Executive Summary

Korea's major pharmaceutical companies (Samsung Biologics, Celltrion, SK Biopharmaceuticals) are not directly developing Parkinson's disease therapeutics as lead programs. However, the K-Bio ecosystem includes significant PD activity through:

1. **Partnerships:** ABL Bio's billion-dollar deals with Sanofi and GSK
2. **CNS platforms:** SK Biopharmaceuticals' CNS expertise (epilepsy)
3. **Emerging modalities:** Microbiome (Celltrion/LISCure), GLP-1 agonists (D&D Pharmatech), Nurr1 activators (HanAll/Daewoong)
4. **Manufacturing:** World-class CDMO capabilities could support PD therapeutic production

## Celltrion

**Overview:**
- Major biosimilar manufacturer
- H1 2025: $1.31B turnover (+11.9% YoY), $285M operating income (3x increase)
- Expanding beyond biosimilars to new drug development

**PD Program:** LB-P4 (Microbiome therapy, partnered with LISCure Biosciences)

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Modality** | Oral live biotherapeutic product (LBP) |
| **Target** | Gut-brain axis, gut dysbiosis |
| **Partner** | LISCure Biosciences |
| **Stage** | Preclinical/Discovery |
| **Announced** | February 2023 |

**Scientific Rationale:**
- >30% of gut microbial species abnormal in PD patients
- Gut microbiome shifted toward pro-inflammatory state
- Alpha-synuclein aggregates may originate in gut and travel to brain

**Partnership Details:**
- Multi-year strategic R&D collaboration
- LISCure: Lead discovery using LMT platform
- Celltrion: Clinical development, regulatory, commercialization
- Expanding Celltrion's neurodegenerative pipeline

**LISCure Platform:**
- LISCure Microbiology-based Technology (LMT)
- Brain-targeting bacterial exosome technology
- Exo-Pass N: Brain shuttle platform
- Pipeline: LB-P4 for Parkinson's disease

## HanAll Biopharma / Daewoong Pharmaceutical

**Overview:**
- HanAll Biopharma: Biopharmaceutical company
- Daewoong Pharmaceutical: Major Korean pharma
- Partnership with NurrOn Pharmaceuticals (US - Harvard spinout)

**PD Program:** HL192 / ATH-399A

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Modality** | Small molecule |
| **Target** | Nurr1 activator |
| **Origin** | Harvard Medical School Molecular Neurobiology Lab |
| **Stage** | Phase 1 completed (November 2024) |
| **Support** | Michael J. Fox Foundation grant |

**Phase 1 Results (November 2024):**
- 76 healthy participants
- 5 ascending dose groups
- SAD, MAD, and food effect components
- Well tolerated, no major safety issues
- No serious treatment-related AEs
- PK supports once-daily dosing

**Next Steps:**
- Phase 1 trial in Parkinson's disease patients planned

**Mechanism:**
- Nurr1: Master regulator for dopaminergic neuron development and maintenance
- Dual role: Development/maintenance AND protection from inflammation-induced death
- Potential disease-modifying therapy

## D&D Pharmatech / Neuraly

**Overview:**
- Parent: D&D Pharmatech (Korea, public company, Seongnam-si)
- Subsidiary: Neuraly (US)
- Origin: Johns Hopkins School of Medicine spinouts
- Focus: CNS diseases (dementia, Alzheimer's, Parkinson's)

**PD Program:** NLY01

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Modality** | Pegylated GLP-1 receptor agonist |
| **Mechanism** | Inhibits astrocyte neurotoxic conversion, neuroprotection |
| **Stage** | Phase 2 completed |

**Phase 2 Results:**
- >250 Parkinson's patients
- 36-week treatment
- Statistically significant reduction in motor function decline (UPDRS III) in ~1/3 of subjects
- Benefit most pronounced in:
  - Young patients (<60)
  - Newly diagnosed
  - Treatment-naive

**Expansion:**
- FDA approved Phase 2 in multiple sclerosis (September 2024)
- Platform: HiF-Seq for neurodegenerative disease target discovery

**Funding:**
- $51M ahead of Korean IPO (2022)
- $137M for mid-phase trials (later funding)

**Sources:**
- [D&D Pharmatech](https://ddpharmatech.com/en/)
- [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/d-d-bags-137m-to-fund-midphase-alzheimer-s-parkinson-s-trials)

## ABL Bio

**Overview:**
- Biopharmaceutical company
- Expertise: Bispecific antibodies, brain shuttle technology

**PD Program:** ABL301 / SAR446159

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Modality** | Bispecific antibody |
| **Target** | Anti-alpha-synuclein + IGF1R (Grabody-B brain shuttle) |
| **Partner** | Sanofi ($75M upfront, up to $985M milestones) |
| **Stage** | Phase 1 completed (April 2025) |
| **Additional Deal** | GSK - ~$2B for neurodegenerative diseases (April 2025) |

**Phase 1 Results:**
- 91 healthy adults (SAD: 56, MAD: 35)
- Safe and well-tolerated
- 7.1% experienced TRAEs
- All TEAEs Grade 1-2
- No deaths or SAEs

**Platform Value:**
- Grabody-B: BBB-penetrating shuttle targeting IGF1R
- Applicable to multiple CNS targets beyond alpha-synuclein
- GSK partnership validates platform for broader neurodegeneration
