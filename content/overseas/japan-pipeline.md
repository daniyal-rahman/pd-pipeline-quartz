# Japan Parkinson's Disease Pipeline Overview

#claude


**Research Date:** January 2026

## Executive Summary

Japan is a global leader in Parkinson's disease research, particularly in iPSC-derived cell therapies. The country's regulatory framework (PMDA) provides accelerated pathways for regenerative medicine, and government funding through AMED has supported groundbreaking work at CiRA/Kyoto University. Japan represents roughly 250,000 PD patients, projected to grow with aging demographics.

## Key Japanese PD Pipeline Assets

### 1. iPSC-Derived Dopaminergic Progenitors (Sumitomo Pharma/RACTHERA)

**INN:** Raguneprocel
**Status:** NDA submitted August 2025
**Indication:** Advanced PD, improvement of motor functions during off-time
**Mechanism:** Allogeneic iPSC-derived dopaminergic neural progenitor cells transplanted into putamen

**Clinical Data (Phase I/II, Nature 2025):**
- 7 patients (ages 50-69), bilateral transplantation
- No serious adverse events (73 mild-moderate events)
- No graft overgrowth on MRI
- 4/6 patients showed improvement in MDS-UPDRS Part III OFF scores
- Average improvements: 9.5 points (20.4%) OFF, 4.3 points (35.7%) ON
- Hoehn-Yahr stage improved in 4 patients
- 18F-DOPA uptake increased 44.7% (high-dose group: 63.5%)

**Regulatory:** Priority Review designation; Sakigake designation (Feb 2017)

**Sources:**
- [Nature Phase I/II Trial Publication](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08700-0)
- [Sumitomo NDA Announcement](https://www.sumitomo-pharma.com/news/20250805-2.html)

### 2. ND0612 (Mitsubishi Tanabe/NeuroDerm)

**Status:** NDA resubmitted, FDA accepted (late 2025)
**Indication:** Motor fluctuations in PD
**Mechanism:** Continuous subcutaneous infusion of liquid levodopa/carbidopa (24 hours/day)
**Developer:** NeuroDerm Ltd. (Israel-based, wholly owned by Mitsubishi Tanabe since 2017)

**Clinical Data (Phase 3 BouNDless):**
- Met primary endpoint: superior increase in "ON" time without troublesome dyskinesia
- Superior reduction in "OFF" time vs. oral IR-LD/CD at 12 weeks
- Long-term safety: 3-year BeyoND extension data consistent with 12-month results
- Some participants in 8th year of follow-up

**Sources:**
- [Mitsubishi Tanabe NDA Acceptance](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mitsubishi-tanabe-pharma-america-announces-us-fda-acceptance-of-new-drug-application-resubmission-for-investigational-nd0612-for-the-treatment-of-motor-fluctuations-in-parkinsons-disease-302466198.html)
- [BouNDless Results - Lancet Neurology](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mitsubishi-tanabe-pharma-america-announces-publication-of-positive-results-from-phase-3-boundless-trial-of-investigational-nd0612-in-people-with-parkinsons-disease-experiencing-motor-fluctuations-302090915.html)

### 3. Equfina/Nouriast (Eisai/Meiji)

**Generic:** Safinamide mesilate
**Status:** Approved and marketed in Japan (Nov 2019), South Korea (2021)
**Indication:** Adjunctive therapy with levodopa for PD
**Mechanism:** Selective MAO-B inhibitor + sodium channel blocker (dual dopaminergic/non-dopaminergic)

**Clinical Data:**
- Extended "ON" time by 0.96 hours vs. placebo (24 weeks)
- Originated from Newron Pharmaceuticals (Italy)

**Sources:**
- [Eisai Japan Approval](https://www.eisai.com/news/2019/news201971.html)
- [Eisai South Korea Launch](https://www.eisai.com/news/2021/news202104.html)

### 4. NOURIANZ (Kyowa Kirin)

**Generic:** Istradefylline
**Status:** Approved Japan (2013), US (2019)
**Indication:** Adjunctive to L-dopa for "OFF" episodes
**Mechanism:** Adenosine A2A receptor antagonist

**Note:** Follow-on compound KW-6356 was discontinued due to regulatory hurdles

**Sources:**
- [FDA Approval](https://www.kyowakirin.com/media_center/news_releases/2019/e20190828_01.html)

### 5. Bemdaneprocel (BlueRock/Bayer - Japan Development)

**Status:** Sakigake designation (December 2025)
**Indication:** PD, dopaminergic neuron replacement
**Mechanism:** Human embryonic pluripotent stem cell-derived dopaminergic neuron precursors
**Developer:** BlueRock Therapeutics (Bayer subsidiary)

**Note:** While not Japanese-originated, received Japan's Pioneering Regenerative Medical Product designation, indicating PMDA prioritization for Japan market entry.

## Discontinued/Exited Programs

- Partnered with AstraZeneca
- Phase 2 failure led to program discontinuation
- AstraZeneca continues development independently

### Kyowa Kirin KW-6356
- A2A antagonist follow-on to NOURIANZ
- Discontinued despite positive Phase 2b data
- Cited regulatory complexity and market timing

## Regulatory Environment

### PMDA Pathways for Regenerative Medicine

**SAKIGAKE Designation:**
- Pioneering Regenerative Medical Product status
- Target review time: 6 months
- More frequent MHLW interaction during development
- Priority review eligibility

**Conditional and Time-Limited Approval:**
- Marketing approval for up to 7 years based on preliminary efficacy
- Requires confirmed safety and predicted efficacy
- Full approval requires post-market confirmatory data

## Market Context

- Japan PD patients: ~250,000 (Bayer), ~150,000 (Otsuka estimates)
- Aging population driving incidence growth
- Strong government commitment to regenerative medicine (110B yen+ invested)
- First country to approve clinical use of iPSCs
