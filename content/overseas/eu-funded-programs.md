## 2. Innovative Medicines Initiative (IMI) / Innovative Health Initiative (IHI)

### Overview
- Public-private partnership: European Commission + EFPIA
- Total NDD portfolio: EUR 385.5 million
- Mean funding per project: EUR 21.4 million
- Launched 2008 with EUR 2 billion initial budget

### Key Neurodegeneration Projects

#### PD-MIND
- **Objective:** Repurpose AZD0328 (nicotinic agonist) for PD with mild cognitive impairment (PD-MCI)
- **Design:** Randomized, placebo-controlled, multi-center
- **Background:** Early trials suggested cognition improvement in PD
- **Partners:** Consortium across multiple European countries

#### Mobilise-D
- **Objective:** Develop digital mobility outcomes for disease progression
- **Technology:** Single wearable device (back-attached)
- **Validation:** 24 digital mobility outcomes clinically validated
- **PD component:** One of four target conditions (with MS, COPD, hip fractures)
- **Key finding:** Single-sensor system matched multi-sensor gold standard (INDIP)
- **Status:** Completed/sustaining

#### IDEA-FAST
- **Objective:** Digital endpoints for daily activities, disability, quality of life
- **Target diseases:** PD, Huntington's (NDD) + multiple immune-mediated inflammatory diseases
- **Approach:** Identify reliable, objective, sensitive digital measures
- **Collaboration:** Created Digital Health Catalyst with Mobilise-D

#### RADAR-CNS / RADAR-AD
- **Focus:** Digital assessment and endpoints for CNS diseases
- **Technology:** Remote Assessment of Disease And Relapse
- **Application:** Multiple NDDs including Parkinson's

#### PHAGO
- **Focus:** Phagocyte dysfunction in neurodegeneration
- **Relevance:** Microglial contribution to PD pathology

#### IMPRiND
- **Focus:** Molecular underpinnings of Parkinson's and Alzheimer's
- **Approach:** Basic science to understand disease mechanisms

#### ADAPTED
- **Focus:** Amyloid and neurodegeneration research
- **PD relevance:** Cross-disease mechanisms

#### PD-MitoQUANT
- **Focus:** Mitochondrial dysfunction in Parkinson's
- **Approach:** Quantitative imaging and biomarkers

### Network Structure
- **IMI Neuronet:** Coordinates all NDD projects
- 5 key connector projects: EMIF, IDEA-FAST, PHAGO, PRISM, RADAR-CNS
- Each connected to all other portfolio projects through shared partners

## 3. JPND (Joint Programme - Neurodegenerative Disease Research)

### Overview
- Largest global NDD research initiative
- Transnational coordination of research efforts
- Multi-country funded calls

### 2025 Research Call
- **Title:** Health and Social Care Research - Moderate and Late Stages of NDDs
- **Budget:** ~EUR 15 million
- **Focus:** Well-being of people with NDDs through social sciences/humanities
- **Requirements:**
  - Multinational consortia
  - Multi-disciplinary approach
  - Address moderate, advanced, and end-of-life stages
  - Ethical considerations

### Funding Countries (2025 call)
- Belgium (F.R.S.-FNRS)
- Canada (CIHR)
- Additional European and international partners

### Previous JPND Initiatives

#### JPco-fuND 2
- Multi-country funding for collaborative NDD projects
- Supported multiple Parkinson's research consortia

#### Personalised Medicine Projects (2019)
- Focus on precision approaches to NDD
- Including genetic and biomarker-stratified Parkinson's research

#### Cross-Disease Analysis Projects
- Pilot studies on preventive strategies
- Understanding common mechanisms across NDDs

## 5. National/Foundation Programs with EU Reach

### Lundbeck Foundation (Denmark)
**PACE Center Grant**
- **Amount:** DKK 313 million (DKK 223M initial + DKK 90M extension)
- **Location:** Aarhus University Hospital
- **Focus:** Lewy Body Disorders (including Parkinson's)
- **Duration:** 6 years (+ potential 4-year extension)
- **Goal:** Clinical trials within 10 years, disease modification documentation

### Cure Parkinson's (UK-based, European reach)
**SLEIPNIR Platform (Norway)**
- **Announced:** 2025
- **Start:** Early 2026
- **Design:** Multi-arm platform trial
- **Leadership:** Prof. Charalampos Tzoulis (Haukeland University Hospital/University of Bergen)
- **Approach:** Test up to 3 disease-modifying treatments vs single placebo
- **Focus:** Short-term studies assessing brain penetration, target engagement, safety

**MTX325 Trial**
- **Stage:** Phase 1 planned early 2026
- **Sites:** UK and other European countries
- **Funding:** Parkinson's Virtual Biotech

**Combination Therapy Initiative**
- **Launched:** October 2025
- **Funding:** GBP 2 million dedicated call
- **Focus:** Rationally-designed combinations to slow progression
- **Status:** Applications received, update expected H1 2026

### Michael J. Fox Foundation (US-based, EU-funded projects)
Funding recipients in Europe:
- Herantis Pharma (Finland) - HER-096 Phase 1b
- Muna Therapeutics (Denmark) - $4.9M for Kv1.3 blockers
- Endlyz (Belgium/UK) - $2.2M for ATP13A2 program
- Gain Therapeutics (Switzerland) - GT-02287 development

### Parkinson's UK (with European reach)
- Co-funded Herantis HER-096 trial
- Invested in Endlyz
- Supports European collaborative trials

### French Ministry of Health
- Funded LIXIPARK trial (lixisenatide)
- Coordinated by Toulouse University Hospital

## 9. Gaps and Opportunities

### Identified Gaps
1. Limited dedicated EU funding for alpha-synuclein biology (most coming from industry)
2. No major EU-funded LRRK2 inhibitor development (primarily US companies)
3. Cell therapy programs primarily industry-funded (Bayer/BlueRock)
4. Gene therapy programs mostly US-originated (Prevail/Lilly, AskBio/Bayer)

### Opportunities
1. **2027 Horizon Call** - Clinical trials for NDD interventions
2. **JPND collaboration** - Transnational social care research
3. **Digital endpoints** - Building on Mobilise-D/IDEA-FAST infrastructure
4. **Platform trials** - SLEIPNIR model for efficient disease-modification testing

### Recommendations for Tracking
- Monitor CTIS for new European PD trial registrations
- Follow Horizon Europe Work Programme updates
- Track JPND call results
- Watch EMA guideline revision for regulatory changes
