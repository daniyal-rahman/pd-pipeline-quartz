# South Korea Parkinson's Disease Pipeline Overview

#claude


*Last updated: January 2026*

## Executive Summary

South Korea has emerged as a significant player in Parkinson's disease therapeutics, with particular strength in cell therapy and bispecific antibody development. The country ranks third globally for new drug discoveries and fourth for clinical trial activity. Key PD programs span cell replacement therapy (S.BIOMEDICS, CHA Biotech), bispecific antibodies (ABL Bio), Nurr1 activators (HanAll/Daewoong), and microbiome therapeutics (Celltrion/LISCure).

## Major Korean PD Companies & Assets

### 1. ABL Bio - ABL301/SAR446159 (Partnered with Sanofi)

**Status:** Phase 1 completed (April 2025)

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Modality** | Bispecific antibody |
| **Target** | Alpha-synuclein + IGF1R (brain shuttle) |
| **Mechanism** | BBB-penetrating anti-alpha-synuclein antibody using Grabody-B platform |
| **Partnership** | Sanofi - $75M upfront, up to $985M milestones |
| **Clinical Progress** | Phase 1 SAD/MAD completed in 91 healthy adults; safe and well-tolerated |

**Key Data:**
- Phase 1 conducted December 2022 - April 2025
- 56 participants in SAD, 35 in MAD
- 7.1% experienced treatment-related AEs (4/56), with 2 of those in placebo group
- All TEAEs were Grade 1 or 2
- No deaths or serious adverse events reported

**Mechanism Highlights:**
- Grabody-B targets insulin-like growth factor 1 receptor (IGF1R) to maximize BBB penetration
- Anti-alpha-synuclein domain shows robust recognition of pathological aggregates with high affinity
- Minimal affinity to monomeric alpha-synuclein
- Superior brain and CSF penetration vs. monoclonal antibody in preclinical models

**Sources:**

- [Nature npj Parkinson's Disease](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41531-025-01117-6)

### 2. HanAll Biopharma / Daewoong / NurrOn - HL192 (ATH-399A)

**Status:** Phase 1 completed (November 2024)

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Modality** | Small molecule |
| **Target** | Nurr1 activator |
| **Mechanism** | Activates Nurr1, master regulator of dopaminergic neuron development/maintenance |
| **Origin** | Harvard Medical School (NurrOn Pharmaceuticals) |
| **Support** | Michael J. Fox Foundation grant |

**Key Data:**
- First-in-human study completed November 2024
- 76 healthy participants across 5 ascending dose groups
- SAD, MAD, and food effect components
- MAD participants received once-daily dosing for 12 days
- Well tolerated with no major safety issues
- PK results support once-daily dosing

**Next Steps:**
- Initiate trial in Parkinson's disease patients (planned 2025-2026)

**Rationale:**
- Nurr1 has dual role in DA neuron development/maintenance AND protection from inflammation-induced death
- Potential for disease modification, not just symptomatic treatment

### 3. D&D Pharmatech - NLY01

**Status:** Phase 2 completed

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Modality** | Pegylated GLP-1 receptor agonist |
| **Mechanism** | Inhibits astrocyte conversion to neurotoxic phenotype, protects against neurodegeneration |
| **Parent** | D&D Pharmatech (Korea) via subsidiary Neuraly |
| **Origin** | Johns Hopkins School of Medicine spinout |

**Key Data:**
- Phase 2 study in >250 Parkinson's patients
- 36-week treatment period
- Statistically significant reduction in motor function decline (UPDRS III) in ~1/3 of subjects
- Appeared to slow progressive decline in motor function in:
  - Young patients (<60 years)
  - Newly diagnosed patients
  - Treatment-naive patients

**Additional Development:**
- FDA approved Phase 2 trial in multiple sclerosis (September 2024)
- Platform technology (HiF-Seq) for neurodegenerative disease target discovery

**Sources:**
- [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/d-d-bags-137m-to-fund-midphase-alzheimer-s-parkinson-s-trials)
- [D&D Pharmatech Website](https://ddpharmatech.com/en/)

### 4. Celltrion / LISCure Biosciences - LB-P4

**Status:** Discovery/Preclinical

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Modality** | Oral live biotherapeutic product (LBP) / Microbiome therapy |
| **Mechanism** | Brain-targeting bacterial exosomes |
| **Platform** | LISCure Microbiology-based Technology (LMT) |
| **Additional** | Exo-Pass N brain shuttle platform |

**Scientific Rationale:**
- PD associated with gut dysbiosis
- >30% of gut microbial species abnormal in PD patients
- Gut microbiome shifted toward pro-inflammatory state
- Alpha-synuclein may travel from gut to brain

**Partnership Details:**
- LISCure leads discovery of novel candidates
- Celltrion responsible for clinical and regulatory development
- Multi-year strategic R&D collaboration announced February 2023

## Regulatory Environment

### MFDS (Korean FDA) Pathways

**Expedited Programs:**
- **Customized Review:** Step-by-step pre-screening
- **Priority Review:** Priority over other drugs
- **Conditional Approval:** Drugs for serious/rare diseases approved from Phase 2 with requirement to conduct Phase 3 post-approval

**Timeline Impact:** Can reduce approval time by 3.5-4.5 years

### Regenerative Medicine Law (February 2025)

As of February 21, 2025, South Korea activated a new law allowing patients with "severe, rare, or incurable" conditions (including Parkinson's disease) to receive:
- Cell therapies not yet approved
- Gene therapies in development
- Must have demonstrated safety and efficacy in clinical studies
- Only 112 authorized medical institutions can administer

**Sources:**
- [Regenerative Medicine Foundation](https://regmedfoundation.org/2025/02/10/korea-permits-stem-cell-therapies-for-arthritis-autoimmune-diseases-and-parkinsons-patients/)

## K-Bio Ecosystem Context

### Industry Statistics
- South Korea biopharma market: $22 billion (13th largest globally)
- Ranks 3rd worldwide for new drug discoveries
- Ranks 4th globally for clinical trial activity
- Seoul: World's top clinical trial city (2023)
- 1,300+ new drug candidates developed in past 3 years (10% of global total)

### Major Deals (Recent)
- ABL Bio/Sanofi: Up to $1.06B for ABL301
- GSK/ABL Bio: ~$2B for neurodegenerative disease collaboration (April 2025)
- Alteogen/AstraZeneca: Up to $1.35B for subcutaneous cancer therapies

### Government Initiatives
- National Bio Committee launched January 2025 (presidential advisory body)
- Target: Increase biotech output from $43B (2020) to $149B by 2035
- Korean Fund for Regenerative Medicine (KFRM) supports clinical trials
