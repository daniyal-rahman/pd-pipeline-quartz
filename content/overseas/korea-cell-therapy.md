# Korean Stem Cell & Cell Therapy Programs for Parkinson's Disease




*Last updated: January 2026*

## Executive Summary

South Korea is a global leader in stem cell therapy for Parkinson's disease, with the most advanced program being S.BIOMEDICS' TED-A9 (Phase 1/2a positive data published in Cell). Korea's strength in this area stems from early government investment, strong academic programs (CHA University, Yonsei), and a supportive regulatory framework including the new Regenerative Medicine Law (Feb 2025). Multiple modalities are in development including hESC-derived dopaminergic progenitors, fetal midbrain precursors, and adipose-derived MSCs.

## Key Cell Therapy Programs

### 1. S.BIOMEDICS - TED-A9 / A9-DPC

**Status:** Phase 1/2a completed - POSITIVE DATA (Published in Cell, October 2025)

This is the most advanced and most significant Korean cell therapy program for PD.

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Cell Type** | hESC-derived ventral midbrain dopaminergic progenitor cells |
| **Manufacturing** | Rigorous GMP conditions |
| **Trial Site** | Severance Hospital, Yonsei University, Seoul |
| **Regulatory** | MFDS approved |
| **Funding** | Korean Fund for Regenerative Medicine (KFRM) |

#### Trial Design

- **Design:** Single-center, open-label, dose-escalation Phase 1/2a
- **Patients:** 12 participants with PD >5 years
- **Inclusion:** Motor complications (wearing-off, freezing of gait, dyskinesia)
- **Age Range:** 50-75 years
- **Dosing:**
  - Low-dose: 3.15 million cells (n=6)
  - High-dose: 6.30 million cells (n=6)
- **Route:** Bilateral putamen transplantation
- **Last Patient Treated:** February 2024
- **Follow-up:** 12 months reported (primary: 24 months + 3 year safety follow-up)

#### Efficacy Results (12 months)

**Motor Function (MDS-UPDRS Part III off):**
| Dose | Improvement | Point Decrease |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| Low-dose (3.15M cells) | 22.7% | 12.7 points |
| High-dose (6.30M cells) | 25.3% | 13.0 points |

**Freezing of Gait Resolution:**
- Low-dose: 50% improvement (1 of 2 patients)
- High-dose: 100% improvement (3 of 3 patients)

**Non-Motor Symptoms (NMSS):**
- Low-dose: 31.7 point improvement
- High-dose: 35.8 point improvement

**Imaging ([18F]FP-CIT PET):**
- Overall increase in putamen DAT signals
- Greater increases in high-dose group
- **Critical finding:** Statistically significant correlation between MDS-UPDRS III improvement and increased DAT signal in posterior dorsal putamen
- Supports hypothesis of synaptic restoration through engrafted cells

**Other Assessments:** Favorable outcomes in PDQ-39 and SEADL

#### Safety Profile (12 months)

- **No tumorigenesis**
- **No overgrowth of transplanted cells**
- **No ectopic cell migration**
- **No immune-related issues**
- **No treatment-emergent AEs related to transplanted cells**
- One mild hemorrhaging incident (not related to transplant site)

#### Publication

- **Journal:** Cell
- **Received:** May 12, 2025
- **Published Online:** October 13, 2025
- [Cell Publication](https://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/S0092-8674(25)01041-4)

#### Key Takeaways

1. First successful Phase 1/2a of hESC-derived DA progenitors with 12-month efficacy data
2. Both efficacy and imaging evidence of functional engraftment
3. Dose-response relationship suggests higher doses may be beneficial
4. Excellent safety profile supports further development
5. Low dose (3.15M cells) was informed by preclinical dose-ranging studies

### 2. CHA Biotech (CHA University) - CBT-NPC

**Status:** Phase 2 completed (awaiting results publication)

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Cell Type** | Fetal midbrain-derived dopamine neuronal precursor cells |
| **Institution** | CHA University Bundang Medical Center (CBMC) |
| **Key Researchers** | Prof. Moon Ji-sook (CHA University), Prof. Kim (CBMC) |
| **International Collaboration** | Germany, USA |

#### Historical Context

- 2005: Prof. Moon Ji-sook succeeded in proliferating dopamine nerve cells from fetal midbrain
- 2013: World's first stem cell transplant for PD (65-year-old woman)
- Patient showed improvement in motor function, able to perform daily activities and use public transportation

#### Clinical Trial Results

**Study Design:**
- 15 patients with idiopathic PD
- 3 dose cohorts with 12-month follow-up
- Three different cell doses administered

**UPDRS-III Motor Recovery:**
| Dose Group | Improvement |
|------------|-------------|
| Low-dose | 11.6% |
| Medium-dose | 26.16% |
| High-dose | 40.0% |

**Safety:**
- No serious clinical complications
- No graft-induced dyskinesia in any patient

#### Publications

- [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36692025/)
- [Korea Biomed News](https://www.koreabiomed.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=20683)

### 3. Biostar Stem Cell Research Institute / Nature Cell - Adipose MSC

**Status:** Approved in Japan (MHLW) for PD treatment

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Cell Type** | Autologous adipose-derived mesenchymal stem cells |
| **Administration** | IV + Spinal cavity injection |
| **Dosage** | 200M cells IV + 50M cells intrathecal |
| **Regulatory** | Japan Ministry of Health, Labor and Welfare approval |
| **Partners** | Japan Angel Stem Cell (JASC), R Bio |

#### Development Status

- Developed regenerative medicine technology in Korea
- Received approval from Japan's MHLW
- Based on Japan treatment experience, planning commercialization in Korea by 2025
- Nature Cell supplies stem cell culture media to JASC and R Bio

#### Mechanism

- Autologous fat-derived stem cells
- Patented culture technology
- Dual administration route (IV + intrathecal)

## Korean Cell Therapy Advantages

### Regulatory Support

1. **MFDS Expedited Pathways:**
   - Conditional approval from Phase 2 for serious/rare diseases
   - Priority review designation
   - Potential 3.5-4.5 year reduction in approval timeline

2. **Regenerative Medicine Law (February 2025):**
   - Patients with PD can access unapproved cell therapies
   - 112 authorized institutions
   - Safety and efficacy must be demonstrated in clinical studies

### Government Funding

- Korean Fund for Regenerative Medicine (KFRM)
- Brain Research Promotion Act (1998)
- Korea Brain Initiative

### Manufacturing Expertise

- Multiple GMP cell manufacturing facilities
- Experience scaling hESC and MSC production
- Strong academic-industry partnerships
