# China PD Research: Information Gaps and Access Limitations




**Research Date:** January 21, 2026
**Focus:** Documentation of what we could not access or verify

---

## Executive Summary

This document catalogs information gaps encountered during research on Chinese Parkinson's disease pipeline assets. These gaps arise from language barriers, blocked websites, limited English-language coverage, and proprietary/confidential information.

---

## 1. Language Barriers

### Chinese-Only Sources Not Accessed:

#### Chinese Clinical Trial Registry (ChiCTR)
- **Issue:** Primary interface and detailed records in Chinese
- **Impact:** May have missed trials not cross-registered on ClinicalTrials.gov
- **Workaround Needed:** Chinese-language search capability

#### NMPA CDE Registry
- **Issue:** Detailed IND filings, approval letters, and trial protocols in Chinese
- **Impact:** Cannot access full regulatory documents for approved trials
- **Workaround Needed:** NMPA account and Chinese language capability

#### Company Websites
- **Companies with Limited English:**
  - Sizhe Biopharmaceuticals (partner to Shanghai East Hospital)
  - Many smaller biotechs in Suzhou BioBay and Zhangjiang
- **Impact:** Cannot verify pipeline details, financing, leadership

#### Academic Publications
- **Issue:** Many Chinese PD studies published in Chinese-language journals
- **Databases Not Searched:**
  - China National Knowledge Infrastructure (CNKI)
  - Wanfang Data
  - Chinese Scientific Journals Database
- **Impact:** Missing domestic research, especially TCM studies
