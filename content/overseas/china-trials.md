#claude


**Research Date:** January 21, 2026
**Focus:** Clinical trials registered and conducted in China

## Trial Registration Systems in China

### Primary Registries:
1. **CDE Drug Clinical Trials Registration (CTR)** - Official NMPA registry; mandatory for drug trials
2. **Chinese Clinical Trial Registry (ChiCTR)** - WHO-recognized; third largest globally after ClinicalTrials.gov and EU CTR
3. **ClinicalTrials.gov** - Many Chinese trials also register here

### Regulatory Framework:
- Drug Registration Regulation (Decree No. 27) effective July 1, 2020
- ICH E6(R3) GCP guidelines apply to all trials after March 31, 2026
- Protocol registration required before trial initiation

## Cell Therapy Trials (iPSC/Stem Cell)

### 1. UniXell UX-DA001 Phase I
- **Registry:** NMPA CDE
- **Sponsor:** Shanghai UniXell Biotechnology
- **Site:** Ruijin Hospital, Shanghai
- **PI:** Dr. Liu Jun; Surgery by Dr. Li Dianyou
- **Status:** Recruiting/Active (First patient March 2025)
- **Population:** Parkinson's disease
- **Intervention:** Autologous iPSC-derived dopaminergic neural progenitors
- **Primary Outcomes:** Safety, tolerability

### 2. iRegene NouvNeu001 Phase I/II
- **Registry:** NMPA CDE / ClinicalTrials.gov (NCT06167681 for Phase II)
- **Sponsor:** iRegene Therapeutics
- **Sites:** Beijing Hospital; Zhongnan Hospital (Wuhan University)
- **Status:** Phase I complete; Phase II multicenter initiated April 2025
- **Population:** Mid-to-late stage Parkinson's disease
- **Intervention:** Allogeneic iPSC-derived chemically-induced dopaminergic neurons
- **Target Enrollment:** ~40 patients (Phase I)
- **Key Finding:** No immunosuppression required after 6 months

### 3. XellSmart XS-411 Phase I
- **Registry:** NMPA CDE
- **Sponsor:** XellSmart Biopharmaceutical
- **Sites:** Beijing Tiantan Hospital (National Neurological Disease Medical Center)
- **Status:** Recruiting (2025)
- **Population:** Parkinson's disease
- **Intervention:** Allogeneic off-the-shelf iPSC-derived dopaminergic neural progenitors
- **Design:** Registrational Phase I

### 4. XellSmart XS-411 Phase I/II
- **Registry:** NMPA CDE
- **Sponsor:** XellSmart Biopharmaceutical
- **Site:** Huashan Hospital, Fudan University
- **Status:** Recruiting (2025)
- **Population:** Early-onset Parkinson's disease (EOPD)
- **Intervention:** XS-411
- **Design:** Randomized, double-blind, controlled

### 5. Shanghai East Hospital/Sizhe iPSC Trial
- **Sponsor:** Shanghai East Hospital (Tongji University) + Sizhe Biopharmaceuticals
- **PI:** Professor Liu Zhongmin
- **Status:** 12-month results announced January 2025
- **Population:** Parkinson's disease
- **Intervention:** iPSC-derived dopaminergic neurons
- **Outcomes:** Good safety and efficacy; motor improvement and quality of life enhancement
- **Note:** Described as "China's first iPSC therapy for Parkinson's"

### 6. USTC Hefei Stem Cell Trial
- **Institution:** First Affiliated Hospital, USTC
- **PI:** Dr. Shi Jiong
- **Status:** Phase I initiated (mid-2025)
- **Population:** Parkinson's disease
- **Intervention:** Stem cell transplant with proprietary >80% conversion efficiency
- **Note:** Academic/investigator-initiated trial

## Small Molecule/Formulation Trials

### 7. Luye Pharma LY03003 Phase III (Completed)
- **Registry:** CDE / ClinicalTrials.gov
- **Sponsor:** Luye Pharma Group
- **Sites:** Multicenter, China
- **Status:** Completed July 2022; Approved for marketing (Jinyouping)
- **Population:** Early-stage idiopathic Parkinson's disease
- **Intervention:** Rotigotine extended-release microspheres (weekly injection)
- **Design:** Multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled
- **Enrollment:** 294 patients
- **Primary Endpoint:** UPDRS Part II+III change from baseline
- **Results:** LY03003 -11.8 vs placebo -5.6 (P<.001)

### 8. Shanghai WD WD-1603 Phase III
- **Registry:** CDE
- **Sponsor:** Shanghai WD Pharmaceutical
- **Sites:** Multicenter, China
- **Status:** Initiating 2025 (Phase II completed)
- **Population:** Early-stage Parkinson's disease
- **Intervention:** Carbidopa-Levodopa bi-modal controlled release tablets
- **Design:** Phase II was randomized, parallel, double-blind, placebo-controlled
- **Phase II Arms:** Low (25/100mg), Medium (25/150mg), High (2x25/100mg), Placebo
- **Duration:** 4 weeks treatment

### 9. Luye Pharma LY03017 Phase I
- **Registry:** CDE
- **Sponsor:** Luye Pharma Group
- **Status:** Active (China); FDA IND cleared for US
- **Indication:** Parkinson's Disease Psychosis (PDP)
- **Intervention:** 5-HT2AR inverse agonist / 5-HT2CR antagonist
- **Note:** Parallel development in China and US

## Traditional Chinese Medicine Trials

### 10. TCM Syndrome Differentiation Trial
- **Registry:** ChiCTR2200056373
- **Sites:** 7 centers including Longhua Hospital (Shanghai TCM University), Xinhua Hospital
- **Design:** Multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled
- **Population:** Parkinson's disease at different stages
- **Interventions:**
  - Early stage (Yin deficiency): Ziyin Pingchan formula
  - Moderate stage (phlegm heat/blood stasis): Jiedu Pingchan formula
  - Late stage (Yin/Yang and Qi/blood deficiency): Fuzheng Pingchan formula
- **Registered:** February 4, 2022

### 11. Chinese Herbal Medicine Adjunct Trial
- **Registry:** ClinicalTrials.gov (NCT05001217)
- **Design:** Pilot add-on, randomized, controlled, pragmatic trial
- **Population:** Parkinson's disease patients on standard therapy
- **Intervention:** Chinese herbal medicine based on subgroup differentiation
- **Primary Aim:** Assess feasibility and preliminary efficacy

### 12. Jia Wei Liu Jun Zi Tang (JWLJZT) Trial
- **Registry:** Various
- **Design:** Randomized, placebo-controlled
- **Enrollment:** 55 patients
- **Duration:** 24 weeks
- **Results:** Significant improvement in UPDRS IVC at 12 and 24 weeks; improved PDQ-39 communication scores

## Registry/Observational Studies

### 13. National Registry Study (NCT03649503)
- **Registry:** ClinicalTrials.gov
- **Sponsor:** Beijing Tiantan Hospital
- **Platform:** PaWei
- **Objective:** Identify, assess, and longitudinally monitor Parkinsonian disorder patients
- **Data Collected:** Demographics, overall health, family history, clinical information
- **Status:** Ongoing

### 14. Parkinson's Disease Progression Neuroimaging Initiative (PDPNI)
- **Sponsor:** PET Center and Neurology Dept, Huashan Hospital, Fudan University
- **Status:** Launched February 2010; ongoing
- **Enrollment:** 224 subjects (176 PD patients, 48 healthy controls) as of March 2019
- **Follow-up:** Every 2 years
- **Imaging:** FDG-PET, DAT-PET, ASL MRI, fMRI
- **Objective:** Identify progression biomarkers
