## Major European Pharmaceutical Companies

### Roche (Switzerland - Basel)
**Prasinezumab (PRX002/RO7046015)**
- **Target:** Alpha-synuclein (aggregated forms)
- **Modality:** Monoclonal antibody
- **Stage:** Phase 3 (advancing from Phase 2b PADOVA)
- **Status:** Ph IIb (PADOVA) and longer follow-up data suggest clinical benefit in delay of confirmed motor progression. Roche making Phase 3 go-decision.
- **Note:** This makes 2026 a pivotal year for alpha-synuclein targeting

### Novartis (Switzerland - Basel)
**Alpha-Synuclein RNAi (via Arrowhead)**
- **Target:** Alpha-synuclein gene silencing
- **Modality:** siRNA
- **Stage:** Preclinical
- **Deal:** September 2025 - $200M upfront, up to $2B+ milestones
- **Status:** Preclinical drug designed to silence genetic instructions for alpha-synuclein

**UCB0599/Minzasolmin (co-development with UCB)**
- **Target:** Alpha-synuclein misfolding inhibitor
- **Stage:** Phase 2 (ORCHESTRA trial)
- **Deal:** December 2021 - $150M upfront, $1.5B potential total
- **Note:** Novartis has rights outside Europe; UCB retains European rights

### UCB (Belgium - Brussels)
**Minzasolmin (UCB0599)**
- **Target:** Oral alpha-synuclein misfolding inhibitor
- **Modality:** Small molecule
- **Stage:** Phase 2 (ORCHESTRA trial enrolling)
- **Origin:** In-licensed from Neuropore Therapies
- **Phase 1/1b:** Acceptable safety/tolerability profile, predictable PK in 73 healthy participants and 21 PD patients

**UCB7853**
- **Target:** Alpha-synuclein (extracellular)
- **Modality:** Monoclonal antibody
- **Stage:** Phase 1
- **Note:** Complementary approach to UCB0599 targeting extracellular spread of pathological alpha-synuclein

### Lundbeck (Denmark - Copenhagen)
**Amlenetug (Lu AF82422)**
- **Target:** Alpha-synuclein aggregates
- **Modality:** Human monoclonal antibody
- **Stage:** Phase 3 (MASCOT trial - NCT06706622)
- **Indication:** Multiple System Atrophy (MSA) primarily, implications for PD
- **Designations:** FDA & EMA Orphan Drug Designation, SAKIGAKE (Japan)
- **Partner:** Co-developed with Genmab A/S
- **Trial Design:** 72-week double-blind period with open-label extension

**Lu AF28996 & Foliglurax**
- **Status:** Additional PD pipeline candidates in development

### BIAL (Portugal)
**Opicapone (Ongentys) - APPROVED**
- **Target:** COMT inhibition
- **Modality:** Small molecule
- **Stage:** Approved and marketed
- **Status:** Third-generation COMT inhibitor for end-of-dose motor fluctuations

**EPSILON Trial (Opicapone expansion)**
- **Stage:** Phase 3
- **Population:** Early PD patients without motor fluctuations
- **Countries:** Belgium, Bulgaria, Czech Republic, France, Germany, Italy, Poland, Portugal, Spain, Turkey, UK, Ukraine

**BIA 28-6156 (ACTIVATE Study)**
- **Target:** GCase allosteric activator
- **Modality:** Small molecule
- **Stage:** Phase 2
- **Population:** GBA1-mutation carriers with PD
- **Note:** Originally LTI-291; once-daily oral, brain-penetrant

### Sanofi (France - Paris)
**ABL301 (partnership with ABL Bio)**
- **Target:** Bispecific antibody (alpha-synuclein + IGF1R)
- **Stage:** Preclinical
- **Note:** In-licensed from South Korean ABL Biotechnologies

**Venglustat**
- **Target:** Glucosylceramide synthase inhibitor

**Ventyx VTX3232 (acquisition interest)**

## European Biotechs - Disease-Modifying Approaches

### MODAG GmbH (Germany - Wendelsheim)
**Emrusolmin/Anle138b (TEV-56286)**
- **Target:** Alpha-synuclein oligomer modulator
- **Modality:** Small molecule
- **Stage:** Phase 2 (starting late 2024)
- **Partner:** Teva Pharmaceuticals (October 2021 deal)
- **Phase 1a:** Safe at doses up to 300mg daily, 12hr half-life, plasma levels exceeded mouse model efficacy
- **Phase 1b:** 70 participants enrolled, concluded December 2022, results not yet released
- **Key differentiator:** Aims to dissolve harmful alpha-synuclein deposits

### Herantis Pharma (Finland - Helsinki)
**HER-096**
- **Target:** CDNF mimetic (cerebral dopamine neurotrophic factor)
- **Modality:** Synthetic peptidomimetic
- **Stage:** Phase 2 planned for 2026
- **MOA:** Modulates UPR pathway, reduces alpha-synuclein aggregation, modulates neuroinflammation
- **Administration:** Subcutaneous (first-in-class crossing BBB)

**Phase 1b Results (October 2025):**
- All primary/secondary endpoints met
- 200mg and 300mg doses safe and well tolerated
- CSF concentration 50-100 ng/ml within 4-12 hours
- BBB penetration confirmed
- 300mg twice-weekly dosing regimen selected for Phase 2

**Biomarker Data (January 2026):**
- Clear evidence of biological response to HER-096
- Modulates proteostasis, mitochondrial function, neuroinflammation pathways
- 2.5 million datapoints analyzed

**Funding:** Michael J. Fox Foundation, Parkinson's UK

### Gain Therapeutics (Switzerland - Geneva)
**GT-02287**
- **Target:** GCase allosteric modulator
- **Modality:** Small molecule
- **Stage:** Phase 1b (90-day dosing complete), 9-month extension ongoing
- **Population:** GBA1-PD and idiopathic PD

**Key Results (December 2025):**
- First GCase modulator to demonstrate CSF GluSph reduction in PD
- All individuals with elevated CSF GluSph showed decreases toward healthy levels
- 19/21 patients completed 90-day dosing
- 15/19 (79%) continued to 9-month extension (concluding September 2026)
- UPDRS Part II/III improvements by Day 90 (not seen at Day 30) - suggests disease-slowing effect

**Funding:** MJFF, Silverstein Foundation, Eurostars-2, Horizon 2020, Innosuisse

### NodThera (UK-based but European trials)
**NT-0796**
- **Target:** NLRP3 inflammasome inhibitor
- **Modality:** Small molecule (oral, brain-penetrant)
- **Stage:** Phase 1b/2a complete

**Results (August 2025):**
- All primary/secondary objectives met in 10 PD subjects (28 days treatment)
- CSF IL-1beta reduced, plus downstream markers (CCL2, IL-6, IL-8, CXCL1)
- sTREM2 and NfL reductions (5/8 participants had 10%+ decreases in both)
- Benign safety profile

**NT-0150 (Next-generation)**
- **Target:** NLRP3 (optimized for CNS)
- **Stage:** Phase 1 SAD/MAD (H2 2025)
- **Plans:** Phase 2 trial following Phase 1

### Endlyz Therapeutics (Belgium/UK - KU Leuven connection)
**ATP13A2 Modulators**
- **Target:** ATP13A2 (PARK9 gene) - lysosomal polyamine transporter
- **Modality:** Small molecule potentiators
- **Stage:** Lead optimization/IND-enabling
- **Founded:** 2021 by Dementia Discovery Fund

**ATP10B Program**
- **Target:** Lipid transporter enzyme
- **Stage:** Early discovery

**Backing:** SV Health Investors, Oxford Science Enterprises, AbbVie Ventures, Parkinson's UK, CD3, MJFF ($2.2M grant)

**Scientific Founders:** Prof. Peter Vangheluwe (KU Leuven), Prof. Richard Wade-Martins (Oxford), Prof. John Davis (Oxford)

### AC Immune (Switzerland - Lausanne)
**ACI-7104.056 (Alpha-synuclein vaccine)**
- **Target:** Alpha-synuclein oligomers
- **Modality:** Active immunotherapy (vaccine)
- **Stage:** Phase 2 (VacSYn trial - NCT06015841)
- **Countries:** Germany, Spain, UK
- **Origin:** Acquired Affitope PD01A from Affiris (2021)

**Phase 2 Interim Results (December 2025):**
- 100% immunogenicity responder rate
- >20-fold increase in anti-alpha-synuclein antibodies vs placebo
- CSF alpha-synuclein and NfL suggest pathology stabilization
- Plasma GFAP and DaT-SPECT show disease modification trends
- MDS-UPDRS Part III trending toward stabilization
- Clear safety profile
- Expected completion: January 2028

### Asceneuron (Switzerland - Lausanne)
**ASN90 (OGA inhibitor)**
- **Target:** O-GlcNAcase (affects tau and alpha-synuclein)
- **Modality:** Small molecule
- **Stage:** Phase I complete (3 studies), Phase 2/3 for PSP planned
- **PD relevance:** Preclinical data showed slowed motor impairment, reduced astrogliosis in PD model

**ASN-51**
- **Target:** Second-generation OGA inhibitor
- **Stage:** Phase I for PD
- **Note:** Phase 2 trial recently terminated for AD

**Funding:** Sofinnova Partners, M Ventures, SR One, JJDC, Kurma Partners

### IRLAB Therapeutics (Sweden - Gothenburg)
**Mesdopetam (IRL790)**
- **Target:** Dopamine D3 receptor antagonist
- **Indication:** Levodopa-induced dyskinesias (LIDs)
- **Stage:** Phase 3 preparation
- **Status:** FDA and EMA alignment on Phase 3 design secured
- **EMA:** Pediatric waiver received (January 2025)
- **Phase 2b:** Dose-dependent anti-dyskinetic/anti-parkinsonian effect, safety on par with placebo

**IRL757**
- **Target:** Falls prevention in PD
- **Stage:** Phase 1b approved (December 2025)

**IRL942**
- **Target:** Cognitive impairment
- **Stage:** Preclinical (deprioritized; Otsuka/MSRD evaluation agreement)

### Muna Therapeutics (Denmark/Belgium - Copenhagen/Leuven)
**Kv1.3 Blockers**
- **Target:** Potassium channel type 1.3 (neuroinflammation via microglia)
- **Modality:** Small molecule
- **Stage:** Preclinical (IND-track)
- **Funding:** MJFF $4.9M grant (October 2022)

**Series A:** $73M (July 2021) - Novo Holdings, Sofinnova, DROIA, LSP Dementia Fund
**GSK Deal:** December 2024 - EUR 33.5M upfront + EUR 140M/target milestones (for Alzheimer's targets)

**Focus:** Neuronal dysfunction repair, neuroinflammation resolution, neuroprotection
