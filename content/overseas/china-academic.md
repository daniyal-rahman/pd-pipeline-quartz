# China Academic Parkinson's Disease Programs




**Research Date:** January 21, 2026
**Focus:** Academic research programs that could become companies or license assets

---

## Executive Summary

China's major research universities and Chinese Academy of Sciences institutes are conducting substantial Parkinson's disease research, with several programs that have already spun out companies or have translational potential. The most advanced programs focus on:
1. Stem cell/iPSC therapies
2. Novel therapeutic targets (FAM171A2, alpha-synuclein uptake)
3. Genetic studies of Chinese PD populations
4. PET imaging biomarkers
5. AI-assisted diagnostics

---

## Tier 1: Programs with Active Spinouts or Clinical Translation

### Fudan University / Huashan Hospital
**Location:** Shanghai
**Departments:** PET Center, Neurology, Institute of Radiation Medicine

**Key Programs:**

#### 1. FAM171A2 Target Discovery (2025)
- **PI:** Professor Yu Jintai (Deputy Head, Neurology)
- **Discovery:** First to identify FAM171A2 as mediator of neuronal alpha-synuclein fibril uptake
- **Publication:** Science (February 2025)
- **Translation Status:** International patent filed; candidate drug identified
- **Spinout Potential:** HIGH - Novel target with drug candidate

**Mechanism:** FAM171A2 mediates neuronal uptake and spread of pathological alpha-synuclein fibrils, a key event in PD pathophysiology.

#### 2. Parkinson's Disease Progression Neuroimaging Initiative (PDPNI)
- **Status:** Ongoing since 2010
- **Cohort:** 224 subjects with longitudinal imaging
- **Assets:** FDG-PET, DAT-PET, ASL MRI, fMRI datasets
- **Application:** Progression biomarker development

#### 3. Deep Learning Diagnostics
- **PI:** Ping Wu, MD, PhD (Neuroradiologist, PET Center)
- **Innovation:** 3D deep convolutional neural network for 18F-FDG PET
- **Application:** Differential diagnosis of PD vs MSA vs PSP
- **Clinical Impact:** Addresses 20-30% misdiagnosis rate in early PD

#### 4. Alpha-Synuclein PET Tracer Development
- **Collaboration:** With Shanghai University of Traditional Chinese Medicine
- **PI:** Dr. Yingfang He (Institute of Radiation Medicine)
- **Focus:** Translating laboratory discoveries to clinical PET imaging

**Sources:**

- [CGTN Coverage of FAM171A2](https://news.cgtn.com/news/2025-02-22/Chinese-researchers-find-new-target-to-treat-Parkinson-s-disease-1Bc8suHvpni/p.html)

### Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS)

**Multiple Institutes Involved:**

#### 1. Institute of Zoology (Beijing)
- **PI:** Qi Zhou
- **Program:** ESC-derived dopamine neuron therapy
- **Status:** Pioneered China's first ESC-based PD clinical trials
- **Trial Location:** Zhengzhou
- **Approach:** HLA-matched ESC bank; differentiated to dopaminergic neurons
- **Note:** First ESC-based PD clinical trial anywhere to use fertilized embryo-derived ESCs

#### 2. Shanghai Institute of Organic Chemistry
- **Focus:** Alpha-synuclein structure and aggregation
- **Collaboration:** University of Chinese Academy of Sciences
- **Research:** Neuronal alpha-synuclein fibril uptake mechanisms

#### 3. Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology
- **Focus:** Population-based cohort studies
- **Research:** Prodromal PD symptoms (constipation as early marker)

### Shanghai University / Shanghai East Hospital (Tongji University)
**Location:** Shanghai

#### iPSC Therapy Program
- **PI:** Professor Liu Zhongmin
- **Partner:** Sizhe Biopharmaceuticals
- **Status:** Clinical trial with 12-month results announced January 2025
- **Results:** Good safety/efficacy; motor and quality of life improvements
- **Spinout:** Sizhe Biopharmaceuticals (active partnership)

---

### University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)
**Location:** Hefei, Anhui Province

#### Stem Cell Therapy Program
- **Institution:** First Affiliated Hospital
- **PI:** Dr. Shi Jiong (Neurology)
- **Innovation:** >80% conversion rate for stem cell to dopaminergic neuron differentiation (vs. ~50% internationally)
- **Status:** Phase I clinical trial initiated (mid-2025)
- **Spinout Potential:** HIGH - Superior differentiation technology

**Quote:** "Internationally leading teams recently reported a conversion rate of around 50 per cent, but our collaborative team achieved a rate of over 80 per cent through a series of efforts."

## Tier 2: Strong Research Programs with Translational Potential

### Peking University
**Location:** Beijing

**Key Laboratories:**
- Key Laboratory for Neuroscience, National Health Commission/Ministry of Education
- State Key Laboratory of Membrane Biology, School of Life Sciences

**Research Areas:**
1. **Immune-inflammatory responses in PD** - Role in alpha-synuclein scavenging
2. **Glycolysis enhancement (Terazosin/PGK1)** - Stimulating cellular ATP production
3. **Neuronal pathology mechanisms**

**Notable Finding:** Drug terazosin enhances phosphoglycerate kinase 1 (PGK1), stimulating glycolysis and increasing cellular ATP levels - potential disease-modifying approach.

---

### Tsinghua University
**Location:** Beijing

**Research Programs:**

#### 1. Deep Brain Stimulation Optimization
- **Department:** School of Aerospace Engineering
- **Collaboration:** Harvard University
- **Focus:** Mechanisms of DBS efficacy; treatment plan optimization
- **Publication:** Revealed key mechanisms behind DBS for PD treatment

#### 2. Alpha-Synuclein Structural Biology
- **Department:** Department of Chemistry
- **Focus:** Cryo-EM structure of phosphorylated alpha-synuclein fibrils
- **Discovery:** pY39 phosphorylation rearranges fibril structure; exhibits enhanced neuronal pathology
- **Technique:** Combined chemical synthesis and bacterial expression

#### 3. AI in Parkinson's Management
- **Institution:** Tsinghua University Yuquan Hospital
- **PI:** Yu Ma
- **Focus:** AI solutions for precision medicine and comprehensive PD management
- **Applications:** Early diagnosis, progression prediction, treatment optimization

#### 4. Genetic Analysis Tools (VPS13C/PARK23)
- **Innovation:** AI-powered variant pathogenicity prediction for Parkinson Disease 23

## Tier 3: Genetic/Biomarker Research Groups

### Major Genetic Studies

#### Chinese Mainland PD Cohort Study
- **Scope:** 1,676 unrelated patients
- **Method:** MLPA + whole-exome sequencing
- **Findings:**
  - Novel pathogenic variants in LRRK2, ATP13A2, FBXO7, LRP10, TMEM230
  - Chinese-specific genetic spectrum different from European populations

#### Chinese Early-Onset PD Study (Huashan Hospital)
- **Scope:** 832 patients with early onset or family history
- **Methods:** Genetic screening for monogenic forms
- **Focus:** SNCA, LRRK2, GBA1 variants

**Key Finding:** In Chinese early-onset PD:
- SNCA pathogenic variants: 0.6%
- LRRK2 pathogenic variants: 3.0%
- GBA1 pathogenic variants: 5.4%

## Traditional Chinese Medicine Research

### Guangdong Provincial Hospital of Chinese Medicine
- **Study:** Real-world evidence analysis (2018-2023)
- **Cohort:** 3,206 patient encounters
- **Finding:** ~60% of patients visit TCM hospital after PD diagnosis, ~4.59 years after motor symptom onset
- **Application:** Understanding TCM's role in PD management

### Shanghai University of Traditional Chinese Medicine
- **Affiliated Hospital:** Longhua Hospital
- **Programs:** TCM syndrome differentiation trials for PD
- **Formulas Under Study:**
  - Ziyin Pingchan (early stage)
  - Jiedu Pingchan (moderate stage)
  - Fuzheng Pingchan (late stage)
