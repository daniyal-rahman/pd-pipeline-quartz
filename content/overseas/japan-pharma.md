## Detailed Company Profiles

### Sumitomo Pharma

**Headquarters:** Osaka, Japan
**PD Commitment Level:** HIGH - Strategic priority

#### Key Asset: Raguneprocel (iPSC-derived dopaminergic progenitors)

**Status:**
- Japan NDA submitted August 2025
- Priority Review designation
- US IND cleared (February 2024)

**Mechanism:** Allogeneic iPSC-derived dopaminergic neural progenitor cells transplanted into putamen to replace lost neurons

**Clinical Results:**
- Phase I/II published in Nature (April 2025)
- Safety: No serious adverse events, no tumors
- Efficacy: 63.5% increase in dopamine (high-dose), motor improvements in 4-5/6 patients

**Corporate Structure:**
- RACTHERA Co., Ltd. (JV with Sumitomo Chemical): R&D entity for regenerative medicine
- S-RACMO: CDMO for cell therapy manufacturing

**Strategic Context:**
- "Front runner in development of cell therapy"
- First iPSC-derived cell therapy for PD globally
- 350B yen revenue target by late 2030s for regenerative medicine

**Sources:**

- [RACTHERA Announcement](https://www.sumitomo-pharma.com/news/20241217-2.html)

### Mitsubishi Tanabe Pharma Corporation (MTPC)

**Headquarters:** Osaka, Japan (part of Mitsubishi Chemical Group)
**PD Commitment Level:** HIGH - Via NeuroDerm subsidiary

#### Key Asset: ND0612

**Developer:** NeuroDerm Ltd. (Israel, wholly owned since October 2017)

**Status:**
- NDA resubmitted to FDA, accepted (late 2025)
- If approved, MTPA will commercialize in US

**Mechanism:** Continuous subcutaneous infusion of liquid levodopa/carbidopa via wearable pump (24 hours/day)

**Target Population:** PD patients with motor fluctuations despite oral L-dopa

**Clinical Data:**
- Phase 3 BouNDless: Met primary endpoint (superior ON time without troublesome dyskinesia)
- Phase 2b BeyoND: 3-year extension data shows sustained safety/efficacy
- Some patients in 8th year of follow-up

**Competitive Positioning:**
- Alternative to AbbVie's Duodopa (intestinal infusion requiring PEG tube)
- Subcutaneous delivery avoids surgical implantation
- Addresses motor fluctuations in advanced PD

**Sources:**
- [NeuroDerm](https://neuroderm.com/)
- [MTPA NDA Acceptance](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mitsubishi-tanabe-pharma-america-announces-us-fda-acceptance-of-new-drug-application-resubmission-for-investigational-nd0612-for-the-treatment-of-motor-fluctuations-in-parkinsons-disease-302466198.html)

### Eisai Co., Ltd.

**Headquarters:** Tokyo, Japan
**PD Commitment Level:** MODERATE - Approved product, limited pipeline

#### Approved Product: Equfina (Safinamide)

**Status:**
- Japan: Approved September 2019, launched November 2019
- South Korea: Approved 2020, launched 2021
- US: Approved as Xadago (partner Newron)

**Mechanism:** Selective MAO-B inhibitor + sodium channel blocker + glutamate release inhibitor
- Dual dopaminergic and non-dopaminergic mechanism

**Efficacy:** Extended "ON" time by 0.96 hours vs. placebo at 24 weeks

**Origin:** Discovered by Newron Pharmaceuticals (Italy), licensed to Meiji Seika Pharma for Japan/Asia, Eisai co-markets in Japan

#### Research Partnerships

**Washington University (2020):**
- Drug discovery collaboration for neurodegenerative diseases including PD
- Focus on biomarkers and drug targets

**Wren Therapeutics (2020):**
- Disease-modifying treatments for synucleinopathies
- Alpha-synuclein aggregation targeting

**Keio University (2013):**
- "Innovative Drug Discovery Project for Refractory Neurological Diseases Using iPS Cell Technologies"
- Established dopaminergic neuron differentiation protocol from patient-derived iPSCs

**Note:** Eisai's primary neurology focus has shifted to Alzheimer's (Leqembi/lecanemab - Japan Bioindustry Award 2025)

**Sources:**
- [Eisai Equfina Approval](https://www.eisai.com/news/2019/news201971.html)
- [Eisai iPSC Research](https://www.eisai.com/news/2018/news201887.html)

### Kyowa Kirin Co., Ltd.

**Headquarters:** Tokyo, Japan
**PD Commitment Level:** LOW - Approved product only, pipeline discontinued

#### Approved Product: NOURIANZ/NOURIAST (Istradefylline)

**Status:**
- Japan: Approved May 2013 (NOURIAST)
- US: Approved August 2019 (NOURIANZ)

**Mechanism:** Adenosine A2A receptor antagonist
- Reduces "OFF" episodes in patients on levodopa/carbidopa

**Clinical Context:**
- ~50% of PD patients experience "OFF" episodes within 5 years of starting L-dopa
- Non-dopaminergic mechanism provides additional pathway

#### Discontinued: KW-6356

**Status:** Development terminated despite positive Phase 2b data
**Reason:** "Global regulatory landscape, development hurdles, and timelines for potential market entry"

**Implication:** Kyowa Kirin unlikely to pursue additional PD assets in near term

**Sources:**
- [NOURIANZ Approval](https://www.kyowakirin.com/media_center/news_releases/2019/e20190828_01.html)

## Market Context

### Japan PD Patient Population
- Estimates range from 150,000 (Otsuka) to 250,000 (Bayer)
- Aging population driving growth
- Dementia cases projected: 5.8 million by 2040 (70% Alzheimer's)

### Government Support
- AMED funding: 110+ billion yen for regenerative medicine R&D
- PMDA accelerated pathways for cell/gene therapies
- Japan was first country to approve clinical iPSC use

### Competitive Dynamics
- Cell therapy: Sumitomo leads domestically, competing with BlueRock globally
- Symptomatic treatment: Multiple approved options (Equfina, NOURIANZ)
- Disease modification: Limited Japanese programs (Eisai partnerships)
