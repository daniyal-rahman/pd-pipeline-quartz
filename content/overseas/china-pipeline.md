# China Parkinson's Disease Pipeline




**Research Date:** January 21, 2026
**Focus:** Chinese biotech companies and clinical-stage PD assets

---

## Executive Summary

China represents the largest PD patient population globally, with an estimated 5 million cases projected by 2030 (approximately half of all global PD patients). This demographic pressure has driven significant innovation in the Chinese PD space, particularly in cell therapies. The country leads in iPSC-derived cell therapy development, with multiple companies holding dual IND approvals from both NMPA (China) and FDA (US).

## Major Chinese PD Companies and Assets

### 1. UniXell Biotechnology (Shanghai)
**Location:** Shanghai Pilot Free Trade Zone (Zhangjiang)
**Founded:** 2021
**Focus:** Autologous iPSC-derived cell therapy

**Lead Asset: UX-DA001**
- **Modality:** Autologous iPSC-derived dopaminergic neural progenitor cells
- **Stage:** Phase 1 (China, initiated March 2025)
- **Key Milestones:**
  - December 2024: NMPA IND approval (China's first iPSC therapy for PD)
  - February 2025: FDA IND approval (dual regulatory pathway)
  - March 2025: First patient dosed at Ruijin Hospital, Shanghai
  - One-month follow-up: Significant improvements in sleep quality and motor function; no SAEs

**Mechanism:** Derived from patient's own peripheral blood cells, reprogrammed into iPSCs, then differentiated into dopaminergic neurons. Transplanted via minimally invasive surgery. No immunosuppression required.

**Pipeline Expansion:**
- UX-DA002: Allogeneic cell therapy for PD (IND application accepted by NMPA)

### 2. iRegene Therapeutics (Wuhan/Suzhou)
**Location:** Headquarters in Wuhan; offices at Suzhou BioBay
**Founded:** 2017
**Focus:** Chemically-induced allogeneic iPSC cell therapy

**Lead Asset: NouvNeu001**
- **Modality:** Allogeneic iPSC-derived chemically-induced dopaminergic neuron precursors ("off-the-shelf")
- **Stage:** Phase I/II (China); FDA-approved for US trials
- **Key Milestones:**
  - August 2023: NMPA IND approval
  - July 2024: FDA IND clearance
  - January 2024: First patient dosed in China (Beijing Hospital and Zhongnan Hospital, Wuhan)
  - April 2025: Phase II multicenter trial launched in China (NCT06167681)
  - August 2025: FDA Fast Track Designation (first allogeneic iPSC therapy globally for PD)
  - January 2026: FDA RMAT Designation (first iPSC therapy to hold both FTD and RMAT)

**Phase I Results (15-month follow-up):**
- Excellent safety and tolerability without immunosuppressants after 6 months
- PET imaging confirmed long-term engraftment, survival, and maturation
- Significant improvement in MDS-UPDRS Part III motor scores
- Extended "on-time" duration

**Distinction:** World's first iPSC-based and chemically-induced universal (allogeneic) cell therapy product for PD.

### 4. Luye Pharma Group
**Location:** Shandong (HQ); global operations
**Focus:** Novel formulations and delivery systems

**Lead Asset: LY03003 (Jinyouping)**
- **Modality:** Rotigotine extended-release microspheres (weekly injection)
- **Stage:** NMPA Approved (China); ongoing global development
- **Mechanism:** Weekly intramuscular injection providing Continuous Dopaminergic Stimulation (CDS)

**Key Features:**
- World's first drug producing long-term CDS
- Once-weekly dosing improves compliance
- Stable drug release reduces "on-off" phenomenon and motor complications

**Phase III Results (China):**
- 294 patients with early-stage idiopathic PD
- LY03003 group: -11.8 improvement in UPDRS Part II+III vs. -5.6 for placebo (P<.001)

**Additional Pipeline:**
- LY03009: Monthly rotigotine microsphere formulation (in development)
- LY03017: 5-HT2AR inverse agonist for Parkinson's Disease Psychosis (Phase I in China; FDA IND cleared)

**Sources:**

### 6. SynuSight Biotech (Shanghai)
**Parent Company:** Mabwell (688062.SH)
**Focus:** Alpha-synuclein PET imaging diagnostics

**Lead Asset: 18F-FD4**
- **Modality:** Alpha-synuclein PET tracer
- **Stage:** Clinical development (US and China trials expected Q1 2026)
- **Application:** Visualize and quantify pathological alpha-synuclein aggregation in brain

**Key Features:**
- High-selectivity and high-affinity for alpha-synuclein fibrils
- Excellent blood-brain barrier penetration
- Rapid washout with clear imaging window
- Low off-target binding

**Funding:** $3.84 million grant from Michael J. Fox Foundation (2025)

**Partnerships:**
- ABLi Therapeutics: Licensed FD4 for risvodetinib clinical trials
- XingImaging: US manufacturing partner

**Significance:** Could become world's first alpha-synuclein-targeted PET tracer for clinical use in PD, MSA, and RBD

### 8. ABLi Therapeutics (US-based, China partnerships)
**Location:** Atlanta and Boston (HQ); partnerships in China
**Focus:** c-Abl kinase inhibition for neurodegeneration

**Lead Asset: Risvodetinib (ABLi-148009)**
- **Modality:** Oral small-molecule c-Abl kinase inhibitor
- **Stage:** Phase 3 planning (EOP2 meeting completed with FDA, May 2025)
- **Mechanism:** Targets c-Abl-mediated alpha-synuclein phosphorylation and aggregation

**Phase 2 Results (201 Trial):**
- Met primary endpoint for safety/tolerability
- MDS-UPDRS Part 2 reached nominal significance at 100mg dose
- Nearly 5-fold reduction in falls vs. placebo
- First treatment demonstrated to reduce alpha-synuclein pathology

**China Connection:** Partnership with SynuSight Biotech for alpha-synuclein PET imaging (FD4) in risvodetinib trials
