# European Biotech Hubs - Parkinson's Disease Research




> Last updated: January 2026
> Overview of major European life science clusters and their PD-relevant companies

---

## 1. Basel/Zurich - Swiss Pharma Cluster

### Overview
- Home to Roche and Novartis global headquarters
- Basel Area: world-class biomedical research institutions
- 2024: Swiss biotech generated $8.9B revenue, $3.2B R&D investment
- 2024: Record $3.1B capital raised (22% increase over 2023)

### Key Institutions
- University of Basel
- ETH Zurich
- Friedrich Miescher Institute for Biomedical Research
- Botnar Institute of Immune Engineering
- Roche Institute of Human Biology

### PD-Relevant Companies

| Company | Focus | Stage | Notes |
|---------|-------|-------|-------|
| **Roche** | Prasinezumab (anti-a-syn mAb) | Phase 3 prep | Lead European alpha-synuclein program |
| **Novartis** | UCB0599 (co-dev), Arrowhead siRNA | Phase 2 / Preclinical | Multiple PD investments |
| **Gain Therapeutics** | GT-02287 (GCase modulator) | Phase 1b | HQ Geneva, positive biomarker data |
| **AC Immune** | ACI-7104.056 (a-syn vaccine) | Phase 2 | HQ Lausanne, VacSYn trial |
| **Asceneuron** | ASN90/ASN51 (OGA inhibitors) | Phase 1/2 | HQ Lausanne, tau and a-syn |

### Other Basel-Based Foreign Pharma
- Bayer
- Boehringer Ingelheim
- Johnson & Johnson
- Moderna

## 3. Copenhagen/Medicon Valley - Nordic Hub

### Overview
- Cross-border cluster: Eastern Denmark + Southern Sweden
- ~600 life science companies
- 9 research universities
- 32 hospitals
- Known for: neurology, inflammatory diseases, diabetes

### Key Organizations
- Medicon Valley Alliance
- Trial Nation (455 commercial trials in 2024)
- Lundbeck Foundation

### PD-Relevant Companies

| Company | Focus | Stage | Notes |
|---------|-------|-------|-------|
| **Lundbeck** | Amlenetug (anti-a-syn mAb) | Phase 3 | MSA focus, PD implications |
| **Lundbeck** | Lu AF28996, Foliglurax | Pipeline | Additional PD programs |
| **Muna Therapeutics** | Kv1.3 blockers | Preclinical | Copenhagen/Leuven; neuroinflammation |
| **Herantis Pharma** | HER-096 (CDNF mimetic) | Phase 2 prep | HQ Helsinki (connected ecosystem) |
| **Genmab** | Partner on amlenetug | Phase 3 | mAb development |

### Research Investment
- **PACE Center** (Lundbeck Foundation): DKK 313M grant
  - Located at Aarhus University Hospital
  - Focus: Lewy Body Disorders
  - Goal: Clinical trials within 10 years

### Infrastructure Strengths
- "Triple helix" model: universities + hospitals + industry
- University of Copenhagen
- Lund University (Sweden)
- COG Nordics conference (annual clinical operations meeting)

## 4. Paris/Lyon - French Life Sciences

### Overview
- Paris Region: Premier hub for life sciences/healthtech in Europe
- Home to Sanofi, Servier, Ipsen headquarters
- Brain&Mind: Greater Paris Neuroscience cluster

### Key Institutions
- Toulouse University Hospital (coordinated LIXIPARK trial)
- Institut Pasteur
- INSERM

### PD-Relevant Companies

| Company | Focus | Stage | Notes |
|---------|-------|-------|-------|
| **Sanofi** | ABL301 (bispecific), Venglustat (discontinued) | Preclinical / Phase 2 failed | Ongoing PD investments |
| **Sanofi** | Ventyx VTX3232 interest | Preclinical | NLRP3 inflammasome |

### Key Clinical Trials (France-coordinated)
- **LIXIPARK Trial**: Lixisenatide (GLP-1 agonist) - investigator-initiated
  - Coordinated by Toulouse University Hospital
  - Funded by French Ministry of Health, Cure Parkinson's
  - Sanofi provided drug

### Strengths
- Strong regulatory pathway alignment
- Neuroscience cluster focused on Parkinson's, Alzheimer's, ALS
- Government funding for neurological research

## 5. Amsterdam/Leiden/Netherlands

### Overview
- Home to European Medicines Agency (EMA) - Amsterdam
- Strong regulatory expertise
- Science parks: Amsterdam, Utrecht, Leiden, Oss
- Particularly well-suited for early-phase trials

### Key Institutions
- Amsterdam UMC (Parkinson cohort ~1,000 patients)
- Leiden University Medical Center
- Centre for Human Drug Research (CHDR) - Leiden
- Radboud University Medical Center - Nijmegen

### PD-Relevant Research

| Organization | Focus | Notes |
|--------------|-------|-------|
| **Amsterdam UMC** | Clinical trials, cohorts, biobanks | 250K+ dementia, 40K+ PD patients in NL |
| **CHDR (Leiden)** | Phase 1 trials, genetic screening | 3,400 PD patients screened |
| **LEAP Trial** | Levodopa disease-modification | Multicenter Dutch trial |

### Collaborative Projects
- Top Institute Pharma (TI Pharma) - large-scale brain material analysis
- Partners: Solvay, DNage, Netherlands Brain Bank, VU Amsterdam, multiple UMCs

### Strengths
- High-quality early-phase trial data
- Expertise in rare diseases, cell/gene therapy, neurology
- EMA proximity for regulatory alignment
