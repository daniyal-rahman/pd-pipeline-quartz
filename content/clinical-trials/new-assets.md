#claude


This document highlights novel or early-stage assets in the PD pipeline that represent new entrants, first-in-class mechanisms, or companies not previously prominent in PD research.

## First-in-Class / Novel Mechanism Assets

### ARV-102 (Arvinas)
- **Mechanism:** PROTAC LRRK2 degrader - first PROTAC approach in PD
- **Phase:** Phase 1 (active)
- **What's new:** Unlike traditional LRRK2 kinase inhibitors (like BIIB122), ARV-102 degrades the entire LRRK2 protein. This eliminates both kinase activity AND scaffolding functions.
- **Key data (2025):**
  - >50% LRRK2 reduction in CSF at single dose >=60mg
  - >90% LRRK2 reduction in PBMCs
  - Blood-brain barrier penetration confirmed
  - Proteomic analysis shows decreased lysosomal pathway and neuroinflammatory markers
- **Next steps:** Phase 1b in PSP planned H1 2026; multiple dose PD data expected 2026
- **Why notable:** First PROTAC in neurology; addresses theoretical limitation of kinase inhibitors
- **Company:** Arvinas (NASDAQ: ARVN)

### Dapansutrile / OLT1177 (Olatec Therapeutics)
- **Mechanism:** Selective oral NLRP3 inflammasome inhibitor
- **Phase:** Phase 2 (DAPA-PD trial)
- **What's new:** First NLRP3 inhibitor in PD clinical trials; targets neuroinflammation driven by alpha-synuclein aggregates
- **Trial design:** 36 patients with early-stage PD and elevated CRP; 12-month treatment
- **Funding:** Cure Parkinson's
- **Site:** Cambridge University Hospitals
- **Why notable:** Novel anti-inflammatory approach; strong preclinical evidence; oral administration

### HER-096 (Herantis Pharma)
- **Mechanism:** CDNF-derived peptide (neurotrophic factor)
- **Phase:** Phase 1 completed; Phase 2 planned 2026
- **What's new:** Novel mechanism targeting endoplasmic reticulum stress and unfolded protein response
- **Key data:** Safe, well-tolerated, brain-penetrant in humans
- **Funding:** Parkinson's UK Virtual Biotech + Michael J. Fox Foundation
- **Why notable:** First CDNF-based therapy to complete Phase 1

### NRG5051 (NRG Therapeutics)
- **Mechanism:** Novel neuroprotective (undisclosed target)
- **Phase:** Phase 1 (first-in-human, active)
- **Status:** First participants dosed 2025 at CHDR, Leiden
- **Indication:** ALS/MND and Parkinson's disease
- **Timeline:** Readout expected by end of 2026
- **Why notable:** New entrant company; cross-indication potential

## Novel Cell Therapy Approaches

### ANPD001 (Aspen Neuroscience)
- **Mechanism:** Autologous iPSC-derived dopaminergic neuronal precursor cells
- **Phase:** Phase 1/2a (ASPIRO trial - Cohort 3 initiated)
- **What's new:**
  - First autologous iPSC cell therapy in US for PD
  - NO immunosuppression required (using patient's own cells)
  - Cohort 3 uses commercial formulation
- **Key data (2025):** 6-month data showed safety + clinician/patient-reported improvements
- **FDA status:** Fast Track designation
- **Why notable:** Personalized medicine approach; avoids immunosuppression burden
- **Company:** Aspen Neuroscience (private, San Diego)

### RNDP-001 (Kenai Therapeutics)
- **Mechanism:** Dopaminergic cell therapy
- **Phase:** Phase 1 (REPLACE trial - NCT07106021)
- **What's new:** New company entering cell therapy space
- **Status:** Recruiting at 3 US sites
- **Target:** 12 patients with moderate-severe idiopathic PD
- **Timeline:** Initial data expected 2026
- **Why notable:** New entrant; represents continued investment in cell therapy field

### NouvNeu001
- **Mechanism:** Cell therapy (limited details)
- **Phase:** Phase 1 completed; Phase 2 planned Q1 2025
- **Key data:** Good safety, tolerability, preliminary efficacy
- **Why notable:** Rapid progression to Phase 2

## Novel Alpha-Synuclein Approaches

### UB-312 (Vaxxinity)
- **Mechanism:** Active immunotherapy (vaccine) against alpha-synuclein
- **Phase:** Phase 1 completed; Phase 1b ongoing; Phase 2 planned
- **What's new:** Vaccine approach (active immunization) vs passive antibodies like prasinezumab
- **Key data (Nature Medicine 2024):**
  - 20% reduction in toxic alpha-synuclein protein
  - Safe and well-tolerated
  - Strong antibody response
- **Advantage over passive Abs:** Lower cost, less frequent dosing, potentially more durable effect
- **Company:** Vaxxinity (formerly United Neuroscience)

### IkT-148009 / Risvodetinib (Inhibikase Therapeutics)
- **Mechanism:** Selective c-Abl kinase inhibitor
- **Phase:** Phase 2 (NCT05424276)
- **What's new:** Targets alpha-synuclein aggregation through Abl kinase pathway; ~20x more potent than imatinib
- **Status:** 120 patients enrolled across 32 US sites
- **Why notable:** Small molecule oral approach to alpha-synuclein; regenerative claims in preclinical models
- **Company:** Inhibikase Therapeutics (NASDAQ: IKT)

- **What's new:** New company; focuses exclusively on GBA1 variants; leverages Gaucher disease experience

## Mechanism Diversity Summary

The 2024-2026 pipeline shows expansion beyond traditional dopamine replacement:

1. **Protein degradation** (ARV-102) - PROTAC technology
2. **Anti-inflammatory** (Dapansutrile) - NLRP3 inflammasome
3. **Neurotrophic support** (HER-096) - CDNF pathway
4. **Active immunization** (UB-312) - Vaccine approach
5. **Autologous cell therapy** (ANPD001) - No immunosuppression
6. **Kinase inhibition novel targets** (c-Abl via IkT-148009)
