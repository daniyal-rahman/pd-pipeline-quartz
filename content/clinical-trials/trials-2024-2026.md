## Key Pipeline Statistics (as of Jan 2024)

- **136 active Phase 1-3 trials** for PD drug therapies on ClinicalTrials.gov
- **30% in Phase 1**, 58% in Phase 2, 12% in Phase 3
- **56% symptomatic**, 44% disease-modifying
- **11 cell therapy trials** (mostly Phase 1)
- **9 alpha-synuclein targeting trials**
- **5 GLP-1R agonist trials**

## Trials to Watch in 2026

1. **Prasinezumab Phase 3** - First anti-alpha-synuclein antibody in pivotal trial
2. **Bemdaneprocel exPDite-2** - First large-scale stem cell Phase 3
3. **ARV-102 Phase 1b** - PROTAC technology for LRRK2
4. **BIIB122 LUMA** - Results expected March 2026
5. **Dapansutrile DAPA-PD** - NLRP3 inflammasome targeting
