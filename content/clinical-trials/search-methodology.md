## Objective

## Limitations

### Direct ClinicalTrials.gov Access
- Direct WebFetch attempts to ClinicalTrials.gov study pages returned template/styling content rather than study data
- Trial details were obtained through:
  - Web searches combining NCT ID + drug name + sponsor
  - Secondary source reporting (NeurologyLive, company announcements)
  - UCSF/UCI/UCLA clinical trials registry cross-references

### Coverage Gaps
- May have missed some trials posted in late 2025/early 2026
- Trials without press coverage or secondary source reporting may be underrepresented
- Some trial statuses may have changed since data collection
- NCT07232147 (stem cell trial) - limited details available

### Search Constraints
- "Not yet recruiting" trials are less visible in search results
- Very new trials (posted within past 1-2 months) may not be indexed in search engines
- Some company-sponsored trials have limited public information

## Recommendations for Future Searches

1. **Direct ClinicalTrials.gov API access** would provide more complete data
2. **Company SEC filings** often contain trial details not in press releases
3. **Conference abstracts** (MDS, AD/PD) announce trials before CTG posting
4. **Patent filings** may reveal pipeline assets before clinical trials announced
5. **Regulatory databases** (FDA breakthrough/fast track designations) identify priority assets

---

## Key Findings Summary

1. **Cell therapy is advancing rapidly** - Multiple assets moving from Phase 1 to Phase 3
2. **LRRK2 remains active target** - Both inhibitors (BIIB122) and degraders (ARV-102)
3. **Alpha-synuclein approaches diversifying** - Passive antibodies, vaccines, kinase inhibitors
4. **GLP-1 mixed results** - Exenatide failed Phase 3; lixisenatide showed promise
5. **New mechanisms emerging** - NLRP3 inflammasome, PROTAC technology, CDNF
