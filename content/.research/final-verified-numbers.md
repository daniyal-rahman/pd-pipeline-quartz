## Unresolved Issues

1. **CREXONT 2025 guidance conflict: $50M vs. $120M+**
   - Amneal's FY2024 press release (Feb 2025) stated 2025 CREXONT guidance of ~$50M
   - A JPMorgan January 2026 conference report (DailyPolitical.com) says Amneal "eyes CREXONT sales doubling in 2025" to $120M+
   - One of these reflects updated guidance / 2025 actuals vs. the original Feb 2025 guidance. The $120M+ figure would imply Amneal revised its guidance upward materially at some point in 2025.
   - **Resolution needed:** Check Amneal's Q1, Q2, or Q3 2025 press releases for updated CREXONT guidance. The Jan 2026 JPMorgan figure likely reflects updated guidance or actual H1 2025 performance.

2. **Neupro (UCB) FY2024 total: €210–240M vs. €246M**
   - H1 2024 = €123M confirmed from primary source
   - File 1 projects €246M FY based on H1 × 2; File 2 argues this is likely overstated because UCB divested China Neupro in November 2024 (so H2 excludes China contribution for last ~2 months of year), and applies the -15% YoY decline trend
   - File 2's €210–240M estimate is more analytically rigorous; File 1's €246M may slightly overstate
   - **Resolution needed:** Access UCB FY2024 integrated annual report product table (PDF was binary-encoded in both retrieval attempts). Contact UCB IR or check their FY2025 comparison disclosures.

3. **Rytary FY2024 exact figure: not individually disclosed**
   - Amneal reports PD franchise-level only in quarterly press releases (Q3=$61M, Q4=$64M — combined Rytary + CREXONT + Ongentys)
   - Range $130–160M for Rytary is inferential, not a reported figure
   - **Resolution needed:** Amneal 10-K filing (~March 2025) may include product-level detail for specialty segment.

4. **Vyalev FY2024 revenue: not disclosed**
   - AbbVie does not break out Vyalev/Produodopa as a separate line in FY2024 filings — either bundled with Duodopa or below disclosure threshold
   - Estimated range: ~$20–30M US (File 3) to <$50M global (Files 1 and 2)
   - **Resolution needed:** AbbVie 10-K or Q1/Q2 2025 quarterly if/when Vyalev becomes material enough to break out. Medicare coverage starting H2 2025 will be inflection point.

5. **Generic CD/LD total market (US and global): no primary data**
   - No IQVIA, CMS, or audited pharmacy-level data found via public search
   - All figures are from secondary market research firms with opaque methodologies
   - The ~$1.5–2.0B global / ~$520M US estimates are directionally plausible but should not be used for precision analysis
   - **Resolution needed:** Requires IQVIA subscription or Evaluate Pharma/Bloomberg Intelligence pull.
