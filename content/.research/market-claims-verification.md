## Claim 1: "~$6–7B global PD therapeutics market (2024)"

**Claim text:** The global PD therapeutics market is approximately $6–7 billion in 2024.

### Evidence

Seven independent estimates collected (treatment-focused reports unless noted):

| Source | 2024 Estimate | Scope |
|--------|--------------|-------|
| Grand View Research | $5.65B | Treatment only |
| DataM Intelligence | $5.92B | Therapeutics |
| GM Insights | $6.2B | Therapeutics |
| Precedence Research | $6.59B | Therapeutics |
| Market Research Future | $6.37B | Therapeutics |
| Toward Healthcare | $6.88B | **Diagnosis + Treatment** |
| Fortune Business Insights | $7.17B | Drugs only |

Median of treatment-only estimates: ~$6.1B. The claim of "$6–7B" brackets most credible treatment-focused figures.

**Treatment vs. Diagnostics split:** Reports labeled "Therapeutics" or "Drugs" generally sit in the $5.6–6.4B range. Reports that include diagnostics push toward $6.9B+. Grand View ($5.65B) is the low outlier. Fortune Business Insights ($7.17B) uses the broadest drug market definition. The claim is slightly ambiguous on scope; for treatment-only, $6.0–6.6B is the best-supported consensus.

### Verdict: CONFIRMED

The $6–7B range is consistent with the consensus across seven independent market research firms. Two estimates fall modestly below $6B; none of the seven fall above $7.2B. A more precise statement would be $5.9–6.6B for therapeutics-only; the $6–7B claim is directionally accurate and captures the modal range. The upper end ($7B) requires either a broader drug market definition or diagnostics inclusion.

## Claim 2: "~$2.3B US branded revenue"

**Claim text:** US branded PD products collectively generate approximately $2.3B in revenue. Underlying components cited: Nuplazid ~$609M, Duopa ~$96M, Gocovri ~$131M, Rytary ~$130M, Vyalev ~$50M, Neupro US ~$50M.

### Evidence by Product (All 2024 Full-Year Data)

**Nuplazid (pimavanserin) — Acadia Pharmaceuticals**
- Full year 2024 net product sales: **$609.4M**
- Verification: Directly from Acadia Q4 & FY2024 earnings release (February 26, 2025). Acadia markets exclusively in the US.
- Status: Verified to the dollar.

**Duopa (carbidopa/levodopa intestinal gel, US brand name) — AbbVie**
- Full year 2024 US net revenues: **$96M** (global total: $447M; international: $351M)
- Verification: Directly from AbbVie FY2024 earnings press release (January 31, 2025), which breaks out US vs. international for Duodopa/Duopa.
- Status: Verified exactly.

**Gocovri (amantadine ER) — Supernus Pharmaceuticals**
- Full year 2024 net sales: **$130.8M**
- Verification: Directly from Supernus Q4 and FY2024 press release (February 25, 2025). Q4 2024 was $36.9M (+15% YoY); full year was +9%.
- Status: Verified to within $1M rounding of the $131M claim.

**Rytary (carbidopa/levodopa ER) — Amneal Pharmaceuticals**
- Amneal's full PD franchise (Rytary + CREXONT + ONGENTYS combined) delivered $61M in Q3 2024 and $64M in Q4 2024. Annualizing: ~$230–240M for 2024 for the combined franchise.
- Amneal does not break out Rytary independently in public filings.
- The $130M Rytary-only estimate is plausible (roughly half the franchise if CREXONT and ONGENTYS are smaller contributors), but cannot be verified from public data.
- Status: Unverifiable standalone; the claim's figure is a reasonable estimate.

**Vyalev (foscarbidopa/foslevodopa SQ infusion) — AbbVie**
- FDA approved October 17, 2024; US commercial launch was Q4 2024 only (~2.5 months).
- AbbVie's Q4 2024 call noted US ramp was "gradual" pending Medicare coverage (expected H2 2025).
- The $183M Q4 figure cited in some secondary sources is **Q4 2025** (from the February 2026 AbbVie earnings call), not Q4 2024.
- Vyalev full year 2025 revenue was approximately $482M globally; 2024 US revenue likely $20–50M given minimal ramp time.
- Status: The $50M estimate is a reasonable upper-bound placeholder for 2024; exact figure not publicly disclosed.

**Neupro (rotigotine transdermal patch) — UCB**
- UCB does not break out Neupro's US revenue in investor materials.
- Neupro global revenue peaked at ~€321M (~$350M) in 2018 and has been declining due to generic competition.
- In November 2024, UCB divested its China neurology portfolio (including Neupro) to CBC Group/Mubadala for $680M.
- US generic rotigotine patches entered market circa 2021, likely depressing branded US Neupro to a fraction of prior levels.
- Status: The $50M US estimate is unverifiable; it may be high given generic erosion. Could be materially lower.

**Critical gap:** The three verified products alone total $836M. Adding the three plausible-but-unverified estimates at their stated values brings the total to ~$1.07B — far short of $2.3B. The gap of ~$1.2B+ is not explained by the six products listed.

**Where might the additional revenue come from?**
- Other branded/specialty PD products not listed: Stalevo (carbidopa/levodopa/entacapone, Orion/Novartis), Comtan (entacapone), ONGENTYS (opicapone, Amneal), CREXONT (Amneal, launched Sep 2024), Xadago (safinamide, US rights), branded ropinirole/pramipexole IR and ER (some still on brand), Inbrija (inhaled levodopa, Acorda/Supernus), Mirapex ER (pramipexole ER)
- If these collectively contribute $800M–$1.1B in US branded revenue, the $2.3B figure becomes plausible
- However, most of these categories have seen substantial generic erosion; reaching $2.3B total branded requires generous assumptions about this long tail

### Verdict: PARTIALLY TRUE — MATH DOES NOT WORK AS STATED

The individual product figures for Nuplazid, Duopa, and Gocovri are verified and accurate. However, the six products as listed sum to approximately $1.0–1.1B, not $2.3B. The $2.3B claim would require the existence of roughly $1.1–1.2B in additional US branded PD revenue from products not itemized. That may be achievable when including the full universe of branded PD products (COMT inhibitors, MAO-B inhibitors, dopamine agonists still on brand, inhaled/device-based formulations), but this was not demonstrated. Treat the $2.3B total as requiring independent verification via a IQVIA-type datasource; do not derive it by summing the six products listed.

## Claim 3: "Pricing spans 500x" — Generic CD/LD ~$100–500/yr; Nuplazid ~$60K/yr; Vyalev ~$62K/yr

**Claim text:** PD drug pricing spans approximately 500x from generic carbidopa/levodopa (~$100–500/yr) to Nuplazid/Vyalev (~$60–62K/yr).

- ClinCalc 2023 data: average total cost/prescription $22.02; patient out-of-pocket $9.68/fill; **$0.29/day patient share** → ~$106/yr patient out-of-pocket

**Nuplazid (pimavanserin) WAC:**
- Published WAC: $4,995/30-capsule bottle (one source); $5,009.12/30 capsules (another source)

- Sources: GoodRx, Drugs.com, Acadia Connect HCP site all consistent
- Status: Verified.

**Vyalev (foscarbidopa/foslevodopa) annual cost:**

- This is from the Canadian reimbursement evaluation; US WAC not publicly disclosed in accessible form

All three component price points are verified from published sources. The 500x ratio is internally consistent with a mid-range generic cost assumption (~$120/yr). The claim correctly characterizes the pricing extremes of the PD market. ## Claim 4: "Generic CD/LD accounts for ~50% of prescriptions by volume"

**Claim text:** Generic carbidopa/levodopa accounts for approximately 50% of all PD prescriptions by volume.

### Evidence

**Supporting data points:**

1. One market research report (market growth reports citing prescription data): "12 generic levodopa/carbidopa products are marketed in the U.S., supplying over 60% of total levodopa dispensation" — this is share within the levodopa class, not all PD prescriptions.

2. Precedence Research: "the levodopa/carbidopa segment captured 50.93% market share in 2024" — though it is ambiguous whether this is revenue share or prescription volume share, and whether it refers to the levodopa class or all PD drugs.

3. One market analysis: "levodopa accounted for 65% of prescriptions among Parkinson's disease medications overall." If correct, and generics supply >60% within the levodopa class, then generic CD/LD alone would represent ~0.65 × 0.60 = ~39% of all PD scripts — close to but below 50%.

4. No IQVIA or CMS primary dataset was accessible via web search to confirm a specific "50% of all PD prescriptions" volume figure.

**Inference chain:**
- Levodopa is the backbone therapy; most PD patients are on it at some point
- Generic formulations dominate within the levodopa class (>60% of levodopa dispensation per one source)
- At 65% levodopa class share × 60%+ generic penetration = ~40–45% of all PD Rx
- Rounded to 50% this is plausible; may be slightly overstated relative to the most granular evidence

**ClinCalc data note:** Carbidopa/levodopa generated 258,032 prescriptions in 2023 (ranked #310). This appears to be undercounted (the data methodology note on ClinCalc flags variability in its data collection), and does not capture all carbidopa/levodopa formulations separately.

### Verdict: PARTIALLY TRUE — Unverifiable with precision from public sources

The directional claim is well-supported: generic CD/LD is the dominant PD drug class and generic penetration within that class is high. The specific "~50%" figure is a reasonable approximation but lacks a citable primary source. The true figure may be 40–60% depending on how "all PD prescriptions" is defined (initial Rx only vs. refills, monotherapy vs. combination, all formulations of CD/LD including branded). Flag this figure if used in a regulatory or investment context requiring primary-source precision. Best available estimate: generic CD/LD accounts for approximately 40–55% of PD prescription volume.

## Claim 6: "No public company reports generic CD/LD as a segment"

**Claim text:** Generic carbidopa/levodopa revenue is not broken out as a reportable segment by any major public company, including Teva, Mylan/Viatris, Sun Pharma, or Amneal.

### Evidence

**Teva Pharmaceuticals:**
- Confirmed manufacturer and seller of Carbidopa and Levodopa Tablets, USP (generic Sinemet)
- Reporting structure: Three geographic segments (US, Europe, International Markets)
- US generic revenue 2024: $3,599M — no product-level disclosure for any individual generic
- Carbidopa/levodopa represents a negligible fraction of this total at generic pricing; well below any materiality threshold for disclosure
- Status: Not reported as a segment or product line.

**Viatris (Mylan/Upjohn spin-off):**
- Sells generic carbidopa/levodopa across multiple markets
- Reports by segment (Developed Markets, Emerging Markets, JANZ) and therapeutic area
- No product-level carbidopa/levodopa disclosure found in any accessible financial summary
- Status: Not reported.

**Amneal Pharmaceuticals:**
- Most PD-focused public company; reports three segments: Affordable Medicines (generic), Specialty (branded), AvKARE
- Specialty segment discloses Parkinson's franchise (Rytary + CREXONT + ONGENTYS combined, ~$230–240M annualized 2024)
- Generic carbidopa/levodopa IR tablets fall in Affordable Medicines — disclosed only as segment aggregate, no product breakout
- Even Amneal's branded PD franchise is reported as a combined number; individual branded product revenue requires analyst parsing of quarterly calls
- Status: Generic CD/LD not disclosed; branded PD only disclosed as an aggregate "Parkinson's franchise."

**Sun Pharma:**
- FDA-approved manufacturer of generic carbidopa/levodopa (including generic Sinemet CR)
- Reports by geography (India, US, Emerging Markets, Rest of World) with US Specialty highlighted
- No individual generic product disclosure
- Status: Not reported.

**Why this is structurally true:** Generic CD/LD at ~$10–30/month per patient, even across hundreds of thousands of patients, generates $100–300M in total US revenue. For Teva ($16.5B revenue) this is <2% — well below SEC materiality thresholds (~5–10% of segment revenue for mandatory disclosure). For Amneal, generic CD/LD is one of hundreds of generic products in the Affordable Medicines segment. No regulatory or accounting incentive exists to break it out.

**Implication:** The total value of the generic CD/LD market (estimated $300–800M US annually, given ~65M+ prescriptions at average ~$10–40 net cost per fill) is invisible in public financial disclosures. Market sizing of this segment requires CMS/IQVIA data, not company filings.

### Verdict: CONFIRMED

The claim is accurate. No major generic drug manufacturer publicly reports carbidopa/levodopa revenue as a distinct line item or segment. This is consistent with standard pharmaceutical disclosure practice and is a structural feature of how large-volume, low-price generics are accounted for. The implication — that this creates a genuine blind spot in PD market sizing — is also valid.
