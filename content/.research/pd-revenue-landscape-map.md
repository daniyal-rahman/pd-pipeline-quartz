# PD Therapeutics Revenue Landscape Map
*Researcher: researcher-landscape | Date: 2026-02-26*
*Purpose: Comprehensive fact-check of PD revenue slide claiming ~$6-7B global market, ~$2.3B US branded*

---

## Executive Summary

The slide being fact-checked has **several material errors**:
1. **Nuplazid revenue overstated** by ~$80M ($609M actual vs. ~$690M claimed)
2. **Duodopa revenue understated/mislabeled** ($447M actual total vs. $381M claimed — the $381M appears to be ex-US only)
3. **Vyalev $482M figure is misleading** — Vyalev/Produodopa had minimal 2024 revenue (US approved Oct 2024, EU launch Jan 2024); the $482M appears to be the historical Duodopa baseline
4. **Gocovri overstated** by ~$13M ($130.8M actual vs. ~$144M claimed)
5. **CREXONT $80M is NOT 2024 revenue** — it is forward guidance/projection (actual 2024: ~$3M launch revenue)
6. **Neupro (rotigotine/UCB) is MISSING** — ~$265M global in 2024 (H1 2024: €123M), declining
7. **Stalevo/Comtan (entacapone/Orion) is MISSING** — €84.1M (~$91M) in 2024, mostly Europe/Japan

## Part 2: Revenue Detail by Product

### 2.1 Nuplazid (pimavanserin) — Acadia Pharmaceuticals

- **FY2024**: $609.4M net product sales (US only; no ex-US approval)
- **FY2023**: ~$560-580M (estimated from growth trajectory)
- **Source**: Acadia Q4/FY2024 earnings release, Feb 2025
- **Slide claim**: ~$690M → **WRONG. Overstated by ~$80M.**
- Note: Acadia total 2024 revenues were $957.8M (includes Daybue for Rett syndrome)

### 2.2 Duodopa/Duopa (levodopa-carbidopa intestinal gel) — AbbVie

- **FY2024 Total**: $447M (-4.49% from $468M in 2023)
  - US (Duopa): $96M
  - International (Duodopa): $351M
- **FY2023**: $468M
- **FY2022**: ~$489M (estimated from trend)
- **Source**: AbbVie FY2024 press release; druganalyst.com historical data
- **Slide claim**: "Duodopa $381M" → **WRONG.** Total is $447M. The $381M appears to approximate the ex-US international figure from 2023 (actual 2024 ex-US: $351M).

### 2.3 Vyalev / PRODUODOPA (foslevodopa/foscarbidopa) — AbbVie

- **FY2024 revenue**: Not separately disclosed; estimated <$50M globally
  - EU launch: January 2024 (as PRODUODOPA)
  - US approval: October 17, 2024 (as VYALEV)
  - >4,200 patients on treatment globally pre-US approval (mostly EU)
- **Peak projection**: Analysts project $1B+ peak sales
- **Context**: Duodopa historically ~$500M/yr; Vyalev expected to replace/surpass it
- **Slide claim**: "Vyalev $482M→$1B" → **MISLEADING.** The $482M is not Vyalev's 2024 revenue. Likely refers to: (a) Duodopa's ~2022 peak ($468-489M) as the baseline being replaced, or (b) combined AbbVie advanced PD franchise. Actual Vyalev 2024 revenue was minimal.
- **AbbVie does NOT separately report Produodopa/Vyalev revenue** — it is bundled or too small to report.

### 2.4 Gocovri (amantadine extended-release) — Supernus Pharmaceuticals

- **FY2024**: $130.8M (+9% from 2023)
- **Q4 2024**: $36.9M (+15% YoY)
- **Owner**: Supernus (acquired Adamas Pharmaceuticals Nov 2021 for ~$450M)
- **Source**: Supernus Q4/FY2024 press release, Feb 25, 2025
- **Slide claim**: ~$144M → **WRONG. Overstated by ~$13M.**
- Note: The slide lists this under "Amneal" in some versions — Gocovri is a **Supernus** product, not Amneal.

### 2.5 Rytary (carbidopa/levodopa ER capsules) — Amneal Pharmaceuticals

- **FY2024**: ~$130-160M estimated
  - Q4 2024 Parkinson's franchise (Rytary + CREXONT $3M + Ongentys partial): $64M
  - Q3 2024 Parkinson's franchise: $61M
  - Amneal 2025 guidance: $120-140M for Rytary "given LOE in Q3 2025"
  - FY2024 full-year estimate extrapolated: likely $150-180M (but 2025 guidance's $130M implies 2024 base was similar)
- **Loss of exclusivity**: Q3 2025 — generics entering
- **Source**: Amneal Q4/FY2024 press release; earnings call
- **Slide claim**: ~$130M → **Plausible but may be slightly understated.** Amneal's 2025 Rytary guidance of $120-140M suggests 2024 was in similar range.

### 2.6 CREXONT (carbidopa/levodopa ER capsules) — Amneal Pharmaceuticals

- **FY2024**: ~$3M (Q4 launch only; launched September 2024)
- **Q4 2024 contribution**: $3M within the $64M franchise
- **Amneal 2025 guidance**: ~$50M for CREXONT
- **Peak projection**: Amneal guides US peak sales of $300-500M
- **Market share**: ~1% after 4 months; targeting 3%+ exit rate
- **Source**: Amneal Q4/FY2024 earnings call
- **Slide claim**: ~$80M → **WRONG as 2024 actual.** This appears to be a forward projection (2025-2026), not 2024 revenue. Actual 2024 revenue was ~$3M.

### 2.7 Neupro (rotigotine transdermal patch) — UCB — MISSING FROM SLIDE

- **H1 2024**: €123M net sales (-15% YoY from generic competition)
- **FY2024 estimate**: ~€240-250M (~$260-270M USD)
- **Generic competition**: Generic Neupro launched in US since 2021; UCB divested China Neupro rights in Nov 2024
- **Historical**: ~€319M in 2019; ~€400M ($400M) globally in 2022
- **Restless legs syndrome**: Also indicated for RLS (some revenue not pure PD)
- **Source**: UCB H1 2024 financial results; UCB FY2024 annual report
- **Slide claim**: NOT LISTED → **MISSING.** ~$265M global is above the $50M threshold.

### 2.8 Stalevo/Comtan (entacapone, entacapone+levodopa+carbidopa) — Orion — MISSING FROM SLIDE

- **FY2024**: €84.1M (~$91M) for all entacapone products (-4.8% from €88.4M in 2023)
- **Markets**: Europe and Asia-Pacific; Japan most important individual market
  - Novartis returned Japan rights to Orion at end of 2024
- **Character**: Mostly off-patent; volume increasing but price declining
- **Source**: Orion Group Financial Statement Release 2024
- **Slide claim**: NOT LISTED → **MISSING.** However, this is primarily generic/off-patent territory and European/Japanese revenue.

### 2.9 Generic Carbidopa-Levodopa

- **US estimate**: ~$520M (26.77% of $1.94B US PD market per Grand View Research)
- **Global estimate**: Multiple sources range $1.5-2.0B
- **Levodopa market**: ~$1.87B globally estimated (Cognitive Market Research)
- **Market share**: CD/LD combinations hold 25-35% of global PD drug market
- **Slide claim**: $1.5-1.9B globally → **Plausible**, within analyst estimates

## Part 6: Key Errors and Anomalies in the Slide

### Error 1: Nuplazid Revenue Overstated
- **Slide**: ~$690M
- **Actual**: $609.4M (Acadia FY2024 earnings release)
- **Delta**: +$80M overstatement (~13%)

### Error 2: Duodopa Revenue Is Confused
- **Slide**: "Duodopa $381M"
- **Actual total (Duodopa + Duopa)**: $447M ($96M US Duopa + $351M ex-US Duodopa)
- **Interpretation**: $381M appears to be ex-US Duodopa only (from 2023: ~$381M possible), not the current total
- **Impact**: The slide is presenting an inconsistent/stale sub-segment figure

### Error 3: Vyalev $482M Is Not 2024 Vyalev Revenue
- **Slide**: "Vyalev ($482M→$1B)"
- **Actual**: Vyalev US-approved Oct 2024; Produodopa EU launched Jan 2024. Revenue not separately disclosed by AbbVie but almost certainly <$50M in 2024 given just launched
- **Interpretation**: The $482M likely refers to Duodopa's historical annual run-rate (~2021-2022 range) as the baseline being replaced, and $1B is peak projection
- **Impact**: This creates false impression that Vyalev already has $482M revenue in 2024

### Error 4: Gocovri Overstated
- **Slide**: ~$144M
- **Actual**: $130.8M (Supernus FY2024 earnings)
- **Delta**: +$13M overstatement (~10%)

### Error 5: CREXONT $80M Is a Projection, Not 2024 Revenue
- **Slide**: ~$80M CREXONT
- **Actual 2024**: ~$3M (September-December launch only; 1% market share after 4 months)
- **Context**: $80M may be 2025 guidance or a mid-year projection (Amneal 2025 guidance: ~$50M)
- **Impact**: Highly misleading if presented as current revenue

### Error 6: Neupro (~$265M) Is Completely Missing
- **Slide**: Not listed
- **Actual**: ~€240-250M ($260-270M) globally in 2024 — a major PD product
