### 2. Duodopa/Duopa (AbbVie)

**Claimed:** $447M FY2024 total ($96M US + $351M intl)
**Verified:** $447M total, $96M US, $351M intl — CONFIRMED
**Source:** [AbbVie Reports Full-Year and Fourth-Quarter 2024 Financial Results](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/abbvie-reports-full-year-and-fourth-quarter-2024-financial-results-302364968.html) (PR Newswire, 2025-01-31)

**Context:** $447M represents a **4.7% operational decline** from $468M in FY2023. AbbVie reports Duodopa (intl) and Duopa (US) as a single line in their product table. Q4 2024 was $108M ($24M US / $84M intl).

### 6. CREXONT (Amneal Pharmaceuticals)

**Claimed:** ~$3M FY2024 (launched Sept 2024), 2025 guidance ~$50M
**Verified:** Q3 2024 revenue of $3M confirmed (launch quarter); FY2024 total not separately disclosed; 2025 guidance of ~$50M CONFIRMED

The JPMorgan figure likely reflects updated 2025 actuals or upgraded guidance, not the original 2025 guidance set in Feb 2025.

**Conclusion:** ~$3M for FY2024 is plausible but technically only the Q3 figure is confirmed ($3M); Q4 2024 would add more. FY2024 total is likely $5–10M. The $50M FY2025 guidance is confirmed from primary source.

### 7. Neupro (UCB)

**Claimed:** ~€246M (~$265M) FY2024
**Verified:** H1 2024 = €123M confirmed; FY total NOT independently extracted
**Sources:**
- [UCB Half-Year Financial Report 2024](https://www.ucb.com/sites/default/files/2025-02/ucb_half-year_2024_report-en-final.pdf) — H1 2024 Neupro = €123M
- [UCB FY2024 Results Capital Market Earnings Call PDF](https://www.ucb.com/sites/default/files/2025-02/ucb_fy_2024_results_capital_market_earnings_call_27_february_2025_0.pdf) — PDF was unreadable (binary encoded)
- [UCB Integrated Annual Report 2024](https://reports.ucb.com/) — Neupro not surfaced in executive summary

**Critical complication — China divestiture:**
UCB divested its China business (including Neupro in China) to CBC Group and Mubadala Investment Company for $680M, **closing November 2024**. This means:
- Neupro China revenues are included in FY2024 through ~October 2024 only
- H1 2024 Neupro = €123M included China; post-divestiture H2 figures would exclude China
- H1 2023 Neupro = €146M (for comparison; 15% YoY decline in H1 2024)

**Back-of-envelope estimate:** If H1 2024 = €123M and H2 was lower (15% decline trend + China divestiture removing ~Oct-Dec contribution), FY2024 Neupro is plausibly in the €210–240M range, not €246M. The €246M claimed figure may be slightly high. However, this is a rough estimate — the claimed figure cannot be confirmed or definitively contradicted without the full annual report product table.

**Conclusion:** Cannot confirm €246M. The H1 figure of €123M is the only primary data point available. The China divestiture in November 2024 creates a real accounting complexity. The claimed figure may be modestly overstated, but this is uncertain.

### Key New Finding: Apokyn $73.9M (Above $50M Threshold)

The research doc classified Apokyn as "Likely <$50M US." This is **incorrect**.

From Supernus's FY2024 press release (GlobeNewsWire, 2025-02-25), individual product revenues were:

| Product | FY2024 |
|---|---|
| Qelbree | $241.3M |
| **APOKYN** | **$73.9M** |
| GOCOVRI | $130.8M |
| Oxtellar XR | $99.5M |
| Trokendi XR | $63.2M |
| Other (Myobloc + Xadago + Osmolex ER) | $29.0M |

Apokyn FY2023 was $75.1M, so FY2024 at $73.9M represents a slight decline. This product has been above $50M for several years. Supernus expects ONAPGO (apomorphine SC pump, same molecule, different delivery) to replace Apokyn commercial revenue when it launches Q2 2025.

**Impact:** Any PD revenue slide that lists products >$50M must include Apokyn. It is the 7th-largest US branded PD product by revenue.
